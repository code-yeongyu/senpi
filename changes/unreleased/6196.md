## 2026-11-08 - A live resumption channel suspends the toolless stall streak (senpi#4265)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/goal/monitor-continuation.ts`: `#recordToollessContinuationTurn` returns before incrementing the in-memory toolless streak when `#activeWakeSourceCount() > 0`. A toolless turn that ends while a wake source (senpi-task child, terminal monitor, background sessions, codemode cell, pending question) is live neither advances nor clears the streak; the streak keeps any weight it built while nothing was on duty. The pre-existing reset in `#setWakeSourceCount` (total across sources reaches zero) is unchanged, so a real delivery or drain still re-bases the streak to zero.
- `packages/coding-agent/test/suite/goal-resumption-channels.test.ts`: the pinned "keeps the timer and toolless streak until the total across sources reaches zero" case asserted the stall check on the third backstop delivery while channels were live — the fossilized-streak bug itself. It now asserts no stall check on any live-waiting delivery nor on the drain delivery, and a new case pins the two halves together: a streak built with no channel on duty is re-based when sources drain to zero, and `<goal_stall_check>` returns only after three fresh toolless turns.
- `packages/coding-agent/test/suite/goal-monitor-stall.test.ts`: the streak-build phase of the monitor-threshold and settle-reset cases moved to genuinely toolless turns (no channel), because waiting turns no longer count; the monitor-flavored notice after a channel joins is preserved.

### Why

- The continuation prompt explicitly endorses ending a turn while a live resumption channel is on duty as progress. Counting those turns toward the stall streak injected `<goal_stall_check>` telling the model to investigate or abandon channels that were working as designed, on the third such turn.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- This is the goal builtin extension's own streak bookkeeping; no extension API observes or gates the in-memory toolless counter.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `monitor-continuation.ts` `#recordToollessContinuationTurn` (fork-only file; the guard line and its comment are the only production change).

### Fixed
- A goal continuation turn that ends while a resumption channel (senpi-task child, terminal monitor, background session, codemode cell, pending question) is live no longer counts toward the toolless stall streak, and the streak re-bases when the channel delivers or drains; three genuinely toolless turns still inject the stall check ([#6196](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/senpi/pull/6196))
