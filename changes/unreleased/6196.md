## 2026-11-08 - A live resumption channel suspends the toolless stall streak (senpi#4265)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/goal/monitor-continuation.ts`: `#recordToollessContinuationTurn` no longer increments the ordinary toolless streak while a wake source is live; instead it counts those turns on a separate `#liveChannelToollessStreak` counter with a higher bound (`GOAL_STALL_LIVE_CHANNEL_THRESHOLD = 4`), so a permanently live, never-delivering channel still surfaces as a stalled wait. The counter re-bases in `#setWakeSourceCount` whenever a channel delivers (its count changes) or drains to zero, and on tool-using turns and goal change.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/goal/continuation.ts`: adds `GOAL_STALL_LIVE_CHANNEL_THRESHOLD` and the `liveChannelToollessStreak` field on `GoalContinuationInput`; `shouldShowStallNotice` now trips on either the ordinary toolless streak or the live-wait streak reaching its bound.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/goal/monitor-continuation.ts` (stall reporting): the stall event, the UI notify line, and `buildGoalStallNotice` report the counter that actually tripped the bound (the live-wait count when a live channel hit `GOAL_STALL_LIVE_CHANNEL_THRESHOLD`, otherwise the ordinary toolless count).
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/goal/lifecycle-helpers.ts`: the session-start `queueGoalContinuation` input passes `liveChannelToollessStreak: 0` alongside `toollessContinuationStreak: 0` (the field is required on `GoalContinuationInput`); the direct-input test fixtures do the same.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/goal/AGENTS.md`: documents the separate live-channel bound next to the 3-turn stall check.
- `packages/coding-agent/test/suite/regressions/4265-live-channel-dead-wait-bound.test.ts`: pins the dead-channel bound (a never-delivering live channel stalls on its fourth waiting turn, not before), that such a channel does not trip the ordinary 3-turn bound, and that a channel which delivers while staying live re-bases the live-wait counter (fails if the re-base in `#setWakeSourceCount` is removed).
- `packages/coding-agent/test/suite/goal-resumption-channels.test.ts`: the pinned "keeps the timer and toolless streak until the total across sources reaches zero" case asserted the stall check on the third backstop delivery while channels were live — the fossilized-streak bug itself. It now asserts no stall check on any live-waiting delivery nor on the drain delivery, and a new case pins the two halves together: a streak built with no channel on duty is re-based when sources drain to zero, and `<goal_stall_check>` returns only after three fresh toolless turns.
- `packages/coding-agent/test/suite/goal-monitor-stall.test.ts`: the streak-build phase of the monitor-threshold and settle-reset cases moved to genuinely toolless turns (no channel), because waiting turns no longer count; the monitor-flavored notice after a channel joins is preserved.

### Why

- The continuation prompt explicitly endorses ending a turn while a live resumption channel is on duty as progress. Counting those turns toward the stall streak injected `<goal_stall_check>` telling the model to investigate or abandon channels that were working as designed, on the third such turn.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- This is the goal builtin extension's own streak bookkeeping; no extension API observes or gates the in-memory toolless counter.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `monitor-continuation.ts` (fork-only file): `#recordToollessContinuationTurn` (the live-channel branch), `#setWakeSourceCount` (the delivery re-base), the stall-notice block that picks the reported count, the `GoalContinuationInput` assembly, and `#resetToollessContinuationStreak`.
- LOW: `continuation.ts`: `GoalContinuationInput` (new field) and `shouldShowStallNotice`.
- LOW: `goal/AGENTS.md`: the stall-check paragraph.
- LOW: `lifecycle-helpers.ts`: the `queueGoalContinuation` input literal.

### Fixed
- Behavior change: a goal continuation turn that ends while a resumption channel (senpi-task child, terminal monitor, background session, codemode cell, pending question) is live no longer counts toward the ordinary 3-turn toolless stall streak; live waits are instead bounded by a separate, higher threshold (4 turns) so a permanently live, never-delivering channel still surfaces as a stalled wait, and the counter re-bases when a channel delivers or drains. Three genuinely toolless turns still inject the stall check ([#6196](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/senpi/pull/6196))
