## 2026-11-04 - A steer can name the turn it is meant for (senpi#5776)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/rpc-session-state.ts` and `rpc-session-state-types.ts`: `get_state` carries `currentTurnId` (the session's turn epoch) while a turn runs.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/rpc-session-command-types.ts` and `method-classes-schema-session.ts`: `prompt` accepts `expectedTurnId`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/client-input-handler.ts`: a `prompt` steer whose `expectedTurnId` is not the running turn (or names one while idle) is refused `turn_conflict` with `errorData.currentTurnId`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/rpc-error-types.ts` and `rpc-types.ts`: `RPC_ERROR_TURN_CONFLICT`.

### Why

A client on another device could not steer the turn it saw: it had no turn id and no way to send the one it expected, so a steer meant for one turn could land in the next. The local gateway already refuses that case with `turn_conflict`.

### Why an extension could not handle it

The turn id and the steer admission live in the RPC host's `get_state` and `prompt` handlers.

### Expected merge conflict zones

`client-input-handler.ts` around the `prompt` case and its refusal mapping; `rpc-session-state.ts` field list.
