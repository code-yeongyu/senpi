## 2026-11-22 - A quiet release refuses live background work; an interrupt names what it ended (senpi#6499)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-release.ts`: busy signal `background` for a live wake source (refusal names `errorData.background`); an interrupt release reports `ended.background` and records `ended_background`.

### Why

- A quiet release answered `released: true` and the teardown silently ended a live background job.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The release decision is the RPC host's own.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- MEDIUM: `busySignals` and the success answer in `session-release.ts` (also touched by #6507).
