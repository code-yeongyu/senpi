## 2026-11-16 - The held follow-up fail loop reads each input's live record (senpi#6868)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/queued-input-after-error.ts`: `failQueuedFollowUps` takes `current(queuedId)`, the input's live queued record, and builds each refusal from it. An entry of its snapshot that is no longer queued is skipped; one edited meanwhile is refused with its edited text.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/agent-session.ts`: passes `current` (a lookup in the session's queued-input order).

### Why

- #6869 review L4: the loop works from a snapshot. A Stop or `clear_queue` that lands while an earlier follow-up's durable dequeue awaits its record had already settled the later inputs as cleared, yet they still received a `queued_input_failed`.
- #6881 review L2: an `editQueuedInput` landing in the same window kept the entry queued, and its refusal carried the pre-edit text. The TUI restores that text into the editor, so the person's edit was lost.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The fail loop runs inside the session's `agent_end` decision on its own queue.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: fork-only `queued-input-after-error.ts`; one line at the `failQueuedFollowUps` call in `agent-session.ts`.
