## 2026-11-26 - GPT-6.2 Astra Ultrafast subscription alias (senpi#7505, senpi#6956)

### What changed

- `packages/ai/scripts/generate-models.ts`: generate the subscription-only `gpt-6.2-astra-ultrafast` alias from the base Astra row, preserving its capabilities and standard pricing metadata while selecting the Ultrafast service tier.
- `packages/ai/src/providers/data/chatgpt-subscription.json` and `packages/ai/src/providers/data/.manifest.json`: regenerate the subscription catalog and its manifest. Catalog and configuration-update tests now exercise the shipped alias.

### Why

- The subscription generator exposed Sol Ultrafast but omitted the documented Astra 6.2 Ultrafast choice, so the exact model ID could not be selected without a local override.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- A local override only repairs one installation. The default alias needs to be present in the shipped provider catalog.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the subscription Ultrafast clone filter in `packages/ai/scripts/generate-models.ts` and the regenerated subscription data and manifest.
