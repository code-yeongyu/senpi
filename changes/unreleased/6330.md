## 2026-11-10 - Account stickiness follow-ups (senpi#2987)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/failover.ts`: the in-place same-account retry for a transient failure now waits the server's `retry-after`/`retry-after-ms` hint when it is present and at most 30 seconds (`TRANSIENT_RETRY_AFTER_CAP_MS`); a longer hint falls back to the doubling backoff so a stalled turn fails and session-level retry takes over.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/stream.ts`: the direct `queryWithAuthLane` branch (non-main requests, and main turns with no resident session because `resumeMode` is `"off"` or there is no session id) now passes the session's `preferredAccount`, derived the same way as the resident path (`getSession(sessionId)?.accountName ?? getBinding(sessionId)?.accountName`), instead of letting a fresh HRW selection pick the winning account.
- `packages/coding-agent/test/suite/regressions/2891-anthropic-subscription-account-stickiness.test.ts`: `runTurn` failures now throw `FailedTurnError` carrying `attempts`/`sleeps`/`cause` instead of module-level `attemptLog`/`sleepLog` variables, and three tests cover the `retry-after` wait (seconds, milliseconds, over-cap fallback).
- `packages/coding-agent/test/suite/regressions/2987-anthropic-subscription-account-stickiness.test.ts` (new): regression tests prove through the real resident path with a two-account pool that the next turn prefers the live session's account, a restored binding's account after restart, and that aborting cuts a transient-retry wait short.

### Why

- senpi#2987 items 1–4: the transient retry ignored the server's `retry-after` and always waited the fixed doubling backoff; a summary request re-ran HRW selection and could land on a different account than the session's transcript lives under; test failure logs were module-level variables shared across turns.
- Item 5 (long sessions no longer split across accounts) is by design, observation only, and is not changed by this PR.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The transient-retry wait and the non-main request's account selection live in this builtin provider's own failover and streaming machinery.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the transient-retry branch in `runFailover` and the `queryWithAuthLane` call site in `stream.ts`.
