## 2026-11-27 - External admission reports the selected delivery's pending index (senpi#7590)

### What changed
- `external-admission.ts` derives a pending delivery's own one-based position and distinguishes a replay of a non-pending ID. `agent-session.ts` supplies the bounded hold projection shared with session listings.
- The line a position is counted in is the held peer deliveries a resume takes, oldest first, then the pending ones. The admission port's `heldPeerDeliveries` (senpi#7628) returns those held ids instead of their count, and the held-delivery cap counts them: `engine-turn-hold-notices.ts` and `engine-turn-hold.ts` expose `peerDeliveryIds` in place of `peerDeliveryCount`, and `agent-session.ts` exposes `heldPeerDeliveryIds` in place of `heldPeerDeliveryCount`.

### Why
- A second sender could be told it was first in line: a later state probe cannot reconstruct the position of an earlier admission.
- After a restart the held notices still name the waiting deliveries while the admission ledger starts empty: counting only `pending` told their resends `null` and a new held delivery position 1.

### Why an extension could not handle it
- The receiver owns the admission ledger and is the only participant that can distinguish a live duplicate from a replay of an already-written delivery.

### Expected merge conflict zones
- LOW: the external admission reply sites and the port's `heldPeerDeliveries` callback.

## 2026-11-27 - Distinguish queue position from queue depth (senpi#7590)

### What changed
- `session-control-types.ts`: `ExternalAdmissionResult` includes `queue_position` and the safe hold on pending admission receipts.

### Why
- A target's total backlog is not evidence of one caller's place in line.

### Why an extension could not handle it
- The runtime owns these return types.

### Expected merge conflict zones
- LOW: additive result fields.

## 2026-11-27 - Publish receiver evidence on peer admission receipts (senpi#7590)

### What changed
- `external_admission_position` advertises `queuePosition` and the existing public `hold` shape on RPC admission receipts.
- `admit-external-message-command.ts`: a resend of an id the transcript already holds, a held notice included, answers that delivery's own position and hold while it waits, and `null` once it has been delivered.

### Why
- An older receiver's missing index was being read as position one.
- The written-id guard (senpi#7588) answers before admission, so it told a held delivery's resend `null`.

### Why an extension could not handle it
- The host constructs each RPC reply after performing the admission.

### Expected merge conflict zones
- LOW: append-only capability and response fields.

## 2026-11-27 - Terminal input reports the receiver's pending position (senpi#7590)

### What changed
- `session-control-input-commands.ts` includes the admission's position and bounded hold; an input replay reads its own current position.

### Why
- An old receipt is not evidence that the same delivery is still pending.

### Why an extension could not handle it
- The interactive control endpoint owns the receipt.

### Expected merge conflict zones
- LOW: the input command's receipt projection.
