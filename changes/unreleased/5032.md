## 2026-10-30 - lazyStream keeps an attached provider diagnostic (senpi#3975)

### What changed

- `packages/ai/src/api/lazy.ts`: `createSetupErrorMessage` reads the diagnostic a provider adapter or credential pool attached to the error (`readProviderDiagnostic`) and keeps it on the error message as `providerDiagnostic`.

### Why

- senpi#3975: a credential pool whose every slot is limited throws its refusal through `lazyStream`. The rate-limit diagnostic it attaches, with the soonest unblock time, was dropped there, so the turn read as unclassified and the goal retried at once.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `lazyStream` builds the error message every lazily loaded API returns; no extension sees the thrown error.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the import block and `createSetupErrorMessage` in `lazy.ts`.

## 2026-10-30 - The all-blocked outcome carries the soonest unblock time (senpi#3975)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/credential-pool/failover.ts`: when no slot can serve and every slot not out for auth or a disabled account waits on a rate limit (at least one), the thrown refusal carries `credentialPoolBlockedDiagnostic(soonest rate-limit reset)`; a slot with an unknown block keeps the refusal unclassified, and a disabled slot's re-probe time is never the pool's reset, and a forwarded terminal event goes through the new optional `withPoolRetryAt` hook. This happens only when the refusal is itself a limit (no attempt ran, or the last attempt was blocked for a rate limit): a probe refused for auth, billing or a missing model keeps its own classification and status, which the goal's 401/403 block reads. Pools blocked only for re-authentication are unchanged.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/credential-pool/rotation-stream.ts`: `withPoolRetryAt` keeps a provider-classified limit's own reset (or adds the pool's), turns a failure with no diagnostic or an unclassified 429 into the pool's rate limit, and leaves anything else (auth, a 402/403/404 status) as the provider said.

### Why

- senpi#3975: after the cooling probe failed, the pool forwarded the probed slot's own error, which carried no reset, so the goal retried before any slot could serve.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The refusal is produced inside the pool engine, before any event reaches an extension.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the no-candidate branch of `runCredentialFailover` and the options passed in `streamWithCredentialRotation`.

## 2026-10-30 - All-accounts refusal is a structured rate limit (senpi#3975)

### What changed

- `affinity.ts`: `AllAccountsBlockedError.rateLimitedUntil` is the soonest reset among the rate-limited accounts, set only when every account not out for billing, organization access, or authentication is rate-limited (account-wide, or on the requested model without an active account-level block), with at least one such account. An unknown block is not a rate limit. The refusal text still names every account with its reason, so a billing account is named as billing.
- `provider-diagnostic.ts`: `anthropicSubscriptionDiagnostic` returns `rate_limit` (`account_pool_blocked`, `resetsAt` = `rateLimitedUntil`) only when `rateLimitedUntil` is set; a pool held by billing, organization, or unknown blocks gets no diagnostic. Every account auth-blocked stays `auth` (`account_pool_auth_blocked`). This replaces the senpi#4672 rule that labeled any non-auth pool refusal a rate limit. `stream.ts` keeps one diagnostic source for the failed turn.

### Why

- senpi#3975: the goal's provider-retry wake prefers a structured reset; the lane's refusal carried its time only in prose. A re-probe deadline alone does not prove a rate limit, so billing and organization refusals must not tell the goal to wait for quota.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- This is the lane's own stream error path.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the catch block of `streamAnthropicSubscription`, the `AllAccountsBlockedError` constructor, and the no-candidate branch of `selectAccount`.

## 2026-10-30 - Goal parks on a provider limit with one notice; refused turns are not stalls (senpi#3975)

### What changed

- `monitor-continuation.ts`: a provider-retry park with a known resume time notifies once ("Goal paused until <local time> (<ISO>) ..."). A turn whose last assistant message ended in an error neither counts toward nor clears the toolless stall streak.

### Why

- senpi#3975: refused turns while every credential was limited injected `<goal_stall_check>` after three blocks, and the park was silent.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- This is the goal extension itself.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `afterAgentEnd` around `#recordToollessContinuationTurn`, and the provider-retry branch of `afterAgentSettled`.
