## 2026-11-22 - Late move-state delivery copies are removed; a failed copy is not a delivery (senpi#3987)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/session-move/extension-state.ts`: with nothing pending, `restoreMovedState` removes this session's delivery folders only; the `delivered` marker is written after a successful copy, and a failed copy removes its partial copy.

### Why

- A handler past its cap could recreate a delivery copy that no later open removed, and a failed copy marked the next delivery as a redelivery of state no handler saw.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The delivery bookkeeping is internal to the session-move core.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `restoreMovedState` in `extension-state.ts`.

### Fixed
- A moved session's extension-state delivery no longer leaves late copies behind, and a failed copy no longer marks the next delivery as a redelivery ([#7327](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/senpi/pull/7327))
