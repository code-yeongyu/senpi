## 2026-10-28 - The supervisor records how long owner death took to become an exit (senpi#3392)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-lifecycle-activity.ts`: `SupervisorOwner` stamps the instant it OBSERVES its lifetime owner gone (first observation wins, cleared when a new owner is adopted) and exposes it as `observedGoneAt()`; `OWNER_PROBE_MAX_INTERVAL_MS` is exported beside `OWNER_EXIT_GRACE_MS`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-lifecycle-ticker.ts`: the owner-death shutdown reason is the exported constant `OWNER_GONE_SHUTDOWN_REASON`, so the record that measures its latency cannot drift from the reason that triggers it.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-lifecycle.ts`: the single-flight `shutdown()` stamps the first decision on `performance.now()`, and an owner-death stop passes `ownerGone { at, decisionDelayMs }` into the terminal record.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-child-exit.ts`: `ChildExitContext.shutdown` carries that optional `ownerGone`, and `describeExit` writes `ownerGoneAt` / `decisionDelayMs` onto the terminal record when present.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-crash-record.ts`: `HostCrashRecord` gains the two optional fields.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-lifecycle-activity.ts`: `SupervisorOwner.shouldExit` ADOPTS an owner record that differs from the one it holds (re-reading through `reload()`, which resets the death detection for the new owner) instead of only refusing the exit. The record re-read already happened there; it could previously never act on what it read.

### Why

- A replacement owner reaches the supervisor only through an `fs.watch` event on `owner.json`, and node does not guarantee that event. When it was dropped, the supervisor stayed bound to the dead predecessor: `shouldExit` re-read the record, saw a mismatch and refused forever, so the replacement's own death could not end the generation and the host outlived its owner until the idle window (15 minutes by default). Measured under 12 CPU burners: the generation wrote no terminal record at all while its supervisor was still serving.
- An owner-death exit had no measurable latency anywhere: supervisor stderr lines carry no timestamps, and `uptimeMs` measures the child's whole life. The only way to check "the host exits promptly after its owner dies" (senpi#3044) was wall-clock time measured by the test process, which a loaded machine inflates until the assertion either flakes or has to be widened into uselessness (senpi#3392). Both numbers are now taken on the supervisor's own clocks, so the measurement excludes every reader's scheduling.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The observation and the decision both happen inside the lifecycle supervisor process, which runs before and outside any session; no extension surface sees the owner pipe, the owner probe, or the shutdown decision.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- `host-lifecycle.ts`'s `shutdown()` wrapper and its child `exit` handler, `host-child-exit.ts`'s `describeExit`, and the `HostCrashRecord` field list are all upstream-adjacent; an upstream change to the shutdown signature or the record shape will meet these additions.

### Fixed
- An RPC host whose ownership was handed to a replacement caller no longer outlives that replacement's death when the filesystem watch on its owner record drops an event: the exit decision adopts the record it re-reads ([#4635](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/senpi/pull/4635))

### Added
- An RPC host generation that ends because its lifetime owner died records when the supervisor observed that death and how long it then took to decide to exit (`ownerGoneAt`, `decisionDelayMs` in `crashes.jsonl`), so a slow owner-death exit is visible instead of being guessed from wall-clock time ([#4635](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/senpi/pull/4635))
