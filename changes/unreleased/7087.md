## 2026-11-19 - a public client is authenticated in bounded time on a busy engine, or refused typed (senpi#7055)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/rpc-public-key.ts`: publish a complete, synced key record with a hard link that cannot overwrite an existing launch record. Readers scanning `rpc/keys` no longer see an empty file while the record is being written.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-spare-hop.ts` (new): the supervisor keeps one spare engine hop, already authenticated through the same internal handshake. It classifies itself as an observer on the engine with one observing `get_protocol_info`, discards the engine's broadcasts while idle, goes to exactly one client, and is closed with that client. The refill runs on the supervisor's timer and backs off 0/250/500/1000/2000 ms before it gives up.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-lifecycle-proxy.ts`: `createPublicProxy` takes the spare (`spareHop`, opted in by `host-lifecycle.ts`) before opening a hop of its own, gives every public filter the challenge budget, and accepts injected `timers`. The readiness probe moved to `host-lifecycle-readiness.ts` (moved code).
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/rpc-public-auth.ts`: a challenge the engine has not answered within `CHALLENGE_BUDGET_MS` (1.5 s) is refused with `host_overloaded` and `errorData.retry_after_ms`, and the late answer is dropped. The proof-carrying answer is built by `challengeAnswer()` in `rpc-auth-proof.ts`, and refusal records by `authRefusal()`; both produce the same bytes as before.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/rpc-auth-client.ts`, `rpc-auth-proof.ts`: `RpcEndpointAuthError` gets the code `host_overloaded` and `retryAfterMs`. The client reconnects after `retry_after_ms` when that fits its deadline and otherwise throws the typed error.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-session-errors.ts`: `host_overloaded` maps to `refuse` with `data.retry_after_ms`. `host-socket-request.ts`: probes treat it as a live host that did not answer in time.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/socket-transport.ts`: `Schedule`, `timerSchedule`, `ChallengeBudget` and `CHALLENGE_BUDGET_MS` sit beside `SOCKET_HANDSHAKE_TIMEOUT_MS`.

### Why

- senpi#7055: a short-lived client's handshake waited for a fresh hop handshake (2 engine turns) and then the forwarded challenge (1 more). On an engine whose loop was 1.1-1.4 s behind, the challenge was answered after 3.8-5.3 s, and every client failed silently at its 2 s budget. The client's credential check already ran in the supervisor and does not move.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The supervisor's public proxy and the pre-admission filter run before any session or extension exists.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- MEDIUM: `createPublicProxy` `accept()` in `host-lifecycle-proxy.ts`.
- LOW: `PublicRpcConnection.line()`/`response()` in `rpc-public-auth.ts`; `connectAuthenticatedRpcSocket` in `rpc-auth-client.ts`.

### Fixed
- A supervised RPC host authenticates a new client in bounded time while its sessions keep the engine busy: a spare, already-authenticated engine connection makes the challenge cost one engine turn instead of three, and a challenge the engine cannot answer within 1.5 s gets a typed, retryable `host_overloaded` with `retry_after_ms` instead of a silent `RPC authentication timed out` ([#7087](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/senpi/pull/7087))
