## 2026-11-18 - A hand page's own text is found by the resultText show_html_page emits (senpi#6936)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/html-render/tool.ts`: `ShowHtmlPageDetails.resultText` carries the exact text part of the result.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/html-render/hand-page-budget.ts`: the brain-side over-budget downgrade replaces only the text part equal to `details.resultText` and leaves every other part. When no part equals it, nothing is replaced and the over-budget sentence is appended as its own part. The downgraded details carry the sentence as their `resultText`.

### Why

- senpi#6934. Matching by position replaced a note a hand-side `tool_result` handler put in front of the page text. Matching by path missed a page text a handler rewrote. In both cases a stale "Local images were inlined." could stay next to `imagesInlined: false`.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- show_html_page and the brain-side page budget are this builtin's own code.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `ShowHtmlPageDetails` and the end of `execute` in `html-render/tool.ts`; `applied` and `overBudget` in `html-render/hand-page-budget.ts`.
