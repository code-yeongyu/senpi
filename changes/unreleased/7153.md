## 2026-11-21 - Report auth-blocked status during interactive provider login (senpi#7153)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/interactive/interactive-mode.ts`: `loginProvider` (covering both OAuth and API-key `/login` flows) emits `herdr:blocked` with `{ active: true, kind: "auth", label: "Sign in to <providerName>" }` before calling `modelRuntime.login` and clears it in `finally`.

### Why

- OSC 7501 rev 0.4 defines `state=blocked:kind=auth` for authentication waits; without emitting a block around `loginProvider`, interactive `/login` left OSC 7501 terminals showing `idle` instead of waiting on sign-in.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `loginProvider` and the login dialog lifecycle live inside `InteractiveMode`; extensions do not receive an interactive login end event covering both success and cancellation.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `loginProvider` and its two private call sites in `showApiKeyLoginDialog` and `showLoginDialog` in `interactive-mode.ts`.

## 2026-11-21 - Report OSC 7501 done, error, and auth-blocked states (senpi#7153)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/herdr/herdr-state.ts`: extends `HerdrBlockedKind` with `"auth"`, adds `turnOutcome` (`"idle" | "done" | "error"`) to `HerdrState` and `reduceHerdrState`, and adds `selectProgramStatus(state)` for OSC 7501 reporting while keeping `selectHerdrReport(state)` strictly `"blocked" | "working" | "idle"` for the Herdr Unix socket.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/herdr/index.ts`: subscribes to `agent_end` and `session_abort`, handles `fallbackExhausted` on `agent_settled`, and feeds `selectProgramStatus(state)` into `ctx.ui.setProgramStatus`.

### Why

- The TUI encoder already supported OSC 7501 `done`, `error`, and `blocked:auth`, but the builtin reporter mapped every non-working settled state to `idle`, so OSC 7501 terminals like Rex could not distinguish a completed turn or a failed turn from an idle prompt.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The builtin herdr reporter owns `ctx.ui.setProgramStatus` reporting for every TUI session.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `HerdrState`, `reduceHerdrState`, `selectProgramStatus`, and `isHerdrBlockedEvent` in `herdr-state.ts`; `reportProgramStatus` and the `agent_start`/`agent_settled` handler neighbourhood in `index.ts`.

## 2026-11-21 - Accept plain-text stdout as additionalContext for exit-0 SessionStart hooks (senpi#7153)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/hooks/output-parser.ts`: when a `SessionStart` hook exits `0` and writes non-empty plain text that fails `JSON.parse` with `SyntaxError`, `parseHookOutput` sets `additionalContext` to the trimmed stdout with no `invalid_root` diagnostic. Structured JSON objects keep their existing parsing; non-object JSON values, non-zero exits, and all other hook events keep strict JSON validation.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/hooks/lifecycle-adapter.ts`: `lifecycleResultDetails` reads `summary.output.additionalContext` for `SessionStart` instead of re-parsing stdout as JSON.

### Why

- Claude Code / Codex-compatible `SessionStart` hooks (such as `rex __internal session-start --agent pi`) emit plain-text context on stdout with exit code 0; previously `hooks` discarded the context and recorded an `invalid_root` error diagnostic.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Builtin hooks stdout parsing and lifecycle result collection live inside the builtin `hooks` extension.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `parseHookOutput` and `parseStdoutJson` in `hooks/output-parser.ts`; the `SessionStart` branch of `lifecycleResultDetails` in `hooks/lifecycle-adapter.ts`.
