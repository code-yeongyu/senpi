## 2026-11-15 - A forwarded boundary walk keeps one handler list (senpi#5898)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/runner.ts`: the new `openBoundaryWalk(event, only)` takes the handler list of one forwarded `turn_end` / `agent_before_settle` walk once (a `BoundaryWalk`). `ForwardedBoundary` now carries that `walk` instead of `only`, and every step resumes by index into it. A walk for a different event is refused.

### Why

- Review of #6766 (L8): the stepped boundary rebuilt its handler list at every step, so a handler that subscribed or unsubscribed between steps shifted the indices and a handler was skipped or ran twice. A local walk takes one list.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The runner owns its handler snapshots and the boundary walk.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `emitBoundary`, `ForwardedBoundary` and the new `openBoundaryWalk` in `runner.ts`.

## 2026-11-15 - Hosted loop hooks: wants delivery, integer indices, delta continuity, boundary walks (senpi#5898)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/loopless-loop-hooks.ts`, `loop-hook-reply.ts`: a changed `wants` stays pending until a reply carrying it has passed the wire check and the loop has not cancelled the call; a refused or cancelled reply leaves it for the next one.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/agent-message-schema.ts`: `count` is an integer (`contentIndex`, a boundary's `from`, token counts). The new `amount` (non-negative, may be fractional) covers costs. `compaction-settings-schema.ts` and `model-schema.ts` use `amount` where the type allows fractions.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/loop-hook-message-stream.ts`, `loop-hook-message-events.ts`: every `message_update` carries `seq`. A delta that does not follow the held update gets the reply `{ resync_required: true }` and runs no handler; the loop resends that update with its whole `message`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/loop-hook-boundary-walks.ts` (new), `loop-hook-turn-events.ts`: a stepped boundary's handler list is kept per loop session under an id that `resume.walk` names. A later step sends `{ from, walk }`; a step naming no walk, or one this hand does not hold open, is refused.

### Why

- Review of #6766 (L5-L8): a lost `wants` change, a fractional index that silently dropped a block, a delta applied after a forwarding gap with no error, and a boundary walk whose indices could shift between steps.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The loop-hook link and its wire contract belong to the loopless runtime.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `loop-hook-message-stream.ts`, `loop-hook-turn-events.ts` and the `wants` tracker in `loopless-loop-hooks.ts`.
