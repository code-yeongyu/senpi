## 2026-11-15 - Building a call's reply ends the call for its writes (senpi#6754)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/loop-hook-effects.ts` (new): the call's effects types, `emptyEffects` and `effectsReply`, moved from `loop-hook-call.ts`. `effectsReply` is async: it seals the call (`LoopHookEffects.seal`), settles the session directory, then builds the reply.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/loop-hook-call.ts`: `seal` ends the call's live state at reply build, not in `run`'s `finally`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/hand-session-port.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/loopless-loop-hooks.ts`: await `effectsReply` instead of settling themselves.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/loop-hook-session.ts`: `LoopHookCallEndedError`'s doc lists every refusal after a call ended.

### Why

- Review L1 on #6784: async work between the reply build and the call's `finally` was accepted into effects the reply never carried. After the seal the wake port, relocation host and session manager refuse, and late messages go to the call's late route as late work already does.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The call's lifecycle belongs to the hand engine.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `run` in `loop-hook-call.ts`; the reply lines of `runInSession`/`runAsSession` and of the loop-hook dispatch.
