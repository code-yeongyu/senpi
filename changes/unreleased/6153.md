## 2026-11-07 - A pane idles after its turn while only a monitor or background session is live (senpi#6150)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/herdr/herdr-state.ts`: `selectHerdrReport` reports `working` only for a running turn (blocked first, as before) and `idle` otherwise, matching herdr's own pi integration (`desiredState` in herdr's `integration/assets/pi/herdr-agent-state.ts`); live wake sources (subagents, monitors, background terminal sessions, detached eval cells, DAG runs) are still named in the message of either state.

### Why

- A persistent monitor or a background server kept the pane `working` forever after the turn ended, so front ends drawing the OSC 7501 state showed a spinner on an idle agent.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The builtin herdr reporter is the extension that owns this report; the change is inside it.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `selectHerdrReport` body.

### Fixed
- A herdr pane no longer stays `working` after its turn while background work (a monitor, a background terminal session, a subagent) is live; it reports `idle` and names it, as herdr's own integration does ([#6153](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/senpi/pull/6153))
