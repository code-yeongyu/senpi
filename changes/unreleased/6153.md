## 2026-11-07 - A pane idles after its turn while only a monitor or background session is live (senpi#6150)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/herdr/herdr-state.ts`: `selectHerdrReport` keeps `working` for a running turn and for bounded wake sources (subagents, DAG runs, detached eval cells, loop-guard recovery, unknown sources), and reports `idle` when only open-ended sources (`terminal-monitors`, `terminal-background-sessions`) are live; the message names the live sources in both states.

### Why

- A persistent monitor or a background server kept the pane `working` forever after the turn ended, so front ends drawing the OSC 7501 state showed a spinner on an idle agent.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The builtin herdr reporter is the extension that owns this report; the change is inside it.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `selectHerdrReport` body and the new `OPEN_ENDED_WAKE_SOURCES` set.
