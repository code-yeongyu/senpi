## 2026-10-28 - Nothing writes after MCP dispose: no stdio-probe restart once aborted, dispose awaits reconnects (senpi#4642)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/mcp/modern/stdio-probe.ts`: a close or error that lands after the probe's abort is reported instead of restarting the child, and `restart()` checks the abort before it logs.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/mcp/reconnect.ts`: `disposeMcpReconnect` returns a promise that settles once an attempt already running has settled; a reconnect waiting on its backoff timer is cancelled and never starts; an attempt that dispose overtook is neither marked degraded nor rescheduled.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/mcp/service-connection.ts` and `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/mcp/shared-connection.ts`: dispose awaits that promise after closing the connection.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/mcp/reconnect.ts` (bounded): dispose aborts every running attempt through a signal passed to the reconnect, tracks all overlapping attempts (a manual reconnect beside a timer attempt) and waits for them at most `MCP_RECONNECT_DISPOSE_WAIT_MS` (2 s); an attempt still running then is detached and marks and reschedules nothing. A reconfigure of the same connection is not a teardown: it hands running attempts and a pending backoff to the new state instead of aborting them.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/mcp/auth/oauth-refresh.ts` and `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/mcp/auth/oauth-fetch.ts`: `ensureFresh(signal)`: the aborted reconnect stops waiting at once, so a stalled authorization server cannot hold dispose. The refresh it started stops only before its token request is sent (lock wait, discovery, a retry backoff) and then writes nothing; once the request is sent it finishes detached and persists the rotated tokens. A refresh the reconnect only joined (started without a signal) is not stopped.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/mcp/service.ts`: `whenAttachSettled`'s doc states it covers the startup race only and that `dispose()` is the no-write guarantee.

### Why

- A never-answering stdio child's close after dispose logged a restart line, and a reconnect running at dispose could still spawn and log, recreating a removed agent dir's `logs/mcp/`. Waiting for that reconnect must stay bounded: its OAuth refresh could otherwise hold dispose for minutes on a stalled authorization server.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/mcp/modern/stdio-probe.ts` and `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/mcp/reconnect.ts` own the transport and reconnect lifecycle below any extension hook.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `awaitLateDiscovery`/`restart` in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/mcp/modern/stdio-probe.ts`, `disposeMcpReconnect`/`runReconnectAttempt` in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/mcp/reconnect.ts`, `ensureFresh`/`#doRefresh` in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/mcp/auth/oauth-refresh.ts`, and the dispose bodies in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/mcp/service-connection.ts` and `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/mcp/shared-connection.ts`.
