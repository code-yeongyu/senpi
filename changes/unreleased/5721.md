## 2026-11-04 - Thinking-off is never sent to a thinking-only model (senpi#5720)

### What changed

- `packages/agent/src/agent-loop.ts`: `streamAssistantResponse` sends thinking-off to a model whose level map vetoes `off` at that model's lowest supported level (`clampThinkingLevel(model, "off")`), and records that level on the reply. Explicit levels and models that support `off` are unchanged.

### Why

- A level carried over from another model (a mid-session switch to Kimi K3.1 Pro at `off`) reached the provider unclamped; K3.1 Pro rejects thinking-off with a 400 on every attempt (code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent#12316).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The request's model and level are fixed inside the loop after `prepareRequest`; only the loop sees the pair that is actually sent.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `streamFunction` call and the `result()` thinking-level stamp in `streamAssistantResponse`.

## 2026-11-04 - Kimi K3.1 Pro never receives disabled thinking (senpi#5720)

### What changed

- `packages/ai/src/api/anthropic-messages.ts`: `DISABLED_THINKING_REJECTING_MODEL_MARKERS` gains `k3.1-pro`, so a thinking-off turn on K3.1 Pro (any row, including models.json and gateway rows without catalog compat) sends no `thinking` and effort `low`.

### Why

- Kimi Code answers `thinking: {type: "disabled"}` on K3.1 Pro with `400 K3.1 Pro does not support disabled thinking mode`; no `thinking` plus effort `low` is accepted (code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent#12316).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The thinking block is built inside the Anthropic request builder.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the marker list.

## 2026-11-04 - A disabled-thinking rejection is terminal (senpi#5720)

### What changed

- `packages/ai/src/utils/retry.ts`: `NON_QUOTA_NON_RETRYABLE_PATTERNS` gains the Kimi (`does not support disabled thinking`) and Anthropic (`"thinking.type.disabled" is not supported`) rejections.

### Why

- The same payload fails every time; a gateway wrapping the Kimi 400 in a 500 classified it retryable (code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent#12316).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Retry classification is shared by every retry caller in this package.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the request-shape block of the pattern list.
