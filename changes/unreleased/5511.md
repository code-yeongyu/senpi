## 2026-11-02 - Connection-level failures are retryable (senpi#3826)

### What changed

- `packages/ai/src/utils/retry.ts`: `RETRYABLE_PROVIDER_ERROR_PATTERN` adds `connection.?closed`, `unable to connect`, `failed.?to.?open.?socket` and word-bounded `ECONNREFUSED`/`ECONNRESET`/`ECONNABORTED`/`ETIMEDOUT`/`EHOSTUNREACH`/`ENETUNREACH`/`EPIPE`.

### Why

- senpi#3826: on Bun a refused connect reads `ECONNREFUSED: Unable to connect. Is the computer able to access the url?`, and Node reports bare errno codes; none matched, so the turn ended as an unknown failure and hopped to the fallback model without a same-model retry.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The shared classifier decides retryability for every provider before any extension hook sees the failure.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the network/transport block of `RETRYABLE_PROVIDER_ERROR_PATTERN`.
