## 2026-11-16 - Attribute terminal loss to owned streams

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/interactive/interactive-mode.ts`: uncaught I/O errors attempt terminal restoration and print the fatal error. Unhandled promise rejections keep the established debug-log path. Silent emergency shutdown is selected only by the terminal stream error handlers.

### Why

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/interactive/interactive-mode.ts`: Windows child-process pipes can report `EIO`, `EPIPE`, or `ENOTCONN` while the terminal is still usable. Treating their errno as terminal loss left a drawn TUI over the restored shell prompt.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/interactive/interactive-mode.ts`: interactive mode owns the last-resort crash handler and terminal restoration.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/interactive/interactive-mode.ts`, the `uncaughtCrash` and `unhandledRejection` methods. Keep terminal stream errors on their owned emergency path.

## 2026-11-16 - Ripgrep pipe failures remain search errors

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/tools/grep/rg/process.ts`: handle stdout, stderr and readline Interface error events before consuming output; fail the search after its child closes.

### Why

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/tools/grep/rg/process.ts`: a child pipe's read ENOTCONN escaped to interactive crash handling because output streams and readline had no error listener. Ordinary search, cancellation and stdin behavior remain covered by the real engine contract.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/tools/grep/rg/process.ts`: the runner creates and owns the ripgrep child and its stdio; an extension cannot bind the missing listeners on those internal streams.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `packages/coding-agent/src/core/tools/grep/rg/process.ts` output event registration and child termination. Retain the awaited close boundary and stdin EPIPE handling.

### Fixed
