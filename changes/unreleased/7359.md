## 2026-11-22 - Denied, challenged and empty fetches are failed sources (senpi#4177)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/webfetch/webfetch/source-denial.ts` (new): `assertSourceRetrieved` throws `WebfetchSourceDeniedError` for a short bot-challenge page, an HTTP error status, or an empty response, naming the URL, status and reason and labelling a bounded excerpt as not source content.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/webfetch/webfetch/tool.ts`: calls it after conversion and rendering, before output capping.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/webfetch/webfetch/errors.ts`: adds `WebfetchSourceDeniedError`.

### Why

- Challenge and denial pages reached the model as successful page content, so research answers cited text the site never served.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The change is inside the builtin webfetch tool.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the result assembly in `tool.ts` `execute`; the end of `errors.ts`.

### Fixed
