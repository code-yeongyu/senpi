## 2026-11-01 - Compact admission IDs are capped while live, not for the socket's lifetime (senpi#5274, part of #4272)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-admissions.ts`: the per-epoch `admission_limit` now counts live attempts only. A fresh attempt evicts the least recently used terminal row instead of being refused; a retry that hits a terminal row refreshes it; an evicted ID answers `admission_retired`, and `cancel_attempt` on it answers `already_complete`. `status()` adds `retiredOverflow`, and the first ring overflow in a process logs one stderr line.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-admission-retention.ts` (new): the terminal-row LRU and the eviction that removes the row file, frees its reservation and moves its digest into a bounded ring.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-admission-storage.ts`: `ADMISSION_LIMITS.retired` (4,096) and the limits comment.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-admission-types.ts`: `Epoch` replaces the lifetime `entries` count with the `done` LRU and the `retired` ring.

### Why

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-admissions.ts`: every ID ever admitted on a socket epoch kept its row, so a long-lived compact socket refused every open after 127 completions (RULING-113).
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-admission-retention.ts`: eviction has to free the reserved bytes and the persisted file together so disk accounting stays honest.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-admission-storage.ts`: retries are not distinguishable from fresh opens on the wire, so the ring is sized to need at least 4,096 completions before an ID is forgotten.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-admission-types.ts`: the epoch tracks rows on disk and retired digests instead of a count that never decreases.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Compact admission is host RPC transport state; extensions cannot reach the per-socket admission index.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-admissions.ts`: the registration block in `run()`, its `finally`, `cancel()` and `status()`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-admission-storage.ts`: the `ADMISSION_LIMITS` declaration.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-admission-types.ts`: the `Epoch` fields.

### Fixed
- With compact socket updates enabled, a long-lived socket no longer refuses every open after 127 admissions: only live attempts are capped, recent retries still get their stored result, and older IDs are rejected as retired instead of running again ([#5274](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/senpi/issues/5274))
