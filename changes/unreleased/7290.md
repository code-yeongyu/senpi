## 2026-11-22 - Reply when a worker grant answer is refused (senpi#4353)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-worker-client.ts` sends `grant_answer_refused` to the submitting connection when the existing grant check refuses its answer.

### Why

- Silently dropping the answer left the submitting client unable to distinguish refusal from acceptance.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The worker binding refuses the command before any extension sees it.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- The grant-response branch in the worker binding.

<!-- changes-log: packages/coding-agent/CHANGELOG.md -->
### Fixed
- Worker sessions acknowledge a refused grant answer with its own frame ID, as in-process sessions do ([#7290](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/senpi/pull/7290)).
