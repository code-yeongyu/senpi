## 2026-11-03 - A stale pre-#4436 writer no longer loses signed-in Claude accounts (senpi#5594)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/anthropic-legacy-sign-out.ts`: `signOutLegacyClaudeLogins` takes an `accountLinks` reader. When the entry holds a token slot, every recorded Claude CLI account dir that no slot references is re-linked to its account (`relinkAccountDirs`) instead of the account being left with a fresh `dirId` and a sign-in prompt.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/auth-storage.ts`: the load seam passes `readAccountLinks(dirname(authPath))` (read lazily, only when a token slot is found).

### Why

- An engine from before senpi#4436 that is still running writes its token-shaped copy of the entry back over the Claude CLI slots; the next load gave the account a new dir and orphaned the dir holding the working login, and a second account the stale copy did not hold was lost (senpi#5594).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The sign-out runs at the auth.json load seam in core, before any extension loads.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `signOutLegacyClaudeLogins` call in `parseStorageContent`.

## 2026-11-03 - Signed-in account dirs record their account (senpi#5594)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/claude-cli-account-link.ts` (new): `writeAccountLink` / `readAccountLinks` keep `.senpi-account.json` (slot name + `signedInAt`, no token, 0600) inside each signed-in account dir; `relinkAccountDirs` re-links unreferenced dirs (newest sign-in per name wins, ties by dir id; a healthy slot keeps its dir; a missing account is appended).
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/oauth-login.ts`: a login writes the record right after it claims its slot.

### Why

- auth.json alone cannot keep the slot-to-dir link while an older engine can overwrite the entry (senpi#5594).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- This is the extension's own login path.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the claim step in `login()` of `oauth-login.ts`.
