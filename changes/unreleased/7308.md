## 2026-11-22 - A device run says why its process ended (senpi#7282)

### What changed

- `packages/agent/src/execution/device-files.ts`: `DeviceRunResult` gains an optional `termination: { kind }` (`exited`, `signaled`, `timed_out`) and `terminationKind()`.
- `packages/agent/src/execution/device-files-node.ts`: the fused `run` reports `timed_out` only when its timer fired and the child then ended on a signal it sent itself; a child that had already exited, or survived the signal and ended otherwise, is reported as observed.
- `packages/agent/src/execution/remote-device-files.ts`: the decoder keeps a known `termination` and leaves it absent for a hand that sends none or an unknown kind.
- `packages/agent/src/index.ts`: exports `DeviceRunTerminationKind` and `terminationKind`.

### Why

- A caller saw only `code` and `signal`, so its own timeout and an external SIGTERM looked the same. Split-session memory needs to know a git commit timed out before it reconciles it (omo#12587).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The timer lives in the process that spawns the child; only that side knows it fired.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `DeviceRunResult` in `device-files.ts` and the run decoder in `remote-device-files.ts`.

## 2026-11-22 - The hand reports a run's timeout as timed_out (senpi#7282)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/hand-run-ops.ts`: `spawnBounded` records whether its timer fired and which signals it actually delivered, and the run outcome carries `termination` attributed from the observed exit.

### Why

- The hand stopped a timed-out child with SIGTERM and sent only the signal, so the caller could not tell its timeout from any other SIGTERM.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The run's timer and child live inside the hand's run operation.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `spawnBounded` and `RunOutcome` in `hand-run-ops.ts`.
