## 2026-11-22 - A device run reports how its process ended and its timer provenance (senpi#7282)

### What changed

- `packages/agent/src/execution/device-files.ts`: `DeviceRunResult` gains an optional `termination`: `kind` is the observed outcome (`exited` or `signaled`); `timeout: { expired: true, signalsSent }` is present only when the spawning side's own timer expired before that side had observed the child's exit, and lists the signals it then requested. It does not claim the child was alive, that a signal was delivered, or that it ended the child. `runTermination()` builds the record.
- `packages/agent/src/execution/device-files-node.ts`: the fused `run` records that provenance. The timer acts one loop turn later (`setImmediate`), after pending child exits are reaped, so a child that ended on its own before the deadline is neither signalled nor marked expired; the pending step is cancelled when the child closes, so no grace timer starts after close.
- `packages/agent/src/execution/remote-device-files.ts`: the decoder keeps a known `kind` and well-formed timer provenance; a hand that sends none, an unknown kind, or malformed provenance yields no claim (absent, or `kind` alone).
- `packages/agent/src/index.ts`: exports `DeviceRunTermination`, `DeviceRunTerminationKind`, `DeviceRunTimeout` and `runTermination`.

### Why

- A caller saw only `code` and `signal`, so its own timeout and an external SIGTERM looked the same (omo#12587). An equal exit signal after a requested SIGTERM cannot prove the timer caused the exit (a child may survive it and end on the same signal later), so the observed outcome and the timer provenance are separate fields.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The timer lives in the process that spawns the child; only that side knows it expired and what it sent.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `DeviceRunResult` in `device-files.ts` and the run decoder in `remote-device-files.ts`.

## 2026-11-22 - The hand reports a run's timer provenance next to its observed end (senpi#7282)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/hand-run-ops.ts`: `spawnBounded` records whether its own timer expired before it observed the child's exit and which signals it then requested, and the run outcome carries `termination` (observed `kind` plus that provenance). The timer acts one loop turn later, after pending child exits are reaped, and is cancelled when the child closes.

### Why

- The hand stopped a timed-out child with SIGTERM and sent only the signal, so the caller could not tell its timeout from any other SIGTERM.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The run's timer and child live inside the hand's run operation.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `spawnBounded` and `RunOutcome` in `hand-run-ops.ts`.

### Added
- A device `run` result reports how the process ended (`termination.kind`: `exited` or `signaled`) and, when the spawning side's own timer expired before it observed the exit, `termination.timeout` with the signals that timer requested. It records what the timer asked for, not whether the process was alive, received a signal, or was ended by it ([#7308](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/senpi/pull/7308))
