## 2026-11-22 - Carry a local sign-in requirement on assistant errors (senpi#5722)

### What changed

- `packages/ai/src/types.ts` accepts a `sign_in_required` error cause containing local account labels.

### Why

- Clients need to distinguish a required sign-in from a provider rejecting a request.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The assistant message contract is shared across providers and the agent.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the optional `AssistantMessage.errorCause` union.

## 2026-11-22 - Preserve the typed Claude sign-in error (senpi#5722)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/stream.ts` carries the account labels from `ClaudeSignInRequiredError` onto the failed message.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/account-context.ts` shares its operational account label filter with the stream.

### Why

- The adapter previously retained the guidance text but lost the distinction between sign-in and rejection.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The built-in adapter is the last boundary with the local exception object.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the stream error handler and account label helper.

## 2026-11-22 - Distinguish sign-in requirements on agent_end (senpi#5722)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/provider-failure.ts` projects the local cause and its account labels into `agent_end.failure`.

### Why

- A sign-in-required turn should not tell the user the provider rejected the credentials.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Core owns the terminal failure contract and publishes the event.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the failure categories and the terminal message projection.
