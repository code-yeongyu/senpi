## 2026-11-28 - An admitted delivery is reported to extensions at admission (senpi#7610)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/external-admission.ts`: `admit()` is a thin wrapper around the unchanged admission body (`admitOnce`). When the delivery id was not pending before the call and is pending after it (started, queued, steered or held), it calls each `onAdmitted` listener with `{ delivery_id, text }`. `onAdmitted(listener)` sits beside `onEmitted`; a refused delivery (`already_admitted`, `turn_conflict`, `held_draft`, a closed admission) calls none.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/agent-session.ts`: the constructor subscribes `externalAdmission.onAdmitted` once and emits `external_message_admitted` through the extension runner current at emit time; a failed emit is logged as `external_message_admitted_emit_failed`.

### Why

- oh-my-openagent#15221 (row 25) passes a child session's outside-send notice up its chain of openers, one hop at a time, on receipt. A delivery from another device to a running session is admitted here, and an extension otherwise saw it only once a turn processed the entry, so a busy opener could not pass it on.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Admission of an `admit_external_message` record happens inside the engine (`ExternalAdmission.admit`), before any extension event; the session-control drain an extension registers sees only deliveries it admitted itself.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `admit()` signature line in `external-admission.ts` (the body below it is unchanged) and the listener fields next to `emittedListeners`; one block after `this._extensionRunnerRef = config.extensionRunnerRef;` in the `AgentSession` constructor.

## 2026-11-28 - external_message_admitted extension event (senpi#7610)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/session-control-types.ts`: `ExternalMessageAdmittedEvent` (`type: "external_message_admitted"`, `delivery_id`, `text`).
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/types.ts`: the event joins the extension event union, with its `pi.on("external_message_admitted", ...)` overload.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/event-placement.ts`: placed `loop`, as `session_control_wake`.

### Why

- Lets an extension act on a delivery another session sent the moment it is admitted, before any turn takes it (oh-my-openagent#15221 row 25).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The event is the extension-facing contract itself; it carries only the delivery's id and the text the model reads.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `session_control_wake` neighbours in the event union, the `on()` overloads and the placement table.
