## 2026-10-30 - An exhausted fallback ladder reports its soonest reset on settle (senpi#3709)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/retry-fallback/limit-reset.ts` (new): `limitResetAt` returns the wall-clock reset a failed request names. It reads the structured quota or rate-limit `resetsAt` first, then usage-limit reset fields or prose, then a Retry-After hint.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/agent-session.ts`: records `limitResetAt` and limit-shaped failures for each run and notes when `retry_fallback_exhausted` fires. At settle it emits `agent_settled.fallbackExhausted` only after limit-shaped exhaustion; outage-only exhaustion keeps the existing provider recovery. `resumeAt` is the soonest future reset, or the latest collected past reset when all passed, so the goal resumes with the 1 s margin rather than parking forever. The per-run state is consumed once and cleared. The fallback wall clock is now held as `_fallbackWallNow`.

### Why

- Once the ladder ran out, the reset times the rungs named were dropped. A goal session then either resumed at once into the same limits or never resumed, and sat idle until input (senpi#3709).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Only the session sees every rung's failure and knows that the ladder ran out. Extensions get `agent_end` before the retry owner decides, and only for the last rung's message.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `_emit` exhaustion flag, the `agent_end` error bookkeeping next to `this._lastAssistantMessage = undefined`, and the `agent_settled` extension emit in `_emitAgentSettled`.

## 2026-10-30 - AgentSettledEvent.fallbackExhausted (senpi#3709)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/types.ts`: `AgentSettledEvent` gains an optional `fallbackExhausted?: { resumeAt?: number }`. It is present when every fallback rung failed and at least one failure was limit-shaped; `resumeAt` (epoch ms) is the soonest future reset the run named, or its latest past reset if all passed. `docs/extensions.md` documents it.

### Why

- A resumer, such as the goal extension, needs to know that the ladder is spent and when it can recover, so it can wait for that reset instead of retrying into the same limits.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- This is the event contract itself. The session is the only producer of the information.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `AgentSettledEvent` interface.

## 2026-10-30 - Goal parks until the soonest reset after an exhausted fallback ladder (senpi#3709)

### What changed

- `monitor-continuation.ts` `afterAgentSettled(fallbackExhausted)`: on a limit-exhausted ladder the generic provider recovery is replaced. With a `resumeAt`, the goal arms one `providerRetry` wait for that time (or the 1 s margin when it already passed); without one, it arms nothing and stays parked until input. Outage-only exhaustion retains immediate first recovery and the existing 15 s to 30 min backoff.
- `provider-retry.ts` `exhaustedLadderResumeDelayMs`: waits until `resumeAt` + 1 s, skips the 6 h wake cap, and is clamped to the timer maximum.
- `index.ts` passes `event.fallbackExhausted` to `afterAgentSettled`.

### Why

- senpi#3709: after exhaustion the goal either resumed at once into the same limits or never resumed.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The change is inside this builtin extension, driven by the new `agent_settled` field.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `afterAgentSettled` in `monitor-continuation.ts`; the provider-retry delay helpers.
