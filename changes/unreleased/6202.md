### Fixed
- The terminal pipe fallback (used when the native PTY binding is unavailable) settles a session when its process exits, after a short drain of the output already written, instead of waiting for every process holding stdout or stderr to close. A command that left a process running (`cmd &`, a daemon, or the children of a shell killed from outside) no longer keeps its background terminal session, and the agent's `waiting_background` wait, open for that process's whole life. The native PTY already settled when the shell exited ([#6202](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/senpi/pull/6202), part of #6201)
