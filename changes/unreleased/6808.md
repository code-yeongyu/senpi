## 2026-11-15 - A cancelled loop-hook call's effects reach the loop as wakes (senpi#5898)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/loop-hook-call.ts`: when the loop cancels a call, the effects its handlers had buffered for the reply (messages, notices, entries, user messages) are sent through the late route (#6767's wake outbox), and every effect after the cancel goes there too. The call stays live for its handlers until they return.
- Correction to the #6753 entry: `LoopHookCall.run` takes a `LoopHookCallBinding` (it named a `LoopHookCallScope`, which does not exist).

### Why

- Review of #6753 (L1, L2): the loop host drops a cancelled call's reply, so effects buffered for it were lost, against #6767's rule that every effect reaches the loop at least once.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The loop-hook call and its effect routing belong to the loopless runtime.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `run` in `loop-hook-call.ts`.
