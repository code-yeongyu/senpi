## 2026-10-30 - Restoring the primary waits for its own account hold (senpi#3347)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/retry-fallback/controller.ts`: `maybeRestorePrimary` does not restore an original that `UnusableEntries` still holds (an account usage limit with the reset it named, or a model-scoped exhaustion). The stranded-fallback exception (senpi#2376) still wins.

### Why

- senpi#3347: the shared circuit caps an open at 30 minutes, while the account hold lasts until the reset the provider named. With a 2-hour reset the session went back to the primary at about 31 minutes, hit the same spent account, and fell back again at every circuit window.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The restore decision is internal to the retry-fallback controller; no extension hook runs between the circuit check and the switch back.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the restore guard in `maybeRestorePrimary` and its doc comment.
