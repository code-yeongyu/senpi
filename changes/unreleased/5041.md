## 2026-10-30 - Restoring the primary waits for its own account hold; an account change drops the hold (senpi#3347)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/retry-fallback/controller.ts`: `maybeRestorePrimary` does not restore an original that `UnusableEntries` still holds (an account usage limit with the reset it named, or a model-scoped exhaustion). The stranded-fallback exception (senpi#2376) still wins. New `noteProviderAccountsChanged(provider)` drops that provider's holds, and `followProviderAccountChanges(controller)` feeds it every `accounts_changed` event.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/retry-fallback/unusable.ts`: `clearProvider(provider, models)` clears the provider's `account:` hold, its recorded account error and every `exhausted:` entry of its models.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/agent-session.ts`: the session follows provider account changes for its controller and stops following on `dispose()`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/model-runtime.ts`: `login()` emits `accounts_changed` for the provider after a successful login.

### Why

- senpi#3347: the shared circuit caps an open at 30 minutes, while the account hold lasts until the reset the provider named. With a 2-hour reset the session went back to the primary at about 31 minutes, hit the same spent account, and fell back again at every circuit window.
- Waiting out the hold must not outlast the remedy the refusal names: after `/login` (a re-login or an added account) nothing cleared the session's hold, so a weekly limit kept the session on the fallback until the 48-hour cap.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The restore decision and the hold it reads are internal to the retry-fallback controller; no extension hook runs between the circuit check and the switch back, and the login path is core.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the restore guard in `maybeRestorePrimary`, the controller construction and `dispose()` in `agent-session.ts`, and `login()` in `model-runtime.ts`.

## 2026-10-30 - Every successful RPC login announces the provider's account change (senpi#3347)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/connection-handler.ts`: `login_start` emits `accounts_changed` (forwarded as `auth_accounts_changed`) after any successful login, not only an Anthropic Subscription one.

### Why

- A session that fell back off a usage limit drops the provider's hold on that event; a login through RPC (the desktop) for any provider must reach it.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The RPC login handler is core.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the success branch of `login_start`.
