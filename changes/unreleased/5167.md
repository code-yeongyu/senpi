## 2026-10-31 - Notification wakes back off while the session keeps answering without a tool call (senpi#5164)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/terminal/notification-wake-gate.ts`: new session-scoped `NotificationWakeGate` shared by monitor and terminal notifications. It counts consecutive turns that ended without a tool call. From the second such turn, a notification that would start a turn waits 30 s after the last turn ended, doubling per further idle turn up to 300 s (the default pace of a persistent monitor). Notifications that arrive meanwhile join the held wake and go out as one message. A turn that starts for another reason takes them as steers. A tool call or person input ends the backoff. A monitor delivery carrying a person's words (`human_input`) is never held and sends anything held with it.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/terminal/extension.ts`: `TerminalNotifier` and `MonitorNotifier` send through the gate. `agent_start`, `agent_end`, `tool_call` and person `input` feed it, and `session_shutdown` disposes it.

### Why

- Some models answer a non-decisive notification with an empty, reasoning-only reply. The 2 s coalescing window only merges events that land together, and the wake budget's streak resets after a quiet gap, so a slow stream (push gates, CI steps) woke an idle session once per line even while every wake produced nothing. In a replay of recorded sessions, about a third of notification-started turns on those models ended empty.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Notification delivery, its coalescing and its wake options are owned by the terminal builtin; an outside extension cannot hold or merge another extension's `sendMessage` wakes.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `session_start` notifier construction and the `agent_start` / `agent_end` / `input` / `tool_call` / `session_shutdown` handlers in `extension.ts`.

### Fixed

- A session that keeps answering monitor or terminal notifications without a tool call is no longer woken once per notification: from the second idle turn, wakes back off (30 s doubling to 300 s) and notifications that arrive meanwhile are delivered together, never dropped; a tool call, person input or a `human_input` monitor ends the backoff ([#5167](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/senpi/pull/5167))
