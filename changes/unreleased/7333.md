## 2026-11-22 - A failed staged OAuth flow keeps the signed-in grant (senpi#6339)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/mcp/auth/oauth-provider.ts`: `invalidateCredentials` on a flow that uses a staged registration discards only that flow's state.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/mcp/auth/registered-redirect.ts`: exports `usesStagedRegistration`, adds `withoutStagedFlowCredentials`, and keeps a known client's `redirectUrisSent` on re-save.

### Why

- A rejected code under a staged client deleted the signed-in client's refresh token, and the SDK's invalid-client retry cleared the whole record, although that flow never presented the signed-in grant.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The MCP OAuth provider and its token store are internal to the builtin MCP extension.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `invalidateCredentials` in `oauth-provider.ts`; `withRegistration` in `registered-redirect.ts`.
