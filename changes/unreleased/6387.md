## 2026-11-11 - Job widgets: every running background bash session has a row below the editor (senpi#6386)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/job-widgets/registry-job.ts`: a running bash job has an entry from its start, `fallback` until a spec is installed, and keeps it after a skip, a held-back request or a failed author; a finished bash job still `fallback` (spec never installed) is sent as `done`. Each entry carries `startedAtMs`. Task jobs and finished never-asked or skipped jobs are unchanged.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/job-widgets/view-types.ts`: `JobWidgetEntry.startedAtMs?` (optional, additive).
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/job-widgets/tui-bash-rows.ts` (new): `JobBashRows`, `setWidget("job-widgets-bash", …, { placement: "belowEditor" })`, one row per bash job in the shape of the background task rows: spinner, command, id, `running` (or a live spec's headline), elapsed; a `✓ … done` row for 10 seconds after the job ends or leaves the state; at most five rows plus `+N more`. It ticks every 250 ms only while a job runs.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/job-widgets/extension.ts`: the TUI feeds bash jobs to `JobBashRows` and only task jobs to the `JobWidgetArea` above the editor, so a job is never drawn twice.

### Why

- A silent background command had no row before its spec request (15 s) and none after the author skipped or failed, so it looked like nothing was running, while background subagents beside it always had a row (senpi#6386).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The job widget registry and its TUI area are this builtin's own state; the fix is inside it.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- Fork-only files. `JobWidgetJob.entry()` in `registry-job.ts`, the `showInTui` block in `extension.ts`.
