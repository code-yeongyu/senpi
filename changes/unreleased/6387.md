## 2026-11-11 - Job widgets: every running background bash session is visible in the TUI (senpi#6386)

### What changed

- `job-widgets/registry-job.ts`: a bash job has an entry from its start, `fallback` until a spec is installed, and keeps it after a skip, a held-back request or a failed author; a finished bash job is `done` (frozen chart, or the plain view when it never got one) unless the author skipped it, and carries the `outcome` its host reported. Each entry carries `startedAtMs`. Task jobs are unchanged.
- `job-widgets/registry.ts`: `JobWidgetSourceHandle.settle(outcome?)`; `terminal/job-widget-tap.ts` passes the session's exit (`success` / `failed` / `killed`).
- `job-widgets/view-types.ts`: `JobWidgetEntry.startedAtMs?`, `JobWidgetEntry.outcome?`, `JobWidgetOutcome` (optional, additive).
- `job-widgets/state-publisher.ts`: a change that is only a `fallback` view's `stage`/`lines` waits `FALLBACK_INTERVAL_MS` (5 s); any other change keeps the 1 s beat.
- `job-widgets/tui-bash-rows.ts` (new): `JobBashRows`, `setWidget("job-widgets-bash", …, { placement: "belowEditor" })`, one row per bash job without a chart (`isChartedBashJob` false) in the shape of the background task rows, themed (muted `+N more`, success/error marks, plain under `NO_COLOR`); `✓ done` / `✗ failed` / `✗ killed` for 10 seconds after the job ends; at most five rows plus `+N more`. It repaints only when its text changes (once a second, once a minute past the first hour).
- `job-widgets/extension.ts`: the area above the editor gets task jobs and charted bash jobs, the rows below get the other bash jobs, so a job is never drawn twice.
- `job-widgets/tui-line.ts`: a finished job that failed or was killed has its id in the error colour.

### Why

- A silent background command had no row before its spec request (15 s) and none after the author skipped or failed, so it looked like nothing was running, while background subagents beside it always had a row (senpi#6386).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The job widget registry and its TUI area are this builtin's own state; the fix is inside it.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- Fork-only files. `JobWidgetJob.entry()` in `registry-job.ts`, the `showInTui` block in `extension.ts`.
