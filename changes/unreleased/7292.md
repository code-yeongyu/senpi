## 2026-11-22 - Show a cost lower bound for unpriced-only sessions (senpi#4075)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/interactive/interactive-mode.ts` and `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/interactive/session-info-format.ts` show the existing unpriced marker even when the known sum is zero.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/interactive/components/footer.ts` includes `costUnknown` in the cost-segment display condition.

### Why

- Suppressing a zero known sum hid the distinction between genuinely free usage and unknown pricing.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The cost section is rendered by the built-in session views.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- The cost-section gate in each view. For the #2471 footer segment, cost is now displayed for known spend, unpriced turns, subscription usage, or subagent spend.
