## 2026-11-27 - Filesystem policies are closed to registration once composed (senpi#7576)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/runtime/bootstrap.ts`: after composing the extensions' filesystem policies, `bootstrapRuntime` sets `filesystemPoliciesComposed` on the extension runtime, so a later `registerFilesystemPolicy` is refused instead of recorded.

### Why

- A policy registered after composition (the oh-my-openagent memory guard registered on `session_start`) was recorded and reported but never consulted by the built-in tools (omo#15251).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Only the engine knows when it composed the policies its tools enforce.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the lines after `composeFilesystemPolicies(...)` in `bootstrapRuntime`.

## 2026-11-27 - Filesystem policies can prune walked entries; late registration is refused (senpi#7576)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/types.ts`: `FilesystemPolicy` gains optional `excludesEntry(canonicalPath, toolName)` and `prunesBelow(canonicalRoot, toolName)`; `FilesystemPolicyChecker` exposes `entryExclusionBelow(root, toolName)`; `registerFilesystemPolicy` returns `FilesystemPolicyRegistration` (`{ applied: true, entryPruning: true }` or `{ applied: false, reason }`); `ExtensionRuntimeState.filesystemPoliciesComposed`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/loader.ts`: `registerFilesystemPolicy` refuses, with a warning, a policy registered after the runtime's policies were composed, and answers the registration result.

### Why

- omo#12466: an extension guarding memory identities needs listing and search walks to leave other identities out at the source, including ones created while the walk runs. omo#15251: a policy registered after composition must not look applied.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The walks run inside the built-in `find`, `ls` and `grep` tools; an extension sees only their finished results.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- MEDIUM: the `FilesystemPolicy` / `FilesystemPolicyChecker` block and `registerFilesystemPolicy` in `types.ts`; `registerFilesystemPolicy` in `loader.ts`.

## 2026-11-27 - find, ls and grep apply a policy's entry pruning before counting (senpi#7576)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/tools/ls.ts`: entries a policy excludes are dropped before the limit and notices.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/tools/find.ts`: entries a policy excludes are dropped; a page left short of `limit` is refilled by asking the walk for twice as many, so the page and its limit notice read as if those entries did not exist.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/tools/grep/index.ts`, `grep/engine.ts`, `grep/rg-engine.ts`, `grep/rg/enumerate.ts`: a walk that may prune runs on the rg engine, whose candidate enumeration drops excluded files before ordinals, binary counts, `filesSearched` and the search argv. A walk below a root no policy prunes keeps the native engine.

### Why

- omo#12466: a pruned entry must leave no trace in the result: no line, no count, no notice beyond what an empty directory would give.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Counts, limits and scan statistics are computed inside the tools.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the policy check blocks in `find.ts`, `ls.ts` and `grep/index.ts`; `enumerateCandidates` in `grep/rg/enumerate.ts`.
