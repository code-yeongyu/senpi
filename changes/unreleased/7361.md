## 2026-11-22 - An out-of-credit refusal is worded as out of credit (senpi#5518)

### What changed

- `packages/ai/src/utils/provider-failure-description.ts`: `describeProviderFailureForUser` with `usageLimit: "account"` words a credit-balance, `insufficient_quota` or credits-required refusal as "The <provider> account is out of credit. Add credit to it, or remove its API key so the session uses another provider." instead of the usage-limit sentence.

### Why

- An empty credit balance never resets, so "usage limit has been reached" with a reset hint sent users to wait or switch instead of adding credit.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The user-facing failure wording is produced inside pi-ai.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `usageLimit === "account"` branch of `describeProviderFailureForUser`.
