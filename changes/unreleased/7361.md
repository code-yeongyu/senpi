## 2026-11-22 - An out-of-credit refusal is worded as out of credit (senpi#5518)

### What changed

- `packages/ai/src/utils/provider-failure-description.ts`: `describeProviderFailureForUser` describes credit-balance, `insufficient_quota`, and credits-required refusals for both account and model scopes. It tells users to add credit or choose another configured provider.

### Why

- An empty credit balance does not reset. A refusal naming a model can be scoped to that model, so formatting only account-scoped refusals left model-scoped errors unexplained.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The user-facing failure wording is produced inside pi-ai.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the usage-limit wording in `describeProviderFailureForUser`.
