## 2026-11-22 - Trash tool images with the selected session (#6608)

### What changed
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/interactive/components/session-selector.ts` sends media to trash after the transcript; it reports a refused media move and leaves the files intact.

### Why
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/interactive/components/session-selector.ts` permanently deleted the images even when the transcript could be restored.

### Why an extension could not handle it
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/interactive/components/session-selector.ts` owns the selector's trash-versus-unlink decision.

### Expected merge conflict zones
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/interactive/components/session-selector.ts`: `deleteSessionFile` and the trash-command result handling.

## 2026-11-22 - Invalidate image byte reservations after trash succeeds (#6608)

### What changed
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/tool-media-store.ts` forgets the cached quota for a directory moved to trash.

### Why
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/tool-media-store.ts` must rescan a restored or recreated directory; a refused move must retain the existing reservation.

### Why an extension could not handle it
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/tool-media-store.ts` owns the byte accounting and validates the media root.

### Expected merge conflict zones
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/tool-media-store.ts`: session media cleanup and the `reserved` map.
