## 2026-10-30 - Validate account selection strategy (senpi#5068)

### What changed

## 2026-10-30 - Select accounts by configured strategy (senpi#5068)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/credential-pool/selection-order.ts`: share cache-affine, stored-order fill-first, and round-robin ordering.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/credential-pool/round-robin-state.ts`: hold process-local per-provider cursors and account choices for stable affinity keys.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/credential-pool/rotation-slots.ts`: carry the strategy in the rotation policy.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/credential-pool/rotation-stream.ts`: apply strategy ordering before the inherited headroom preference and retain round-robin replacements after a block or skip.

### Why

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/credential-pool/rotation-stream.ts` previously offered only affinity ordering, not stored-order filling or cursor-based distribution of new sessions.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/credential-pool/rotation-stream.ts` selects credentials before dispatching provider requests.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: imports and selection callback in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/credential-pool/rotation-stream.ts`, ordering helpers in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/credential-pool/selection-order.ts`, and the policy type in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/credential-pool/rotation-slots.ts`. Failure classification and block lifetimes are unchanged.

## 2026-10-30 - Honor Claude account strategy (senpi#5068)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/settings.ts`: load and validate `anthropicSubscriptionProvider.accountStrategy` with project-over-global precedence.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/affinity.ts`: use shared strategy ordering and remember round-robin choices, preserving pins, resident preference, disabled fallback, and headroom selection.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/auth-lane.ts`: forward the configured strategy into request selection.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/account-command.ts`: show the strategy's account choice in account listings.

### Why

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/affinity.ts` owns account selection independently of the generic pool, so it must honor the same distribution choices.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/affinity.ts` is already the builtin provider's selection owner.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: selection branches in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/affinity.ts`, provider settings parsing, and selector options in the auth lane and account command.
