## 2026-10-27 - Bounded escalation and the typed Repair result: reason, action, repair anchor, busy-owner wait (desktop#4199)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/endpoint-reservation-store.ts`: moves to schema 6. A reservation carries `escalation` (the escalated reason recovery persisted for the entry it could not resolve: `revision`, `subject`, `reason`, `observations`) and `repairAnchor` (`revision`, `kernelSession`); both are read only at the record's current revision, so any transition discards them, and neither bumps the revision. `escalate`, `clearEscalation` and `recordRepairAnchor` (refuses an unverified session with `anchor_unverifiable`) write them on a fenced generation. `RecoveryEscalationReason` names the five design reasons. A schema-5 record is refused.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-decision.ts`: `ReservationRecoveryRefusal` gains `action` (`RecoveryAction`: `wait_for_owner`, `drain_owner`, `restart_computer`, `resolve_on_recorded_host`, `restore_store_access`, `retry`), `holder` (`entry`, `hostLabel`, `kernelSession`) and, for the unknown reasons, `escalation` (`reason`, `observations`, `cached`).
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/endpoint-recovery.ts`: `recoveryProber` takes options (`observe`, `clock`, `remainingMs`, `fresh`, `held`). An `ObservationBudget` per attempt gives each recorded identity an initial reopen and at most one requery inside the smaller of the caller's remaining deadline and `OBSERVATION_BUDGET_MS` (5 s), with the deadline armed once as an event and a late answer never read; what stays unknown is persisted as the escalated reason and refused with the typed action, holder and escalation. An unchanged escalated record answers an automatic attempt from its marker without a query (`cached`), while presence objects are still probed and the kernel session still read; `fresh` opens one new bounded assessment; a fresh attempt that resolves the record to `live` drops the marker. The gate's two outcomes are typed (`owner_identity_domain_mismatch` / `_unverified`), a missing or noncomparable identity is `owner_identity_incomplete`, a denied presence open is `restore_store_access`. A proven prior boot (the Revision 6 proof, or a repair anchor on a store recorded and still classified `local` under a verified different session) discharges process evidence without a query. A guard the busy-owner wait acquired (`held`) is step 3's acquisition of its object through `presenceProbeRetained`, released after the retirement commit or at once when the attempt cannot retire.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/endpoint-repair.ts` (new): `repairEndpointReservations`, the engine half of the Repair connection action. One bounded check under the endpoint lock as a new assessment; when it finds a presence object held, the bridge's blocking cancellable wait outside the endpoint lock and every transaction, then the check again holding the wait's handle; with `confirmRestart`, the repair anchor when the refusal offers `restart_computer` (`anchor: null` under an unverified session). Dismissal cancels the wait and releases nothing it did not acquire.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-runner.ts`: `HostRequest` gains `{ action: "repair", target, confirmRestart?, signal? }`, refused `unsupported_endpoint_kind` for a tui endpoint and `unsupported_platform` off the reservation route; it answers `{ action: "repair", recovered }` (exit 0), the `reservation_recovery_required` refusal with `recovery` and `anchor` (exit 3), or `cancelled` (exit 3).
- `packages/coding-agent/src/cli/host-command-args.ts` and `packages/coding-agent/src/cli/host-command.ts`: `senpi host repair [--confirm-restart] [--socket <path>] [--json]`; SIGINT and SIGTERM cancel an outstanding wait.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-ensure-start.ts`: the start's recovery passes its readiness deadline as the caller's remaining budget and throws the whole typed refusal.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/socket-secret-native.ts`: `presenceProbeRetained` is part of the presence binding the loader requires.
- `crates/senpi-socket-secret`: `PresenceRegistry::probe_retained` and the `presenceProbeRetained` export apply the probe's admissibility rules to a guard the registry already holds, through that handle, releasing it at once when inadmissible (`unknown`, `inadmissible`) and answering `unknown`, `not_held` without one.

- Review fold (senpi#4481): a prior-boot proof or repair anchor resolves a census entry only after its observation could not decide it (a live entry blocks, an absent one is gone by observation; an entry the deadline left unobserved stays unknown); an unchanged escalation marker answers only its own subject without a query; `escalate` stamps the revision the attempt read and refuses a record that moved (`revision_changed`), so no stale marker is written.

### Why

- Design 4.2a "Bounded unknown escalation", Revision 6 "Bounded recovery", "Fresh evidence after escalation" and "Visible Repair connection action", Revision 7 "Repair amendments" ("Busy owner first", "Restart only where it proves something", "Foreign or host-gone rows"), Revision 8 r7 note 4; tests 12, 20 and 26 (engine half). An unknown identity got a new query at every ensure, the CLI result named no action, nothing recorded a restart's boot provenance, and a busy owner could only be retried.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Escalation, the anchor and the busy-owner wait run inside the endpoint-locked recovery before extensions load, and the wait needs the native blocking lock.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/endpoint-reservation-store.ts`: schema SQL, the rejection union, the row mapping and the three new methods.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/endpoint-recovery.ts`: `recoveryProber`, `observeRecorded`, `census`, `recoverGeneration`'s `refused`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-decision.ts`: `ReservationRecoveryRefusal`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-runner.ts` and `packages/coding-agent/src/cli/host-command-args.ts`: the request union, the runner switch and the subcommand list.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/socket-secret-native.ts` and `crates/senpi-socket-secret/src/presence_binding.rs`: the presence exports and the loader's required list.

### Changed

- A Windows public-endpoint start that cannot prove a recorded owner ended observes its identity at most twice inside five seconds (or its shorter startup deadline), records the escalated reason on the reservation, and refuses with the typed Repair action (`wait_for_owner`, `drain_owner`, `restart_computer`, `resolve_on_recorded_host`, `restore_store_access`, `retry`), holder and escalation; an unchanged record is not queried again by a later automatic ensure, while its presence objects still are (sisyphuslabs/omo-desktop-app#4199, design 4.2a "Bounded unknown escalation").
- New `senpi host repair [--confirm-restart]`: the Repair connection action of such an endpoint. It reruns the bounded check, waits for a busy owner's lock release through the native blocking wait (never a deadline; SIGINT cancels) and recovers with that handle as the retained guard, and with `--confirm-restart` records the repair anchor that lets the same endpoint recover after a confirmed restart on a local store (sisyphuslabs/omo-desktop-app#4199, tests 20 and 26).
