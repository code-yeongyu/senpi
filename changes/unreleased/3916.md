## 2026-11-07 - Wait once for an exhausted subscription pool to reset (senpi#3916)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/failover.ts`: wait for the earliest account-level rate-limit reset, at most once per turn and for at most five hours, then retry using the expired-block-cleared pool. Newly exhausted accounts must carry a confirmed subscription-usage verdict; ordinary request throttling retains upstream retry handling. Preserve upstream login handling, probe persistence controls, pool diagnostics, and model-specific fallback. Cancellation and ineligible resets retain the typed failure instead of replacing it with generic exhaustion text.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/affinity.ts`: export the existing earliest-unblock helper.

### Why

- An exhausted subscription pool can recover without another user prompt when a known reset is within the bounded wait. The upstream merge also needs to preserve newer auth and diagnostic contracts.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- This builtin extension owns account selection, failover, and reset timing.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/failover.ts`: account selection, pool exhaustion, and the attempt loop.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/affinity.ts`: the earliest-unblock helper export.
