## 2026-11-22 - Remove a delivered image-only input by its queue identity (#4064)

### What changed
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/agent-session.ts`: delivery removes the identified queue record and its matching occurrence in the visible text list.

### Why
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/agent-session.ts`: image-only inputs share empty text. Text-first removal could leave the delivered image’s queue ID listed instead of the one still pending.

### Why an extension could not handle it
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/agent-session.ts` owns both the delivery event and the internal queue records updated before subscribers see that event.

### Expected merge conflict zones
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/agent-session.ts`: `message_start` handling, `_removeQueuedInput`, and `_changeQueuedInput`.
