## 2026-11-22 - Streaming commands deliver every line under Bun (senpi#2947)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/cli/print-then-exit.ts`: `delivered()` writes in 32 KiB chunks, each awaited within the existing overall delivery bound - one large awaited write never completes under Bun 1.4.4 when it exceeds the pipe buffer, and queued un-awaited writes are dropped at `process.exit`. New `captureStdoutWhenDroppable(run)` collects a run's stdout only under Bun with a non-TTY stdout (a pipe reader parses complete output; a terminal keeps live progress).
- `packages/coding-agent/src/main.ts`: the package and `schedule` command paths route through `captureStdoutWhenDroppable` and deliver the collected text to `exitAfterOutput`; streaming behavior on Node and on terminals is unchanged.

### Why

- Under Bun 1.4.4 (`CODING_AGENT_BUN_VERSION`), output a streaming command queues ahead of a slow pipe reader is dropped at `process.exit` with exit code 0: a 4,000-line child delivered 3,500 lines to a reader that consumed after exit. Node delivers in full.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Exit-time stdout delivery is the CLI bootstrap's own flush; no extension hook runs there.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `delivered()`/`captureStdout` in `print-then-exit.ts`, and the package/schedule exit branches in `main.ts`.
