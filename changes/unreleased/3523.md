## 2026-10-17 - Keep native web-search replay out of tool-free side queries

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/btw/side-query.ts` removes native `web_search_call` blocks from the copied side-query history before budgeting, omitting assistant messages only when that removal leaves no content.
- The existing `test/suite/btw-side-query.test.ts` owner captures actual Responses and Codex payloads for native-only and mixed-text history.

### Why

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/btw/side-query.ts` deliberately declares no tools. Replaying completed native searches without their declaration makes a side question fail after a successful main-turn search. Visible context and the stored main conversation must remain intact.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/btw/side-query.ts` owns the builtin's private snapshot construction, which bypasses main-turn provider hooks. Adding a native tool declaration would also enable new searches in a read-only side question.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/btw/side-query.ts`, `buildSideQueryContext` history preparation. Main-turn native serialization and command credential lookup are unchanged.
