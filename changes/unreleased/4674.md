## 2026-10-28 - An account block keeps the failure that caused it and is logged (#4674)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/failover.ts`: when an account fails and `selectFn` finds no other account (`AllAccountsBlockedError`), `runFailover` throws the original `ClassifiedSdkError` and attaches the pool refusal as `poolState`, instead of letting the refusal replace it. Any other `selectFn` error still propagates. Every block that is saved (shared or session-scoped) writes one `account_blocked` session.log line.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/account-block-log.ts`: new `logAccountBlocked`, which writes `account_blocked {account, reason, until, shared, detail}` (detail capped at 200 characters) through the extension's session.log boundary.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/affinity.ts`: `AllAccountsBlockedError` carries `accounts` (name, reason, until for each candidate) and appends them to its message after the unchanged first sentence. An auth-blocked account is named with `/login anthropic-subscription`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/guidance.ts`, `stream-guidance.ts`: `allAccountsBlockedGuidance` takes the account states and lists one line per account, so the user sees which account needs `/login`.

### Why

- One account failing (a refresh failure, a 401) while the others were already blocked surfaced only as "All Claude accounts ... are currently blocked", and nothing in any log recorded which account was blocked or why (code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent#11683).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- This is the anthropic-subscription extension's own failover and pool selection.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the catch block of `runFailover` around `selectFn`, the `AllAccountsBlockedError` constructor and its `selectAccount` call site, and `allAccountsBlockedGuidance`.

## 2026-10-28 - The usage poll logs the auth block it saves (#4674)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/subscription-limits/usage-accounts.ts`: `markAuthRejected` writes one `account_blocked` session.log line (`detail: usage_poll_token_rejected`) when it actually locks the slot.

### Why

- The background usage poll could add an `auth_error` block with no record, so a later local pool refusal had no visible cause (code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent#11683).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The usage poll is core code; it reuses the anthropic-subscription extension's logger.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the tail of `markAuthRejected`.
