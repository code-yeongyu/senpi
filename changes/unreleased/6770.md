## 2026-11-15 - A held Stop on a hand waits for a turn start, not any call (senpi#5898)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/loop-hook-call.ts`: `LoopHookCallBinding` gains `startsTurn`. After a call with `endsTurn` (an `agent_end`) ended, its work's `isIdle` answers `true` until a call with `startsTurn` starts for that session; other calls (an `input` the loop handles without a turn, a preview, `session_start`) leave it idle. The `latest` map (one entry per loop session ever served) is gone. The `LATE_PER_SESSION` doc names the delay of late effects while the loop is idle (no hand-to-loop push yet, senpi#5778).
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/loop-hook-session-turns.ts` (new): `createSessionTurns` holds a loop session only from a turn's end until its next turn starts.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/loopless-loop-hooks.ts`: a real (non-preview) `before_agent_start` runs with `startsTurn`; the `agent_end` branch documents when a held Stop's writes reach the loop.

### Why

- #6751 review L2: any later call ended the idleness a held Stop waits on, so a call that started no turn silently dropped it, while a local session drops it only on `agent_start`. L3: the map kept a token per session for the hand's lifetime. L1: the delay of a held Stop's late effects was not named.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The loop-hook call and its per-session state belong to the loopless runtime.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `LoopHookCallBinding`, `run` in `loop-hook-call.ts`, and the `before_agent_start` and `agent_end` branches of `extensionLoopHooks` (#6753 adds events beside them).
