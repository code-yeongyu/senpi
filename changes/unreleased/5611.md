## 2026-11-03 - Stopping an inbox watch joins its arming writes (senpi#5609)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/interactive/session-control-wake.ts`: `InboxWatch.stop()` returns a promise (idempotent, never rejects) that settles once the arming loop's last sentinel write and removal and the watch teardown have settled.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/interactive/session-control-lifecycle.ts`: `ActivationParts.stopInbox` returns that promise; the terminal endpoint's `dispose` awaits it after closing the server.

### Why

- `stop()` only flagged the arming loop, so a sentinel write already in flight and its removal still reached the inbox after the endpoint was disposed; a teardown removing the inbox's tree right after failed with ENOTEMPTY.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The arming loop is private to the watch; nothing outside it can wait for its writes.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `InboxWatch` interface and the returned `stop` in `watchInbox`; the `dispose` body in `activateControlEndpoint`.

## 2026-11-03 - Disposing a host session control joins its inbox watches (senpi#5609)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-session-control.ts`: `dispose()` still stops every edge source synchronously and now returns a promise joining every released inbox watch (a registration still arming included); a registration's `dispose` awaits its release.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/connection-handler.ts`: the connection's `dispose` awaits the session control's release before disposing the runtime; the rebind keeps it fire-and-forget.

### Why

- The host returned from `dispose()` while the inbox watch's arming loop still wrote its sentinel into the inbox, so an owner removing the inbox's tree raced it (ENOTEMPTY).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The registration and its inbox watch are owned by the host; an extension only sees the registration handle.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `register`/`dispose`/`releaseActive` in `HostSessionControl`; the two `sessionControl?.dispose()` call sites in `connection-handler.ts`.
