## 2026-11-15 - A hand claims each boundary walk step before its handlers run (senpi#5898)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/loop-hook-boundary-walks.ts`, `loop-hook-turn-events.ts`: a boundary walk step is claimed before its handlers run and stays in flight until its reply. A second copy of that step, or a new walk opened for the session meanwhile, is refused; a step that fails releases its claim.

### Why

- Review of #6799 (L1): the `from` check ran before the await and the advance after it, so two copies of one step both ran the handler.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The loop-hook link and its wire contract belong to the loopless runtime.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `loop-hook-boundary-walks.ts` and the stepped boundary in `loop-hook-turn-events.ts`.
