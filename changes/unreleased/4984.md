## 2026-10-30 - System theme: subtle neutral panel fills and readable secondary text (#4941)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/interactive/theme/system-theme.ts`: message and tool panels (`userMessageBg`, `customMessageBg`, `toolPendingBg`, `toolSuccessBg`, `toolErrorBg`) take the terminal background's own hue at low saturation and half the previous panel step. Selection and search highlights are unchanged. Text gets WCAG floors checked after 8-bit rounding: body and `dim` at least 4.5:1, secondary text (muted, tool output, thinking, quotes, link URLs, comments) at least 5.5:1 so it stays stronger than dim, and hue-carrying text (accent, status, markdown, syntax, diff) at least 4.5:1.

### Why

- Panels were drawn at full family saturation, so tool, edit and todo blocks read as saturated cards, and only body text had a contrast floor: hints, paths and tool output fell to 2.45-3.6:1 on light and dark terminals (#4941).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The default `system` theme is generated in core from the terminal's reported colours; extensions can only replace the whole theme, not fix the generated defaults.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- `generateSystemThemeColors` (panel solve loop and the text-floor passes after it) and `withTextContrast` in `system-theme.ts`.
