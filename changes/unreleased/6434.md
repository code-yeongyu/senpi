## 2026-11-11 - the socket-secret bridge loader fails typed on a non-object publish result (senpi#6434)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/socket-secret-native.ts`: `publishSocketSecretNoReplace` throws `SocketSecretPublishError` (`unexpected bridge result ...`, no Windows error, no leftover staging) when the binding returns a non-object, instead of a `TypeError` from reading `stagingLeftBehind`.

### Why

- The bridge result crosses a native boundary, and every failure of a public-secret publication must reach the caller as the typed error (review follow-up for senpi#4312, sisyphuslabs/omo-desktop-app#4199).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The socket-secret bridge loader runs in the RPC host lifecycle, outside any extension.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the start of `publishSocketSecretNoReplace`.
