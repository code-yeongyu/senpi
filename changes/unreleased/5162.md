## 2026-10-31 - GPT presets state eval batching as a rule (senpi#5162)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/prompt-preset/gpt-eval-routing.ts`: `buildGptEvalRoutingTuning()` no longer says "follow its Tool Guidelines"; it says the guidelines are binding for multi-call work, that the independent reads, searches, symbol lookups and probes a step already knows it needs go into one cell, and that a turn spent on a single read or search while the others are known is a defect. Every GPT preset renders it.
- `packages/coding-agent/test/suite/prompt-presets-native-baseline.json`: the hashes of the GPT presets that render the bridge are recaptured; every other preset's hash is unchanged.

### Why

- Batching lived only in the eval tool description; the preset sentence read as advice, and GPT sessions issued independent reads and searches one per turn.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The preset text is owned by this builtin extension.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the one return string in `gpt-eval-routing.ts`; the GPT keys of the native baseline.
