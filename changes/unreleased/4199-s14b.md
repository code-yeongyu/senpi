## 2026-10-27 - Presence cleanup: holder deletion, retirement deletion and the bounded sweep (desktop#4199)

### What changed

- `crates/senpi-socket-secret`: add `presenceObjectHold`, `presenceObjectRelease` and `presenceRootList`. A hold is an exclusive open of an existing object (`DELETE | GENERIC_READ`, share mode 0, no reparse following) that reports the object's volume serial, file ID and whole content and stays open under its hold ID; `busy` is a sharing violation, `missing` is not-found. A release with deletion sets `FileDispositionInfo` delete-on-close through the held handle, then closes it. Nothing deletes by path.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/socket-secret-native.ts`: the three cleanup exports are part of the placement binding the loader requires.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/presence-cleanup.ts` (new): `deletePresenceObject` (hold, recorded identity and tag checked through it, delete-on-close; anything else leaves the object typed), `presenceObjectMissing` (the issuer's post-commit check) and `sweepPresenceObjects` (at most `PRESENCE_SWEEP_BOUND` = 32 objects per run, in name order; an object is considered only when its rows are `released` or retired, or when no row names it and its header is older than `PRESENCE_SWEEP_AGE_MS` = 24 hours; a held object is skipped; the row is rechecked in a short store transaction while the object is held).
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/endpoint-reservation-store.ts`: `presenceObjectDisposition(path)` answers `referenced`, `released`, `retired` or `unreferenced` inside one `BEGIN IMMEDIATE` transaction. The schema is unchanged.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-launch-reservation.ts`: `HeldPresence.deleteAfterRelease`; `releaseReservingOwner` and a participant's `releaseAfterQuiescence` delete the actor's object after its `released` commit and the close of its guard. `offerSupervisorLaunch` and `offerHostRole` cancel the generation with `presence_object_missing` when the object they predeclared is missing once the offer committed.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/endpoint-recovery.ts`: a not-found probe rereads the row and continues when the entry committed `released` meanwhile; after the retirement commit and the release of the recovered guards, the retired generation's objects are deleted through exclusive opens.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-ensure-start.ts`: the sweep runs after recovery and before the owner creates its own object, in the root the start resolved, under the endpoint lock the ensure holds; its outcome never decides the start.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/presence-placement.ts`: the `presence_object_missing` rejection.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/supervisor-route.ts`: comment only. The host still commits no `released`: its win32 shutdown bounds the session dispose and the listener close by timers, so quiescence is not provable there; its object is deleted at retirement or by the sweep.

### Why

- Design 4.2a Revision 7 "Presence cleanup" and test 24: every deletion is made by a process that holds the object at that moment and only after the database no longer needs it; a clean exit is a `released` commit, then deletion; retirement deletes the generation's objects and keeps the tombstone; the sweep runs only under the endpoint lock, handles a bounded number of objects, deletes only what it holds, and uses the 24-hour age as a liveness filter only. Revision 7 folded finding: presence-file growth.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Cleanup runs inside the endpoint-locked start, in the recovery transaction and at the participants' quiescence, before extensions load, and needs the native exclusive open.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-launch-reservation.ts`: `HeldPresence`, `releaseReservingOwner`, the offer helpers and `admitClaimedParticipant`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/endpoint-recovery.ts`: the probe loop and the `finally` of `recoverGeneration`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-ensure-start.ts`: the start prologue around `acquireReservingOwner`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/socket-secret-native.ts` and `crates/senpi-socket-secret/src/lib.rs`: native exports and the loader's required list.

### Changed

- Windows public-endpoint presence objects no longer accumulate: an actor deletes its own object after its `released` commit, recovery deletes a retired generation's objects, and a bounded sweep under the endpoint lock removes released, retired and old unreferenced objects, each only through an exclusive open the deleter holds (sisyphuslabs/omo-desktop-app#4199, design 4.2a "Presence cleanup").
