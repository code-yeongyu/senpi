## 2026-11-06 - Cancel expired moved-path canonicalization (senpi#6127)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/moved-path-guard/resolve-async.ts` passes a cancellation signal to canonicalization and aborts it when the step expires or the probe stops.

### Why

- A 500 ms guard deadline previously left the underlying canonicalization running, allowing its later fallback to block the session loop.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/moved-path-guard/resolve-async.ts` owns the builtin guard's probe lifetime and runs before other tool handlers.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/moved-path-guard/resolve-async.ts`, around step resolution and probe shutdown.

## 2026-11-13 - Existing per-OS CI runs path-resolution regressions (senpi#6127)

### What changed

- `.github/workflows/ci.yml`: `terminal-cross-os` (Linux/Windows) and `macos-combined` explicitly run the retained filesystem, error classification, cancelled-write and moved-path guard regressions, followed by the existing Node/Bun integration script. The terminal job installs pinned Bun; both jobs use the existing test runner and temporary-directory guards.
- `.github/workflows/ci.yml`: the terminal job's five path-resolution steps and its existing PTY/terminal suites continue after test failures once setup succeeds, and stop on cancellation. Failures still fail the job.
- `.github/workflows/ci.yml`: the terminal job builds workspace entries before its Node integration run. The macOS path steps have IDs and two-minute caps, with their ten-minute budget included in the combined job.

### Why

- `.github/workflows/ci.yml`: deleting the feature-specific workflow left its Windows/macOS regressions without an existing job selecting them. Explicit verbose steps restore that coverage without adding jobs or runner matrices.
- `.github/workflows/ci.yml`: downstream suites also need an explicit status condition; guarding only the new steps would leave the existing terminal suites subject to GitHub's implicit `success()` check.
- `.github/workflows/ci.yml`: clean installs lack compiled workspace export targets, and macOS suite accounting includes only identified, bounded steps.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `.github/workflows/ci.yml` selects hosted test jobs before any runtime extension loads.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- `.github/workflows/ci.yml`: the `terminal-cross-os` setup/test steps and the `macos-combined` steps immediately after workspace installation.
