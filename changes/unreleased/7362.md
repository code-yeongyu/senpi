## 2026-11-22 - A 408 or a reset connection retries on the same model (senpi#3251)

### What changed

- `packages/ai/src/utils/retry.ts`: `RETRYABLE_PROVIDER_ERROR_PATTERN` gains a digit-bounded `408` and `ECONNRESET`.

### Why

- A gateway's empty-body 408 and a reset socket were classified as hard errors, so the first one skipped the same-model retry and went straight down the fallback chain, or ended the turn without one.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Retry classification lives in pi-ai.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the transport-failure group of `RETRYABLE_PROVIDER_ERROR_PATTERN`.
