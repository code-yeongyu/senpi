## 2026-11-28 - Held peer deliveries: every resume settles what it carries, and the held cap bounds every admission (senpi#7628)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/delivery-ids.ts` (new): `deliveryIdOf` and `writtenDeliveryIds`, moved unchanged from `external-admission.ts`, plus `deliveryIdsIn` / `deliveryIdsOf`, every delivery a message's entry settles: a delivery's own id and the `folded_delivery_ids` it carries, or the held deliveries a `session-resumed` message carries.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/external-admission.ts`: `admit()` answers `held_deliveries_full` (`HeldDeliveriesFull`, outside the public `ExternalAdmissionKind`) and writes nothing once the session holds `MAX_HELD_PEER_DELIVERIES`, counting the held notices written (the port's new `heldPeerDeliveries`) plus held starts whose notice is still on its way. A held start has its own `held` lane, neither busy nor joinable, so a delivery arriving before the previous held notice is written is held too, not steered. `observePersisted` / `observeRefused` settle every id the entry carries.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/session-control-actions.ts`: `pi.session.admitExternalMessage` throws `HeldDeliveriesFullError` (`code: "held_deliveries_full"`) for that answer, as it throws once admission is closed.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/held-peer-batch.ts`: a folded run records the others in `details.folded_delivery_ids` and the run's highest `chain_count`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/run-resume-turn.ts`: the resume turn after a Stop or an interrupted run names the held deliveries it carries in its `details.folded_delivery_ids`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/stop-resume-directive.ts`: the directive's `details` are typed (`StopResumeDetails`).
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/agent-session.ts`: the admission port's `heldPeerDeliveries`; `deliveryIdOf` imported from its new module.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/lifecycle-session-binding.ts`: `deliveryIdOf` imported from its new module.

### Why

- Deliveries folded into one turn after the first, and every delivery the Resume after a Stop carried, stayed `pending` for the life of the process and `onEmitted` never fired for them. The fold kept the first delivery's `chain_count`. The cap was checked only by the RPC command, and a delivery that arrived while the previous held start had not settled was steered into memory instead of held, so a burst passed the cap.
- The extension call throws instead of answering a new kind: a caller that knows only the existing kinds would read an unknown one as admitted and drop the delivery.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The admission ledger, the hold and the resume turns are engine state; extensions only call `pi.session.admitExternalMessage`, the entry point the cap now covers.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- MEDIUM: `admit()` and the settle methods of `ExternalAdmission`.
- LOW: `fold()` in `held-peer-batch.ts`; the send in `startRunResumeTurn`; `stopResumeDirective`; `admitExternalMessage` in `createSessionControlActions`; the admission port in `agent-session.ts`.

## 2026-11-28 - admitExternalMessage refuses a full held set; folded entries name their deliveries (senpi#7628)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/session-control-types.ts`: `SessionControlDeliveryDetails` gains `folded_delivery_ids`; `admitExternalMessage` documents that it throws an error whose `code` is `held_deliveries_full` while the session holds `MAX_HELD_PEER_DELIVERIES` peer deliveries. `ExternalAdmissionKind` is unchanged.

### Why

- `pi.session.admitExternalMessage` was not bounded by the held cap, and a folded entry could not say which deliveries it carries.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- These are the types of the engine's own admission call and transcript entry.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `admitExternalMessage` declaration in `SessionControlActions`; the `SessionControlDeliveryDetails` interface.

## 2026-11-28 - admit_external_message takes the held cap from the admission (senpi#7628)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/admit-external-message-command.ts`: the command's own pre-check is gone; it answers the admission's `held_deliveries_full` as the same `held_deliveries_full` error as before.

### Why

- One cap for every entry point, decided where the delivery is admitted.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The RPC command is the engine's own entry point.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the cap block of `admitExternalMessage`.

## 2026-11-28 - The terminal control endpoint refuses a full held set (senpi#7628)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/interactive/session-control-input-commands.ts`: an admission answered `held_deliveries_full` is refused with that code, like `held_draft` and `turn_conflict`.

### Why

- The endpoint shares the admission's cap.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The endpoint answers the engine's admission directly.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `switch` on the admission kind.
