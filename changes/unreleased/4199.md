## 2026-10-26 - Reserve and admit the supervisor-to-host hop (desktop#4199)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-launch-reservation.ts` generalizes claim admission and verified grants to either role.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-launch-reservation.ts` treats typed busy reads as not yet claimed or granted within the existing polling deadline; other errors still propagate.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/endpoint-reservation-store.ts` routes granted-role reads through the shared contention mapper.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-ensure-start.ts` selects the supervisor role explicitly when granting.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-lifecycle.ts` offers the host role before spawning and grants the OS-verified claimant before readiness, using the established public-secret commitment. Failure uses the existing child shutdown path.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-lifecycle.ts` gives the host claim the same 30-second startup budget as its listener.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-lifecycle-launch.ts` forwards the host locator through default and explicit child-command argv.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/supervisor-route.ts` admits a reserved host before CLI parsing, then strips the private locator flags. Reservation modules remain lazy on both entry paths.

### Why

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-lifecycle.ts`: a host missed its 10-second claim deadline at load average about 210.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-launch-reservation.ts` and `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/endpoint-reservation-store.ts`: at load about 240, a writer's lock aborted claim/grant polling instead of allowing startup to complete.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-launch-reservation.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-ensure-start.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-lifecycle.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-lifecycle-launch.ts`, and `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/supervisor-route.ts`: design 4.2a step 3 and test 6 of sisyphuslabs/omo-desktop-app#4199 require the actual host participant, including a wrapper descendant, to be verified and granted before it listens.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/endpoint-reservation-store.ts`: reservation reads precede extension loading.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-launch-reservation.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-ensure-start.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-lifecycle.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-lifecycle-launch.ts`, and `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/supervisor-route.ts`: admission and child launch precede extension loading and own process identity, argv and listener startup.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/endpoint-reservation-store.ts`: granted-role reads.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-launch-reservation.ts`: role helper signatures.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-ensure-start.ts`: supervisor grant call.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-lifecycle.ts`: reserved startup and child readiness.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-lifecycle-launch.ts`: child argv construction.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/supervisor-route.ts`: lazy launch entry dispatch.

## 2026-10-26 - Admit reserved hosts before parsing CLI arguments (desktop#4199)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/main.ts` awaits reserved host admission before public argument parsing or listener startup.

### Why

- `packages/coding-agent/src/main.ts`: a host with a reservation must not listen before its own verified grant, and private locator flags must not reach downstream consumers.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `packages/coding-agent/src/main.ts`: the entry point controls startup before extensions load.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- `packages/coding-agent/src/main.ts`: internal supervisor dispatch and public CLI parsing.
