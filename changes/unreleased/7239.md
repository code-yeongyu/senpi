## 2026-11-22 - Serialize streamed reasoning details once per thinking block (senpi#3934)

### What changed

- `packages/ai/src/api/openai-completions.ts` accumulates reasoning details per thinking block and writes their merged JSON to the existing signature slot when the block finishes, including on an interrupted stream.

### Why

- `packages/ai/src/api/openai-completions.ts` previously parsed and serialized the growing signature after every detail. The final signature needs only one serialization.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `packages/ai/src/api/openai-completions.ts` owns the provider detail loop and the normal and error finalizers; extensions see the resulting content, not the accumulation step.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- `packages/ai/src/api/openai-completions.ts`: the streaming detail accumulator, detail loop, and thinking-block finalization.

<!-- changes-log: packages/ai/CHANGELOG.md -->
### Fixed

- OpenAI-compatible streams no longer repeatedly serialize the growing `reasoning_details` signature. Completed and interrupted thinking blocks retain the same replay data.
