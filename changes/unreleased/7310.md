## 2026-11-22 - Handle-inherit clearing caches a failed loader and rejects -2 (senpi#7310)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/utils/private-fd-inherit.ts`: a failed native loader is cached as fail-closed, and `_get_osfhandle` results of -2 are rejected with the other invalid handles.

### Why

- A partial `dlopen` failure repeated both load attempts on every descriptor, and -2 reached `SetHandleInformation` (senpi#4873).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Descriptor inheritance is cleared at the process receipt boundary before extensions load.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `clearPrivateFdInherit` in `private-fd-inherit.ts`.
