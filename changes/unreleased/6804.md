## 2026-11-15 - The active set and prompt options answer only the call's own work after it ended (senpi#6754)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/loop-hook-call.ts`: after the call ended, `getActiveTools` and `getSystemPromptOptions` answer only the call's own work (as `hasPendingMessages` and main's carried-session reads do, for a Stop it held) and throw `LoopHookCallEndedError` from any other context.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/loop-hook-effects.ts`: no held-queue code. The earlier fix (put held late writes back when the call that took them throws) is superseded by main's late route (H3): a late write goes out as a durable wake at once, so no call takes it over and a throwing call cannot lose it.

### Why

- Review L1 on #6800: a late `pi.getActiveTools()` silently answered `[]` and `ctx.getSystemPromptOptions()` answered from any context. Main's held Stop reads what its call carried from the call's own work, so those reads stay open there and refuse elsewhere; the table's docblock now states that rule.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The call's lifecycle belongs to the hand engine.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `getActiveTools` and `getSystemPromptOptions` members in `createLoopHookCall`.

## 2026-11-15 - A late pi.getActiveTools reaches its call (senpi#6754)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/runner.ts`: a runner without a session routes `pi.getActiveTools` through the call's writer, as `pi.setActiveTools` already is, so a late read from a call's work reaches the call's set; outside any call it answers the empty set as before. The `loopHookWrite` and `bindSessionlessFacts` docs name what they route (review L2 on #6800).

### Why

- Routed through `current()`, a late `pi.getActiveTools` silently answered `[]`.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `pi.getActiveTools` is bound by the runner.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `bindSessionlessFacts` in `runner.ts`.
