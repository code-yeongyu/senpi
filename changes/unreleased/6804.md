## 2026-11-15 - Held late writes survive a call that throws (senpi#6754)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/loop-hook-call.ts`: a call copies the session's held late writes into its own effects and leaves the held set untouched; when its dispatch throws, the held set goes back on the queue ahead of newer late work, for the next reply.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/loop-hook-effects.ts`: `appendHeld`; `effectsReply`'s doc states that only a throwing call's own writes are discarded.

### Why

- Late writes earlier calls left were discarded with the next call's error.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The held queue belongs to the hand engine.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the start and the `try` of `run` in `loop-hook-call.ts`.
