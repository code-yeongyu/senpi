## 2026-11-11 - Native socket-secret crate review follow-up (senpi#6441)

### What changed

- `crates/senpi-socket-secret`: once `probe_retained` admits a wait-retained handle, it clears `wait_retained`. The admitted recovery guard then leaves only with a receipt, and a later `probe_retained` answers `NotHeld`. `QuiescenceReceipt` has a private field and a crate-only constructor. `CancelToken::has_pending_wait` is test-only (`#[cfg(test)] pub(crate)`), and its doc now says it reports a registered canceller.
- `crates/senpi-socket-secret`: `PublishResult.outcome` is a `#[napi(string_enum)]`. An owner SID that is not UTF-16 is its own failure (`owner_sid_utf16`, no Windows error) instead of `dacl` with code 0, and `PublishFailure.code` is optional. The `Gone` contract names the kernel-session comparison-domain gate. Test-only fixes: the DACL receipt frees its descriptors through an RAII guard, the wrapper receipt asserts the exact `Gone(CreationTimeDiffers)`, and the binding receipts remove their temp directories.
- `socket-secret-publish.ts`: `NativePublishResult.outcome` names the bridge's string enum, and `failure.code` may be absent for the `owner_sid_utf16` step only; that failure throws `SocketSecretPublishError` with `windowsError: null`. Any other step without a numeric code still fails closed as an unexpected bridge result (senpi#6434).
- `socket-secret-native.ts`: the `presenceProbeRetained` doc says an already admitted guard answers `not_held`.

### Why

- These are review findings left open on senpi#4310, #4326, #4370 and #4470. Without the first fix, an admitted guard could be dropped without a receipt, and outside code could forge a quiescence receipt.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The presence registry, publication primitive and their N-API surface live in the native bridge and its headless loader, outside any extension.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `PublishResult`/`PublishFailure` in the crate's `lib.rs`, `probe_retained` in `presence.rs`, and `NativePublishResult` and the failure parse in `socket-secret-publish.ts`.
