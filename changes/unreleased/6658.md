## 2026-11-13 - A fallback rung's level is the configuration_update in force (senpi#6655)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/reasoning-baseline.ts`: new `hasStaleConfigurationUpdate(messages, thinkingLevel)`, true when the newest `configurationUpdate` message names another level than the live one.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/agent-session.ts`: `_switchActiveModel`, after the switch is admitted, appends a `configuration_update` for the live level and rebuilds the context when the target model supports configuration updates and the newest update is stale.

### Why

- On a flagged model the newest update is the effort the request carries (replayed item, or the Sign in with ChatGPT `reasoning.effort` fold). A retry-fallback rung brings its level as `ephemeralThinkingLevel` without an update entry, so the older update stayed in force and the request ran at the wrong effort.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The model switch and the session's transcript entries are owned by `AgentSession`; no extension hook runs between the switch's level change and the next request's context build.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the block right after `_assertModelUsableForSwitch` in `_switchActiveModel` and the import of `reasoning-baseline.ts` in `agent-session.ts`.
