## 2026-11-27 - Answer a queued prompt before publishing it (senpi#7579)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/client-input-handler.ts`: answer a newly queued prompt synchronously after recording the prepared input, before queue publication.

### Why

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/client-input-handler.ts`: waiting for preflight to finish let a tool boundary deliver the steer before its response.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/client-input-handler.ts` owns the response frame; extensions cannot order writes to the submitting connection.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/client-input-handler.ts`: the prompt-path admission callbacks shared by classic, shared-session and worker RPC.
