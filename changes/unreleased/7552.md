## 2026-11-27 - /share redacts secrets passed as command-line flags and stated in prose (senpi#3577)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/share/share-redaction-flags.ts` (new): a long flag whose name is sensitive by the existing key-name rule (`--password x`, `--token x`, `--api-key x`, `--secret="x y"`) loses its value, also across a line continuation (`--password \` then the value on the next line) and after `=`; a flag that names where the secret comes from (`--password-stdin`, `--token-file`) keeps its argument, and so does a bare `--password` after `psql`/`mysql`/`mariadb`, which only asks for a prompt (`psql --password mydb` keeps `mydb`). Known clients lose their password flag's value: `-p<pw>` glued (also across a line continuation) or quoted after `mysql`/`mysqldump`/`mysqladmin`/`mariadb`; `sshpass -p pw` (only sshpass's own options, not the command it runs); `redis-cli -a pw`; `docker login -p pw`; the password half of `curl -u`/`--user` (`-u user:pw`, `--user=user:pw`, `-uuser:pw`, clustered `-su user:pw`, quoted whole). A rule never crosses `;`, `&&`, `||` or `|`. `PGPASSWORD`, `MYSQL_PWD`, `REDISCLI_AUTH` and `SSHPASS` assignments lose their value. Prose that states a password, passphrase, secret or token (`my password is hunter2x`, `the token is 'a b'`) (also after `is:`) loses the value only when it looks like a secret (a digit or a symbol in it, or quoted): a plain word is kept (`my password is swordfish` is uploaded as is, since a letters-only password cannot be told from a sentence). In an argument list, the item after a sensitive long flag, and a known client's password value, are redacted (`["--api-key", "x"]`, `["mysql", "-ppw"]`, `["sshpass", "-p", "pw", ...]`). An existing `<redacted>` is never redacted or counted again.
- `share-redaction.ts` runs the flag and prose rules before the generated-value pass; `share-redaction-value.ts` applies the argument-list rule to arrays.

### Why

- Tool-call arguments are uploaded by `/share` by default, and these forms have no `key: value` shape, so a short secret in them reached the shared session unredacted (#3577). Ordinary flags stay: `--verbose`, `--path x`, `mkdir -p dir`, `--max-tokens 4096`, and prose that names no value.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The redaction runs inside senpi's own `/share` export, before anything leaves the machine; an extension cannot reach the bytes that export builds.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `scripts/realm-census.json` (three new entries beside the other share-redaction regexes).
