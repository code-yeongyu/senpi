## 2026-10-26 - Publication is the commit point of a generation handoff (code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent#10091, senpi#4284)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-publish-token.ts` (new): the handoff's one-shot publication token, written into the successor's generation directory before the spawn and revoked by one rename when the readiness window closes.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/socket-ownership.ts`: `adoptPublicSocket` consumes the token (one rename of the same file) as the last step before the rename; `consumePublishToken`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-lifecycle-launch.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-lifecycle.ts`: the supervisor takes `--publish-token <path>` and passes it to the adoption.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-successor.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-successor-readiness.ts` (new, split out), `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-successor-abandon.ts` (new, moved out unchanged), `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-handoff.ts`: a readiness timeout revokes the token before stopping the successor and follows one that consumed it first; every step after publication repairs forward and is reported as `HandoffResult.repair`.

### Why

- A successor could take the public socket between a handoff's last readiness probe and its SIGKILL, and a bookkeeping failure after publication killed the generation every client reached (code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent#10091 design, revision 2: publication is the boundary between abandonment and forward repair).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The handoff and the supervisor's adoption of the public socket are host internals.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- MEDIUM: `host-successor.ts` `startSuccessor` (restructured around the publication boundary); LOW: `host-lifecycle-launch.ts` `parseSupervisorArgs`, `socket-ownership.ts` `adoptPublicSocket`.

### Fixed
- A generation handoff never stops a successor that took the public socket: a closing readiness window withdraws the successor's one-shot publication token first, and a registration or enrollment failure after publication is repaired forward instead of killing the new generation (code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent#10091, [#4284](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/senpi/pull/4284))
