## 2026-10-26 - A runtime-identity handover never closes admission and answers within a bound (code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent#10091, senpi#4298)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-handover-coordinator.ts` (new) replaces `HostIdleHandover`: no command is gated; `begin_handover` answers within 45 s with `migration_deferred`, `draining` (successor serves new sessions), `rolled_back` or `recovery_required`; a 10-minute soft drain deadline reports retained sessions (`completed_with_retained_sessions`, `host_handover_overdue`) and never aborts them.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-handover-operation.ts` (new): phases, bounds and the legacy `state` projection. `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-handover-eligibility.ts` (new): overlap needs strict transfer, `SENPI_RPC_HOST_OVERLAP_HANDOVER=1` and both client capabilities (`generation_separated_admission_v1`, `session_transfer_reconciliation_v1`) on every application connection; a host holding nothing proceeds.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-handover-journal.ts` (new): durable `handovers/<operationId>.json` per phase, terms by digest. `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-handover-hooks.ts` (new, out of `multi-session-host.ts`).
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-router-handover.ts` (new), `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-router-admission.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-router-routing.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-router-context.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-router-attachments.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-command-router.ts`: application-connection classification and the membership seal around a publication.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-core-gate.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-idle-handover.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-handover-wire.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/multi-session-host.ts`: the gate and the global `handover_pending` refusal are removed; `get_protocol_info.handover` gains `phase`, `published_at`, `drain_deadline_at` and `retained`; operation ids are validated.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-handoff.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-successor.ts`: a `deadline` bounds the lock wait and the readiness window.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-handover-request.ts`: the CLI defers (`migration_deferred`, `legacy_predecessor_transfer`) without sending `begin_handover` to a host lacking `strict_session_transfer_v1`, and its probes are observing reads; `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/custom-capability.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-capabilities.ts`: hosts advertise it.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-observe-request.ts`: `isObservingRecord`, the parsed-record form of the observing rule the router classifies connections by.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/rpc-host-lifecycle-types.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-event-writer.ts`: the `host_handover_overdue` host record and its wire projection. `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-status.ts`: the `handover` field's description.

### Why

- The conditional idle handover refused every new prompt on the whole host until all sessions were idle, with no deadline (code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent#10091, approved design revision 2).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Host admission and the handover protocol are host internals.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- HIGH: `host-idle-handover.ts`, `host-core-gate.ts`, `host-handover-wire.ts` (rewritten); MEDIUM: `multi-session-host.ts` `runSocketHost` hooks, `host-handover-request.ts`; LOW: router context/routing/attachments, `host-handoff.ts`, `host-successor.ts`. Open senpi#4252 edits the same handover files.

### Changed
- A runtime-identity handover (`senpi host handoff --when idle`) never refuses new work on the running host: it answers within 45 s with the successor serving new sessions while existing ones finish where they run, or with `migration_deferred` when the update may not overlap yet (it needs `SENPI_RPC_HOST_OVERLAP_HANDOVER=1` and clients that declare `generation_separated_admission_v1` and `session_transfer_reconciliation_v1`); a host from before this change is never asked and updates at its next cold start (code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent#10091, [#4298](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/senpi/pull/4298))
