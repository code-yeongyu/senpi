## 2026-11-12 - An answer handed to an admission baseline is the request's one answer (senpi#6561)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-admission-baseline.ts`: `BaselineAnswer` carries the answer a request writes through its admission baseline. An answer handed to a baseline the connection took is told to the record of answered requests; one handed to a baseline the connection did not take, or to none, is not.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/socket-event-fanout.ts`: `installBaseline` answers whether it took the baseline into the queue. What it takes and what it drops is unchanged.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-event-writer-recovery.ts`: `installAdmissionBaseline` passes that answer on, and `baselineAnswer` makes the object for a connection.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-open-admission.ts`, `compact-subscribe-admission.ts`, `compact-reset-stream.ts`: each hands its answer over through that object. A compact open whose answer was handed over queues no refusal after it, and clears its route's retirement before calling it, so a retirement that throws is attempted once.

### Why

- An answer written through an admission baseline is written later by the connection, outside `enqueueControl`, so the record that keeps a throwing handler from being answered twice never heard of it. A compact open cancelled while it ran, whose route retirement then failed, got two responses under one id.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- It is the RPC host's own answer path.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- MEDIUM: the failure path of `compactOpen` in `compact-open-admission.ts`.
- LOW: `installBaseline` in `socket-event-fanout.ts`, a file at 249 of 250 pure lines before and after.
