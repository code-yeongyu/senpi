### Fixed
- A claim winner that died before raising its epoch floor no longer strands the scope (`src/execution/remote-epoch.ts`, senpi#5860): `ownerOf(scope)` may return the owner's recorded epoch, and the hand acks a raise by the owner of record up to exactly that epoch at any step from the floor. A raise above the recorded epoch or by anyone else is still refused, a floor never lowers, and every earlier owner stays fenced. When a raise's epoch is above the hand's copy of the record, the hand re-reads the record once (coalesced per scope) before deciding; a raise at or below the copy never triggers a read. With no floor yet, an earlier instance of the record's owner opens the floor at the recorded epoch, never its own lower one. The record is read before the raise joins the ledger's admission line, so a slow read never holds a ledger step, and the latest read is kept. A raise by the record's principal below its recorded epoch is acked with the floor in force, and the session's floor gate (`src/execution/remote-epoch-gate.ts`) reads that answer as `epoch_superseded`.

## 2026-11-05 - Epoch floor catch-up to the owner of record (senpi#5860)

### What changed

- `packages/agent/src/execution/remote-epoch.ts`: the fence accepts the owner of record's raise to its recorded epoch, refreshes a stale record copy once (coalesced, outside the admission line, latest read kept) for a raise ahead of it, opens a first floor at the recorded epoch, and answers a raise below the record with the floor in force.
- `packages/agent/src/execution/remote-epoch-gate.ts`: an ack whose floor is below the requested epoch ends the lane as `epoch_superseded`.

### Why

Ownership moves by claim one epoch per won claim, but the floor rose only when an owner raised it. A winner that died before its raise left the next owner two steps above the floor, refused for good (senpi#5860).

### Why an extension could not handle it

The decision runs inside the hand's fence, on the same admission path as every invoke.

### Expected merge conflict zones

`remote-epoch.ts` raise handling and options; `remote-epoch-gate.ts` ack handling.
