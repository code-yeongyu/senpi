## 2026-11-08 - A still-running background session reports its age; auto-reap is opt-in (senpi#6201)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/terminal/background-age.ts` (new): `BackgroundAgeSchedule` keeps one unref'd timer per session bundle, armed for the earliest due notice across every background session, and reports each session at 10 minutes and then every 30 minutes (10, 40, 70, ...). A late timer or a session registered after it started reports once for the milestones it missed, never a burst. `backgroundAgeEvent` builds the `TerminalBackgroundAgeEvent` payload.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/terminal/session-bundle.ts`: `notifyBackgroundStart` takes the command and the auto-reap age and adds the session to the schedule; exit, teardown and the schedule's `remove`/`dispose` clear it. A new sink `onBackgroundAge` receives `running` notices and, on an exit whose view says it was reaped, one `reaped` notice. While parked only the newest notice per session is kept, and it is dropped when the session exits.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/terminal/extension-state.ts`: the `onBackgroundAge` sink emits `terminal_background_age` on `pi.events` and `pi.rpc`, shows a TUI notice, and hands a `running` notice to the notifier; `onBackgroundStart` forwards the command and the auto-reap age; the tool context exposes `backgroundAutoReapMinutes`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/terminal/notify.ts`: `describeBackgroundAge` writes the notice (session id, command or label, age, the `kill_bash` call); `TerminalNotifier.notifyAge` delivers it like a completion notice; a completion notice of a reaped session says it was stopped automatically after N minutes.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/terminal/tools/background-auto-reap.ts` (new): `trackBackgroundSession` announces a background session and, only when `auto_reap_minutes` or the setting asks for it, arms one unref'd timer that stops the session through `manager.stop` (TERM, then KILL to the group, as `kill_bash` does) at that age and marks its exit view `autoReapedAfterMinutes`; the timer is cleared on exit. `resolveAutoReapMinutes` gives the call's value precedence and treats 0 as off.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/terminal/tools/bash.ts`: new `auto_reap_minutes` parameter; both background starts (`run_in_background` and the auto-detach) go through `trackBackgroundSession`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/terminal/tools/context.ts`: `backgroundAutoReapMinutes`, the `autoReapMinutes` argument of `onBackgroundStart`, and `TerminalExitView.autoReapedAfterMinutes`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/terminal/tools/child-placement.ts`: the job args carry `backgroundAutoReapMinutes`; the child's start wake carries the command and the reap age and its exit wake the reap, so the same notices work under `--tool-runtime child`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/terminal/settings.ts`: `terminal.backgroundAutoReapMinutes` resolves to a whole number of minutes >= 1, or undefined (off, the default).

### Why

- A background session that is still running keeps the agent in `waiting_background` for as long as it runs, and nothing told the agent, the user or an RPC driver (senpi#6201). Killing it on a timer would remove the capability of long sessions, so the default is a notice that names the session and the call that stops it; stopping at an age is opt-in.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The terminal builtin owns the background sessions, their bundle and their stop path; this is fork-only code.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `notifyBackgroundStart`/`notifyBackgroundExit`/`teardown` in `session-bundle.ts`, the sinks in `extension-state.ts`, the two background-start sites in `tools/bash.ts`.

## 2026-11-08 - terminal_background_age event (senpi#6201)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/monitor-state-event.ts`: `TERMINAL_BACKGROUND_AGE_EVENT` (`terminal_background_age`) and its payload `TerminalBackgroundAgeEvent` (`kind` running|reaped, `id`, `description`, `command?`, `startedAtMs`, `ageMs`, `ageMinutes`, `autoReapMinutes`, `stop`).

### Why

- RPC drivers need a typed event they can act on when a background session runs long (senpi#6201).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The event name and payload are the shared cross-extension seam, like the other terminal events in this file.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the constant block at the top of `monitor-state-event.ts`.

## 2026-11-08 - Terminal setting backgroundAutoReapMinutes (senpi#6201)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/terminal-settings.ts`: `TerminalSettings` gains `backgroundAutoReapMinutes?: number` (default unset: off), the age at which the terminal extension stops a background session.

### Why

- Opt-in auto-reap of long background sessions (senpi#6201); off by default so no long session is killed unless asked.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `TerminalSettings` is the typed shape of the `terminal` settings block that the terminal extension reads.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the end of the `TerminalSettings` interface.

## 2026-11-08 - Tool-child terminal jobs carry the background auto-reap (senpi#6201)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/tool-child/terminal-jobs.ts`: the job args carry `backgroundAutoReapMinutes` into the child's tool context; the `background_start` wake carries the command and the reap age, and the `background_exit` wake carries `autoReapedAfterMinutes`.

### Why

- The reap runs where the session's process group lives, which is the tool child under `--tool-runtime child`; the server needs the reap age for its age notices and the reap for the completion notice (senpi#6201).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The tool child's terminal jobs are the host's own runtime, not an extension surface.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `parseArgs`, `exitSummary` and `contextFor` in `terminal-jobs.ts`.

### Added
- A background terminal session that is still running is reported at 10 minutes and then every 30 minutes, naming its `bash_id`, its command and the `kill_bash` call that stops it. The report reaches the agent like a completion notice, shows in the TUI, and goes to RPC clients as the typed `terminal_background_age` event. Nothing is killed by default; the new `terminal.backgroundAutoReapMinutes` setting or the `bash` parameter `auto_reap_minutes` opts into stopping a background session at that age (TERM, then KILL to its process group, as `kill_bash` does), which its completion notice and a `reaped` event report ([#6212](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/senpi/pull/6212))
