## 2026-11-22 - Release finished child sessions' idle resident processes (senpi#5689)

### What changed

- A settled child identified by `spawnedBy` releases its resident process once its active turn is idle. Saving its binding preserves any pending model switch. Ordinary sessions and forks retain their normal idle TTL.
- `SENPI_CLAUDE_SDK_OAUTH_MAX_IDLE_SESSIONS` caps idle residents (default 8). The registry evicts least-recently-used idle entries without counting active turns.

### Why

- Finished task children previously kept their provider processes for the same 30-minute interval as interactive sessions.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The builtin subscription provider owns the resident registry, its reaper, and the binding needed to resume a child.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- The registry's admission and settlement handlers share lifecycle state with the idle reaper and model switching.
