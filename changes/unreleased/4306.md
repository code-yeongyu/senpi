## 2026-10-26 - A successor reopens its predecessor's parks for as long as a predecessor runs (code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent#10091, senpi#4306)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-handover-reopen.ts`: the successor's reopen of its predecessor's parks no longer ends after 120 s, a quiet streak or eight failed opens. It subscribes to the park records, the path claims and the generations before its first scan (a 30 s rescan covers a missed notification), consumes until no other generation runs and no inherited park remains, removes a record only while it still names the transfer it reopened, and keeps a record whose reopen failed, with the failure on it, trying it again when a claim or a generation changes.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-handover-parked.ts`: `failedTransfer { reason, at, attempts }` on a park record and `recordHandoverParkedFailure` (written only while the record holds that transfer); `handoverParkedRecordDir`; `dropHandoverParkedSessionIfFileGone` removed (the reopen drops a park whose file is gone by its transfer).
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-gc-evidence.ts`: `anyGenerationLive(paths, except)` answers whether a generation other than the caller runs.

### Why

- A handover that never closes admission transfers each session when it is quiet, possibly hours after the successor took the socket. The 120 s watch missed such a park, and eight failed opens deleted a retained session's record (code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent#10091, approved design revision 2).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The successor's reopen of parked sessions is host startup internals.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- MEDIUM: `host-handover-reopen.ts` (the consumer is rewritten); LOW: `host-handover-parked.ts` record shape and helpers, `host-gc-evidence.ts` `anyGenerationLive`.
