## 2026-11-15 - Late hand results reach the brain over a durable wake link (senpi#5778, PR #6767)

### What changed

- `packages/agent/src/execution/wake-link.ts` (new): the wake link on the hand transport's `hand.wake` lane. `createHandWakeLink` (hand end) commits each wake to the outbox before sending it, re-offers every un-acked wake when it starts and when the brain sends `wake_reoffer`, applies the brain's `wake_receipt`, and reports wakes that cannot be recorded (`unrecorded`) or that expired un-acked after their scope closed (`expired`) as `wake_dropped`. `serveHandWakes` (brain end) commits each wake to the inbox, answers the receipt only after the commit, and hands first commits (`onCommitted`) and drop reports (`onDropped`) to the brain.
- `packages/agent/src/execution/wake-outbox.ts`: `due()`, the wakes a re-offer sends; `reoffer` uses it.
- `packages/agent/src/index.ts`: exports the wake link, `createWakeOutbox`, `createWakeInbox`, their types, `WAKE_RETENTION_MS` and the `MemoryStoreMedium` type.

### Why

- S14 built the durable outbox and inbox but nothing wired them, so a hand's late results had no reliable way to the brain. `packages/agent/src/index.ts` exports them so the brain's host wires the link with no protocol code of its own.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The outbox, the inbox and the hand transport are agent-core primitives below the extension layer, and only `packages/agent/src/index.ts` makes them reachable from the package.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the export block of `packages/agent/src/index.ts`.

## 2026-11-15 - Hand wake payloads and their delivery on the brain (senpi#5778, PR #6767)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/execution/wake-delivery.ts`: replaces the terminal-only `terminalWakeSender` / `deliverTerminalWake` (S14) with the general hand route. `loopWakeRoute` turns `sendMessage`, `sendUserMessage` and `ui.notify` from a hand runtime's late work into wakes (`message`, `user_message`, `notice`) carrying exactly the session call's arguments, plus the loop session id when a loop-hook call started the work. `lateLoopRoute` is a loop-hook call's late route (the wake route, or a reported drop on a runtime without one). On the brain, `parseHandWake` parses a taken wake at that boundary (`MalformedHandWakeError` on anything else) and `deliverHandWake` makes the same call a local session makes.

### Why

- Every late effect of a hand's work must reach the loop as a local session would see it (senpi#5778), not only terminal notices.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The wake payload is the contract between the hand runtime and the hosted brain, below any one extension.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `wake-delivery.ts` is rewritten whole.

## 2026-11-15 - pi.isLoopRemote() and the hand's wake route (senpi#5778, PR #6767)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/types.ts`: `ExtensionAPI.isLoopRemote()` and the runtime's `isLoopRemote`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/loader.ts`: the runtime answers `isLoopRemote` false until a host binds it; `pi.isLoopRemote()` reads it.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/runner.ts`: `LoopWakeRoute`; `bindSessionlessFacts({ loopWake })` sends `pi.sendMessage` from work no loop-hook call started, and every `pi.sendUserMessage`, to the route, and makes `pi.isLoopRemote()` true. Without the route both stay loop-side and unbound, as before.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/ui-presentation.ts`: `PresentationBroker` takes an optional `lateNotify`; a notice made outside any call, or after its call's port closed, goes there instead of nowhere. It is presentation-only, so `hasUI` stays false.

### Why

- On a hand, a late `pi.sendMessage` threw `sendMessage is loop-side and not bound` (the sessionless binding in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/runner.ts`), and late notices reached no UI. Builtin notifiers (terminal, monitor, eval) never notified at all, because a hand never gets `session_start` for builtins and their guards read loop-side facts; `pi.isLoopRemote()` (`packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/types.ts`, backed by `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/loader.ts`) tells them the loop applies those guards.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The runtime's `sendMessage` / `sendUserMessage` binding (`packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/runner.ts`), the `pi` object built in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/loader.ts` and the `ExtensionAPI` contract in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/types.ts` are runner internals and the public API itself.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- MEDIUM: `bindSessionlessFacts` in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/runner.ts` (open PR #6751 adds `loopHookWrite` there); the `ExtensionAPI` message section of `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/types.ts`.
- LOW: the runtime literal and the `sendUserMessage` member of `pi` in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/loader.ts`.

## 2026-11-15 - Terminal notices from a hand (senpi#5778, PR #6767)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/terminal/extension.ts`: `bindNotifiers` builds the completion and monitor notifiers at load as well as at every `session_start`, so a hand (which never gets `session_start` for builtins) has them. They send through `pi.sendMessage`; `createTerminalExtension({ sendMessage })` (S14) is removed.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/terminal/notify.ts`: `TerminalNotifierDeps.loopRemote`; on a hand the session guards (mode, model) are the receiving loop's, so they are skipped here.

### Why

- A background command's exit notice and monitor events on a hand were dropped without a trace.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The notifier guards live inside the terminal builtin.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `session_start` notifier block of `extension.ts`.

## 2026-11-15 - The hand's durable wake outbox (senpi#5778, PR #6767)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/hand/hand-wakes.ts` (new): `openHandWakeStore`, the memory store's commit semantics over one owner-only JSON file written atomically on each commit (each opening claims the next owner epoch); `openHandWakeOutbox`; `handWakesOver`, a hand's scopes' wakes over its wake link.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/hand/hand-entry.ts`: opens the outbox at `<agentDir>/rpc/hand-wakes.json` after the live-hand check, splits the `hand.wake` lane off the transport (`splitLanes`), starts the hand wake link and gives the loopless hand its wakes.

### Why

- A wake committed before a hand process dies must be re-offered by the next one.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The hand entry owns the transport and the process lifetime.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- MEDIUM: `runHand` in `hand-entry.ts` (open PR #6747 changes the `createLooplessHand` call there).

## 2026-11-15 - Late loop-hook and tool-side results go to the scope's wake outbox (senpi#5778, PR #6767)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/loopless-tool-handlers.ts`: `createLoopHookCall(late)` sends a call's messages and notices that arrive after it ended to `late(sessionId)`. The per-session hold that rode the next reply (`LATE_PER_SESSION`, `droppedLate`) is removed. (Open PRs #6747/#6751 move this region to `loop-hook-call.ts`.)
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/runtime-factory-loopless.ts`: `LooplessRuntimeProfile.wake`; the runtime binds the scope's wake route (`loopWake`), the loop-hook call's late route and the presentation broker's late notices.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/remote-executor-scope.ts`: `HandWakes`; `LooplessHandOptions.wakes`. Each scope's runtime offers to its own scope, and disposing a scope closes it in the outbox (its retention starts).

### Why

- Held late items were lost when the reply carrying them never reached the loop, when the scope was disposed, or when the session made no further call (senpi#5778). Tool-side late work had no path at all.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The loop-hook call and the scope runtimes are hand internals.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- HIGH: `createLoopHookCall` and `LoopHookEffects` (moved by #6747, extended by #6751).
