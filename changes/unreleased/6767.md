## 2026-11-15 - Late hand results reach the brain over a durable, bounded wake link (senpi#5778, senpi#6763, PR #6767)

### What changed

- `packages/agent/src/execution/wake-link.ts` (new): the wake link on the hand transport's `hand.wake` lane. `createHandWakeLink` (hand end) commits each wake before sending it; on start and on the brain's `wake_reoffer` it sweeps the outbox, then sends every due drop report as `wake_dropped` with its `eventId` and every un-acked wake as `wake`, and applies the brain's `wake_receipt`. A wake it cannot record goes out as a best-effort `wake_dropped` (`unrecorded`, no `eventId`). `serveHandWakes` (brain end) passes the brain's `check` to `inbox.receive`, which runs it only for a new wake of a bound scope (an unbound scope is retryable `unknown_scope`; `not_scope_session` is reported as `WakeRefusedError`), commits each wake or durable drop report to the inbox, answers the receipt only after the commit, hands first commits to `onCommitted` and best-effort drops to `onDropped`, and reports an undecodable frame to `onFault`.
- `packages/agent/src/execution/wake-outbox.ts`: optional `limits` (per scope and total; past either a wake the brain refused for good is evicted with all its state, reported `refused`, or else the new wake is refused before it is sent, `WakeOverflowError`, reported `overflow`; a sent wake is never evicted) and `onFault`. Drop reports (`overflow`, `expired`, `refused`, `malformed`) are durable records in the outbox document (`drops`), acked like wakes, coalesced only while this instance never sent them, never bounded and never reported dropped themselves. A refusal for good records its time; when it lapses after the retention the wake is reported `refused`, and `sweep` purges refusal entries whose wake is gone. `sweep` also removes malformed entries (reported `malformed`, `MalformedWakeError` to `onFault`). `due()` lists drop reports first, then un-acked wakes.
- `packages/agent/src/execution/wake-outbox-record.ts` (new): the strict outbox record codec and the drop-report bookkeeping.
- `packages/agent/src/execution/wake-inbox.ts`: `receive(sender, wake, check?)` acks a duplicate first, then refuses a closed scope, then checks the binding and runs `check`; optional `onUndeliverable` and `maxAttempts` (default 3). With them a wake whose consumer throws is reported, kept for later drains with its failure count in the record (`failures`), and taken as poisoned at its last attempt, while the wakes behind it are delivered. Without them a failure rejects the drain, as before. `sweep` keeps the other fields of the record.
- `packages/agent/src/execution/wake-types.ts`: `WakeRefusal` adds `not_scope_session`; `WakeDropReason`, `WAKE_DROPPED_SOURCE`, `WakeLimits`; the outbox record's `drops` and timed `refused`; the inbox record's `failures`; the meaning of `WakeEvent.scope`.
- `packages/agent/src/execution/wake-delivery.ts` (new): the brain side of a hand's wakes. `parseHandWake` (sources `message`, `user_message`, `entry`, `notice`, `wake.dropped`), `deliverHandWake` (the call a local session makes; a wake that would start a turn is skipped in a one-shot session or one without a model), `deliverHandDrop` (the consumer of `onDropped`: a warning notice), `scopeSessionCheck` (for `serveHandWakes({ check })`), `ONE_SHOT_MODES`.
- `packages/agent/src/index.ts`: exports all of the above, `createWakeOutbox`, `createWakeInbox`, `WAKE_RETENTION_MS` and the `MemoryStoreMedium` type.

### Why

- S14 built the durable outbox and inbox but nothing wired them, so a hand's late results had no reliable way to the brain (senpi#5778, senpi#6763). The #6767 review asked for a bound on un-acked wakes with reported drops (M1), strict decoding (L2) and a session check on the brain (L3). The hosted brain's lane (omo-service#1176) asked for the delivery functions from `packages/agent/src/index.ts`, the package entry it already imports, for undecodable frames to be reported, and for one undeliverable wake to stop blocking the inbox.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The outbox, the inbox, the wake link and the hand transport are agent-core primitives below the extension layer, and only `packages/agent/src/index.ts` makes them reachable from the package.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the export block of `packages/agent/src/index.ts`.

## 2026-11-15 - A hand's late effects become wakes; the brain-side delivery moves to agent-core (senpi#5778, PR #6767)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/execution/hand-wake-route.ts` (new): `loopWakeRoute` turns `sendMessage`, `sendUserMessage`, `appendEntry` and `ui.notify` from a hand runtime's late work into wakes (`message`, `user_message`, `entry`, `notice`) carrying exactly the session call's arguments, plus the loop session id when a loop-hook call started the work. `lateLoopRoute` is a loop-hook call's late route (the wake route, or a reported drop on a runtime without one).
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/execution/wake-delivery.ts` (removed): S14's terminal-only `terminalWakeSender` / `deliverTerminalWake`; the brain-side parse and delivery now live in agent-core (`execution/wake-delivery.ts`).
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/hand/hook-contract.ts`: no longer parses `droppedLate` from a hand's loop-hook reply.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/egress/wire.ts`: `HookModelRequestReply.droppedLate` is removed; a hand never sends it now (late effects go to the loop as wakes).

### Why

- Every late effect of a hand's work must reach the loop as a local session would see it (senpi#5778), not only terminal notices, and the hosted brain must import the delivery from the package it already uses.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The wake payload is the contract between the hand runtime and the hosted brain, below any one extension; `packages/coding-agent/src/core/hand/hook-contract.ts` and `packages/coding-agent/src/core/egress/wire.ts` are that contract's hook-reply side.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `HookModelRequestReply` in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/egress/wire.ts`; `loopHookRequestHooks` in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/hand/hook-contract.ts`.

## 2026-11-15 - pi.isLoopRemote() and the hand's wake route (senpi#5778, PR #6767)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/types.ts`: `ExtensionAPI.isLoopRemote()` and the runtime's `isLoopRemote`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/loader.ts`: the runtime answers `isLoopRemote` false until a host binds it; `pi.isLoopRemote()` reads it.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/runner.ts`: `LoopWakeRoute`; `bindSessionlessFacts({ loopWake })`: `pi.sendMessage`, `pi.sendUserMessage` and `pi.appendEntry` go to the live loop-hook call's sender or writer when a call's work makes them (#6751's `loopHookWrite`), and otherwise to the scope's wake route; `pi.isLoopRemote()` answers true. Without the route they stay loop-side and unbound.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/ui-presentation.ts`: `PresentationBroker` takes an optional `lateNotify`; a notice made outside any call, or after its call's port closed, goes there instead of nowhere. It is presentation-only, so `hasUI` stays false.

### Why

- On a hand, a late `pi.sendMessage` threw `sendMessage is loop-side and not bound` (the sessionless binding in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/runner.ts`), and late notices reached no UI. Builtin notifiers (terminal, monitor, eval) never notified, because a hand never gets `session_start` for builtins and their guards read loop-side facts; `pi.isLoopRemote()` (`packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/types.ts`, backed by `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/loader.ts`) tells them the loop applies those guards.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The runtime's `sendMessage` / `sendUserMessage` / `appendEntry` binding (`packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/runner.ts`), the `pi` object built in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/loader.ts` and the `ExtensionAPI` contract in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/types.ts` are runner internals and the public API itself.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- MEDIUM: `bindSessionlessFacts` in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/runner.ts`; the `ExtensionAPI` message section of `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/types.ts`.
- LOW: the runtime literal and the `sendUserMessage` member of `pi` in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/loader.ts`.

## 2026-11-15 - The hand's durable, bounded wake outbox (senpi#5778, PR #6767)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/hand/hand-wakes.ts` (new): `openHandWakeOutbox` on the hand's state file (it claims the file's next owner epoch, so an older hand is refused from then on), with `HAND_WAKE_LIMITS` (256 per scope, 1024 in total) and `onFault`; `handWakesOver`, a hand's scopes' wakes over its wake link.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/hand/hand-entry.ts`: opens the outbox at `<agentDir>/rpc/hand-wakes.json` after the live-hand check (faults reported on stderr), splits the `hand.wake` lane off the transport (`splitLanes`), starts the hand wake link and gives the loopless hand its wakes.

### Why

- A wake committed before a hand process dies must be re-offered by the next one, and the file must stay bounded by what is pending (#6767 review H1, M1).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The hand entry owns the transport and the process lifetime.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- MEDIUM: `runHand` in `hand-entry.ts`.

## 2026-11-15 - Late loop-hook and tool-side results go to the scope's wake outbox; a locked state-file store (senpi#5778, PR #6767)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/loop-hook-call.ts`: `createLoopHookCall(late)` sends what a call's work does after the call ended (messages, notices, #6751's entries and user messages) to `late(sessionId)`; the per-session hold that rode the next reply (`LATE_PER_SESSION`, `dropped`) is removed. A held Stop's stop state and block follow-up therefore reach an idle loop as wakes (senpi#6770 L1).
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/loopless-loop-hooks.ts`: a reply no longer carries `droppedLate`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/runtime-factory-loopless.ts`: `LooplessRuntimeProfile.wake`; the runtime binds the scope's wake route (`loopWake`), the loop-hook call's late route and the presentation broker's late notices.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/remote-executor-scope.ts`: `HandWakes`; `LooplessHandOptions.wakes`. Each scope's runtime offers to its own scope, and disposing a scope closes it in the outbox (its retention starts).
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/state-file-store.ts` (new): `openStateFileStore`, a durable state store in one owner-only JSON file that keeps documents and the owner epoch, never the log, so the file and every rewrite are bounded by the state (#6767 review H1). Every mutation is a locked read-check-write against the disk (`ownership-safe-lock.ts`), so a stale epoch is refused (L1). `claimStateStore` claims the next epoch. The hand-link endpoint's store (#6758) converges on it.

### Why

- Held late items were lost when the reply carrying them never reached the loop, when the scope was disposed, or when the session made no further call (senpi#5778); tool-side late work had no path at all (senpi#6763). The first outbox file kept the memory store's whole log and an older process could still overwrite it.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The loop-hook call, the scope runtimes and the hand's durable state are hand internals.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- HIGH: `createLoopHookCall` and `LoopHookEffects` in `loop-hook-call.ts` (#6751's region).
