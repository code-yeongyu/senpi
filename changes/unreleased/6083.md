## 2026-11-06 - A claimed question refuses a late progress draft (senpi#5960)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/connection-question-bridge.ts`: a question records its claim when an answer wins, and `progress` answers `question_already_resolved` once that claim exists instead of overwriting the selections the winner is settling with. A `beforeAnswer` that throws releases the claim, so the question stays answerable.

### Why

- An `extension_ui_progress` that arrived while the winner's `beforeAnswer` ran rewrote the pending answers: a winner answering A with a draft B arriving during admission settled as `answered` with B's selections, which breaks "the first answer wins" in the same re-entrant window #5420 guards (item 3 of #5960).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The claim and the draft both live in the RPC connection's question bridge; the extension raising the question never sees either.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `claimed` flag and the `progress`/`respond` closures inside `ask`, plus the `progress` method of `ConnectionQuestionBridge`.
