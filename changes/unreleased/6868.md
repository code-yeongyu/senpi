## 2026-11-22 - Start or refuse queued input after a provider error (senpi#6868)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/agent-session.ts`: after retries and fallback finish, queued input starts a new turn unless a provider usage hold refuses it before dispatch. User aborts still leave the input waiting. Transient errors without a hold keep the omo#14428 backoff for peer deliveries.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/pending-input-session.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/core/pending-input-types.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/core/pending-input.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/core/retry-fallback/queued-usage-hold.ts`, and `packages/coding-agent/src/core/retry-fallback/usage-limit.ts`: reuse the existing usage-limit error and pending-input journal to record the refusal.

### Why

- A terminal provider error ends the agent loop before it polls for queued follow-ups. A remote sender cannot provide an unrelated prompt to wake its own already-queued input.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The session owns retry settlement, queue admission, and the journal transaction that removes refused input.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- The queued continuation decision in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/agent-session.ts`.

## 2026-11-22 - Report the refusal of a queued RPC prompt (senpi#6868)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/client-admissions.ts` and `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/effect-status.ts`: project the pending-input journal's usage-limit refusal as a failed input effect, including its reason and detail.

### Why

- A waiting sender needs a terminal receipt even when its input never reaches a turn.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The RPC admission ledger owns effect receipts and their recovery after reopen.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- The admission event subscriber and restore path.
