## 2026-11-21 - Detect Rex terminal Kitty graphics and OSC 8 hyperlinks (senpi#7152)

### What changed

- `packages/tui/src/tmux-image-capability.ts`: adds `isRexTermName` (matching `rex`, `xterm-rex`, and `xterm-rex-*` without matching unrelated substrings) and adds `"rex"` to `TmuxKittyTerminal`, `parseTmuxKittyTerminalOverride`, and `identifyTmuxKittyClient` with placeholder placement.
- `packages/tui/src/terminal-capabilities.ts`: `detectTerminalCapabilities` recognizes Rex via `TERM_PROGRAM=rex` (outside `tmux`/`screen`) or `isRexTermName(TERM)` and returns `{ images: "kitty", trueColor: true, hyperlinks: true, kittyUnicodePlaceholders: true }`. `outerKittyGraphicsMode` also classifies Rex termnames as `"placeholder"`.
- `packages/tui/src/terminal-image.ts`: `detectCapabilitiesFromEnvironment` recognizes Rex via `TERM_PROGRAM=rex` or `isRexTermName(TERM)` and returns `{ images: "kitty", trueColor: true, hyperlinks: true }`.

### Why

- Rex (libghostty-backed terminal multiplexer) sets `TERM_PROGRAM=rex` locally and `TERM=xterm-rex` locally and over SSH (when `TERM_PROGRAM` is not forwarded). Without recognition, both detectors fell back to `{ images: null, hyperlinks: false }`, disabling inline Kitty graphics and clickable OSC 8 links even though Rex supports both including Kitty Unicode virtual placements (`U=1`).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Terminal capability detection lives in `packages/tui` and runs before extensions load; extensions cannot register built-in terminal matchers in `detectCapabilitiesFromEnvironment`, `detectTerminalCapabilities`, or `decideTmuxImageCapability`.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the Ghostty neighbourhood in `packages/tui/src/terminal-capabilities.ts`, `packages/tui/src/terminal-image.ts`, and `packages/tui/src/tmux-image-capability.ts`.
