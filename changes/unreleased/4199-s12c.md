## 2026-10-27 - A launch participant holds its presence lock from before its claim until quiescence (desktop#4199)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/endpoint-reservation-store.ts`: moves to schema 4. A reservation records the reserving owner's presence object and its `released` commit (`releaseOwner`, refused with `owner_not_terminal` while pending). A claim of a role with a predeclared object needs a binding to that object (`presence_not_bound`) at the revision its claimant read (`revision_changed`), and records `presenceBound`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-launch-reservation.ts`: `acquirePresence` takes a predeclared object's lock through the bridge's nonblocking probe of the recorded tag, identity and locality; busy is `presence_occupied`, anything else `presence_unknown`. `claimLaunchRole` acquires before the claim transaction and releases the unused handle when the claim is refused. `admitClaimedParticipant` keeps the lock once admitted and returns a release that commits `released` before closing it. `offerSupervisorLaunch` takes the owner's held presence and records it.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-lifecycle-launch.ts`: adds the `presence_occupied` and `presence_unknown` rejections.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-ensure-start.ts`: acquires the reserving owner's presence before `ensureSocketSecret` and the reservation, and commits the owner's `released` after `markServing`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-lifecycle.ts` and `host-lifecycle-shutdown.ts`: a reserved supervisor commits `released` and closes its lock only at the end of a shutdown that observed its child's exit and closed its listener.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/supervisor-route.ts`: the host keeps its lock until its process exits.

### Why

- Design 4.2a, "Acquire before authority; release after quiescence": the reserving process acquires its presence lock before persisting its reservation and before credential mutation; each supervisor/host acquires its predeclared object before the nonce-claim transaction, which revalidates the offer and revision and records the binding; failure releases only its own unused handle and exits without spawn/listen; a busy lock proves only occupied authority; a claimant holds its lock through grant and shutdown and releases it after its `released` commit.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Reservation, claim and the native presence guards run before extensions load and own process identity and child launch.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/endpoint-reservation-store.ts`: schema SQL, `reserve`/`claim` signatures and row mapping.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-launch-reservation.ts`: offer, claim and admission helpers.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-ensure-start.ts`: the start prologue and `reserveSupervisorLaunch`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-lifecycle-shutdown.ts`: the end of `performShutdown`.
