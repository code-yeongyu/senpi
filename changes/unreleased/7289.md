## 2026-11-22 - Preserve goal limit waits through session reopening and hold release (#6405)

### What changed
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/goal/session-start-continuation.ts` classifies an all-error fallback ladder by its final answer. `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/goal/index.ts` rechecks the branch when a held wake is released.

### Why
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/goal/session-start-continuation.ts` mistook an earlier rate limit for the final outcome. `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/goal/index.ts` lost the pending limit exemption on release.

### Why an extension could not handle it
- Wake admission is owned by the builtin lifecycle in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/goal/index.ts` and the branch reader in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/goal/session-start-continuation.ts`.

### Expected merge conflict zones
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/goal/index.ts`: `held_wakes_released`. `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/goal/session-start-continuation.ts`: `wasRefusedForLimit`.
