## 2026-11-22 - Read current session-owned activity (senpi#3733)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/session-activity.ts` exposes the tracker's per-source counts.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/agent-session.ts` reports those counts together with `isSessionBusy`.

### Why

- A client that reattaches cannot reconstruct work that started before it connected.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The engine owns both its in-session work barrier and the aggregate wake-source registry.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- Activity getters in `AgentSession` and `WakeSourceTracker`.

## 2026-11-22 - Report occupancy in RPC session state (senpi#3733)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/rpc-session-state-types.ts` declares the optional `activity` field.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/rpc-session-state.ts` populates it from the engine.

### Why

- A session can be busy between turns, even when `isStreaming` is false.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- RPC clients need a current snapshot from the engine's shared state builder.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- `RpcSessionState` and `buildRpcSessionState`.
