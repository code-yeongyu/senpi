## 2026-11-05 - Sealed secret answers on the worker runtime (senpi#4249)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-worker-secret-route.ts` is new: `WorkerSecretRoutes`, the isolate's secret route, moved out of `session-worker.ts`. It covers:
  - `raiser` and `sealed` from the host's select;
  - `accepts` is raiser-only;
  - the request key's `secret_sealed_offer` and the settle with outcome and winning grant;
  - `answer` runs 7.1 steps 3 and 6 at the isolate's latch, with its own step-up `jti` cache.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-worker-host-secrets.ts` is new: `WorkerHostSecrets`, the host half, moved out of `session-worker-client.ts`. It:
  - selects with the 5.2 semantics, reporting `raiser` and `sealed`;
  - bounds frames, signs the sealed offer and fans it out, and refuses a malformed `secret_sealed_offer` instead of relaying it as a raiser frame;
  - admits the isolate's `secret_settled` once per held id, with a known outcome and a winner only among the grants whose answers it forwarded for that id, and fans the settle out;
  - forwards authenticated sealed answers only for held ids, one in flight and four queued per id, re-checking a queued answer's grant when it leaves the queue;
  - relays the isolate's sealed answer result only in the shapes its latch can produce, rebuilt field by field;
  - unbinds its latch when the worker exits.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-worker-secrets.ts`: `isSealedOffer` is added and requires a 32-byte base64url `hpkePk`; a `secret_sealed_offer` is admitted once per id within `secretFrameBytes`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-worker-protocol.ts`: adds `secret_sealed_answer` and `secret_sealed_result`; `secret_settled` carries `outcome` and `winnerGrant`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-worker-requests.ts`: a sealed answer is a control-class request.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-worker.ts` and `session-worker-client.ts`: use the two new modules.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-event-writer-presenters.ts`: a secret reply's `type` and `id` are written after its fields, so no field replaces them.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/secret-input-requests.ts`: `SealedAnswer` is plain data, the latch takes a claim context (grant re-check, `jti` cache), and the route gains `raiser`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/secret-input-presentations.ts`: passes the claim context to the latch, reports `raiser`, and retires a settled offer instead of withdrawing it.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/secret-sealed-holders.ts`: `answer` returns data and `admitJti` is added; `settle` compares grant ids; a settled offer is retired, taking no more token records while staying answerable for `replay` until forgotten.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/secret-sealed-answers.ts`: the open takes the plain `SealedAnswer` data, so the isolate opens the same values the host checked.

### Why

- D11 secret phase (senpi#4249), row S5-4: the worker runtime, the default for stdio hosts and embedders, accepts sealed answers with the request key confined to the isolate that owns the latch (design 5.5).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Worker isolates, their host protocol and the secret prompt latch are host internals of the RPC mode.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- None: every path is fork-only.
