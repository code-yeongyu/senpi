## 2026-11-05 - Rendered read for client-rendered app shells (senpi#5939)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/webfetch/webfetch/content.ts`: `looksLikeAppShell` detects static HTML that runs scripts and shows almost no text.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/webfetch/webfetch/content.lazy.ts`: lazy export of `looksLikeAppShell`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/webfetch/webfetch/rendered-read.ts`, `rendered-read.lazy.ts`: a `PageRenderer` over senpi's main-thread `Bun.WebView` service, loaded on first use.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/webfetch/webfetch/tool.ts`: `createWebfetchTool({ renderer })`; an app shell is read again rendered within the call's timeout, with `details.rendered`/`renderNote` and a model-only note; `PI_WEBFETCH_RENDER=0` turns it off.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/webfetch/webfetch/renderers.ts`: `rendering` progress phase and `rendered` result tag.

### Why

- Client-rendered pages returned 0-12 characters (excalidraw.com, app.diagrams.net, open.spotify.com, web.telegram.org) with status 200 and no sign the page was never rendered (senpi#5939).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The conversion lives inside the builtin webfetch tool; an outside extension would have to replace the whole tool.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- MEDIUM: `tool.ts` execute body and `WebfetchDetails` when pi-webfetch is re-synced.
