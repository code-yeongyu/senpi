## 2026-11-02 - `--supervised` lets a parent-watched process skip its lifetime crash marker (oh-my-openagent#10334, PR #5563)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/main.ts`: the lifetime seam passes `supervised: processHasSupervisor(appMode, parsed.supervised === true)` (`src/core/process-crash-record.ts`) instead of `appMode === "rpc"`. An RPC process is still always supervised; any other mode is supervised only when launched with `--supervised`. The sweep of dead peers' markers runs in every mode, as before.

### Why

- omo's memory-reflection supervisor deadline-kills its `senpi -p` child on purpose. The child's marker survived the SIGKILL and the next start recorded it as `{kind: "print", detection: "unclean_exit"}`, which omo reported as a crash with `signal=unknown`. The process that does the kill owns the record of the death, the same contract the RPC host and omo's task parent already follow.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The marker is written at launch, before any extension's session hooks run, and an extension cannot remove another process's marker without racing the sweep.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `recordProcessLifetime(...)` call after `readPipedStdin` in `main()`, and its import line.

## 2026-11-02 - `--supervised` built-in flag (oh-my-openagent#10334, PR #5563)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/cli/args.ts`: `Args.supervised`, parsed from `--supervised` as a built-in boolean flag (no longer an unknown extension flag) and listed in `--help` after `--no-session`.

### Why

- A launcher that watches its child's exit and records the death itself needs to tell senpi so, otherwise a deliberate kill is recorded as a crash. A flag applies to exactly the process it is passed to; an env var would leak into the child's own tool subprocesses and silence their crash markers.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The flag decides launch behaviour in `main()` before extensions load, and extension flags are only resolved after loading.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `Args` interface after `noSession`, the `--no-session` branch of `parseArgs`, and the matching help line.
