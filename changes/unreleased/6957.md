## 2026-11-18 - One hold predicate for the leaf guard and the automatic-input hold (senpi#6957)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/agent-session.ts`: new `automaticInputHoldLive()`; `noteAutomaticInput` uses it.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/automatic-input-gate.ts`: `automaticInputWouldBeHeld` calls `automaticInputHoldLive()` instead of repeating the hold check.

### Why

- The `prompt` leaf guard (senpi#6922) predicts whether a hold would keep the prompt as a notice. With its own copy of the predicate, that prediction could drift from the hold it predicts.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Both call sites are core RPC admission and session hold code that run before any extension hook.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `noteAutomaticInput` in `agent-session.ts`.
