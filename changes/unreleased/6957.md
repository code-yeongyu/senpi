## 2026-11-18 - AgentSession names the automatic-input hold check once (senpi#6957)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/agent-session.ts`: new `automaticInputHoldLive()` (the user's Stop or a parent's run hold is live); `noteAutomaticInput` uses it instead of its inline check.

### Why

- The RPC `prompt` leaf guard (senpi#6922) predicts whether this hold would keep a prompt as a notice. It needs the same predicate the hold uses, not a copy that can drift.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The hold state lives in the session's engine turn hold, which extensions do not own.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `noteAutomaticInput` in `agent-session.ts`.

## 2026-11-18 - The leaf guard's hold prediction reuses the session's predicate (senpi#6957)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/automatic-input-gate.ts`: `automaticInputWouldBeHeld` returns `!interactiveUser && session.automaticInputHoldLive()` instead of repeating the hold check.

### Why

- With its own copy of the predicate, the `prompt` leaf guard's prediction could drift from the hold it predicts.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Command admission happens in the RPC connection handler before any extension hook sees the prompt.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `automaticInputWouldBeHeld` in `automatic-input-gate.ts`.
