## 2026-11-22 - Queue-scoped removal of a queued message (senpi#7316)

### What changed

- `packages/agent/src/agent.ts`: `removeQueuedMessage(message, queue?)` removes only from the named steering or follow-up queue when one is given; without it, behavior is unchanged.

### Why

- Post-compaction requeue must never move a message between queues (senpi#4068).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The steering and follow-up queues are private to `Agent`.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `removeQueuedMessage` in `agent.ts`.

## 2026-11-22 - Post-compaction requeue collects before removing (senpi#7316)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/agent-session.ts`: `_requeueAtOriginalPlace` collects newer same-mode input before removing it, removes only from that mode's queue, and the redundant flush after the post-compaction exemption check is gone.

### Why

- Review follow-ups from senpi#4046 (senpi#4068): a side effect inside a filter predicate, and a call that read as releasing parked input when the consume already had.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Parked-input ordering is internal to `AgentSession` admission.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `_requeueAtOriginalPlace` and the `agent_end` post-compaction exemption block in `agent-session.ts`.
