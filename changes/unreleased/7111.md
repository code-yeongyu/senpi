## 2026-11-20 - Report admission of a guarded notice-only resume (senpi#7104)

### What changed

- `agent-session.ts` waits for the pending notice's turn-admission decision before answering a guarded `resume_session`. A refused turn is not reported as started and its effect ID is not persisted as a successful resume.

### Why

- A notice can be queued when the RPC arrives but still be held when a Stop overtakes final admission. Counting the notice before admission reported a model turn that never ran.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The coding agent owns the final admission claim, held notice and RPC receipt.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- `agent-session.ts`: notice-only branch of `_resumeOnce`.
