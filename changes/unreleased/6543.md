## 2026-11-12 - A move's landing asserts its lock after the stamp check, and a record's stamp writes only its own keys (senpi#6463's reviews)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/session-move/move-ledger.ts`: the closure `commit` hands its landing is the stamp check, then the move record's lock; the separate lock line after the first stamp check is gone, since `commit` runs the same closure.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/session-move/move-record-file.ts`: a record write takes only `up` and `host` from its stamp and writes them before the record, its landing and its time.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/session-move/move-landing.ts`: the stamp's type is named, `UptimeStamp`.

### Why

- The stamp check's read is awaited; a lock taken during that read inside the landing was seen only after the file had landed.
- A stamp typed as any record, spread last, could replace the record's custody fields; spread first, it could still add a `committing` the landing did not have.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- It is the move ledger's own commit path and record format.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `commit` in `move-ledger.ts`, `write` in `move-record-file.ts`.
