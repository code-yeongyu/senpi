## 2026-11-03 - Clipboard frames pass through the stdout guard (senpi#5680)

### What changed

- `packages/tui/src/terminal.ts`: pass complete BEL-terminated OSC52 clipboard frames with base64 payloads through the captured stdout writer, preserving the original chunk, encoding, callback and return value.
- `packages/tui/test/external-stdout-guard.test.ts`: cover clipboard delivery, rejected non-clipboard-only output, callback completion, backpressure and writer errors.

### Why

- `packages/tui/src/terminal.ts`: the external stdout guard hid clipboard frames while the TUI was running.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `packages/tui/src/terminal.ts`: ProcessTerminal owns the process-wide stdout guard and its captured raw writer.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `packages/tui/src/terminal.ts`, inside installExternalStdoutGuard.
- LOW: `packages/tui/test/external-stdout-guard.test.ts`, the guard harness and clipboard cases.
