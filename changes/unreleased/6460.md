## 2026-11-17 - Await asynchronous permission admission (senpi#6460)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/permission-system/index.ts`: subscribe before `ask`, wait for the request's pending admission or a completed decision, and remove the temporary listener before checking whether to show the prompt.

### Why

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/permission-system/index.ts`: path identity preparation yields before a pending request is registered. Checking the pending list immediately skips the approval dialog and waits indefinitely for its answer.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/permission-system/index.ts` owns the builtin permission preflight and the decision to show its dialog. Another extension cannot repair this skipped prompt without replacing that boundary.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/permission-system/index.ts`, the `ask` and pending-list block inside `authorizeToolCall`.
