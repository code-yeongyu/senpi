## 2026-11-12 - The most-headroom account strategy (senpi#6498)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/credential-pool/selection-order.ts`: `most-headroom` joins `AccountStrategy` and `parseAccountStrategy`. `orderAccounts` takes optional headroom readings; under `most-headroom` it stably sorts the cache-affine base (HRW with the stored order applied) by the percent left in each account's most-used live window. An account with no reported usage counts as unused. `preferHeadroom` reads window liveness through the same `isLiveWindow` helper.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/credential-pool/session-slot.ts`: `orderRotationSlots` passes the policy's headroom readings to `orderAccounts`, so the stream and `selectSessionSlot` rank the same way.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/credential-pool/rotation-stream.ts`: under `most-headroom`, every non-pinned pick is remembered for the session.

### Why

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/credential-pool/selection-order.ts`: reported usage only acted as a 95% cut-off, so an account at 30% and one at 80% ranked the same and one account could drain while others sat idle.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/credential-pool/rotation-stream.ts`: the ranking moves as usage changes; without remembering the pick, a session would hop accounts between requests and lose its prompt cache.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/credential-pool/selection-order.ts` and `packages/coding-agent/src/core/credential-pool/rotation-stream.ts` are the credential pool's own selection path; no extension hook orders pooled accounts.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the strategy switch in `orderAccounts`, the `orderRotationSlots` call, and the remember condition in `rotation-stream.ts`.

## 2026-11-12 - The Claude lane honors most-headroom (senpi#6498)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/affinity.ts`: `selectAccount` passes headroom readings to `orderAccounts`, also on the `accountFallback: false` path, and remembers each affinity key's `most-headroom` pick (pins excluded), so a session keeps its account until a block or the headroom preference moves it.

### Why

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/affinity.ts` owns Claude account selection apart from the generic pool, so it must honor the same strategies.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/affinity.ts` is already the builtin provider's selection owner.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the remembered-choice setup and `remember` calls in `selectAccount`, and the `orderAccounts` calls in `affinity.ts`.

## 2026-11-12 - Credential policies accept most-headroom (senpi#6498)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/credential-policy-schema.ts`: `accountStrategy` accepts `"most-headroom"`.

### Why

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/credential-policy-schema.ts` would otherwise reject the new strategy in `models.json`.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/credential-policy-schema.ts` validates provider configuration before the generic credential pool runs.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `accountStrategy` union in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/credential-policy-schema.ts`.
