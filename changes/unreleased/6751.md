## 2026-11-15 - A hand's loop-hook call reaches the session through the session port (senpi#5898)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/runner.ts`: `LoopHookCallFacts` gains `appendEntry`, `sendUserMessage` and `hasPendingMessages`. `bindSessionlessFacts` takes `loopHookWrite`, routed like `loopHookSend`, so `pi.appendEntry` and `pi.sendUserMessage` from a loop-hook call's work reach that call. A call context binds `hasPendingMessages`.

### Why

- A hosted loop's Stop hook runs on the hand. It writes entries and a follow-up user message to a session that the loop owns, so those writes must go back to the loop instead of failing as loop-side.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `pi.appendEntry` and `pi.sendUserMessage` are bound by the runner. An extension cannot redirect another extension's writes.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `LoopHookCallFacts`, `bindSessionlessFacts` and the loop-hook call branch of `createContext`.

## 2026-11-15 - A hand serves agent_end through the session port (senpi#5898)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/loop-hook-call.ts`: the session port.
  - `run(binding, dispatch)` takes one `LoopHookCallBinding` (`{ sessionId, cwd, idle, carried?, endsTurn? }`) instead of five positional arguments.
  - A call may carry `{ leafId, handEntries }` (`CarriedSession`): the hand-authored custom entries after the loop's last compaction. The read-only session answers `getLeafId` and `reduceHistory` over exactly those, during the call and afterwards for the call's own work (its async context, e.g. a Stop held for background work); from any other context after the call they throw `LoopHookCallEndedError`. The session's identity (`getSessionId`, `getSessionFile`, `getCwd`) stays bound to the call only.
  - After a call with `endsTurn` (an `agent_end`) ended, `isIdle` answers `true` until the loop starts another call for that session, then `false`; other calls keep the idle value they carried.
  - `appendEntry` and `sendUserMessage` are captured as `entries` and `userMessages` effects, and late ones are held under the existing per-session bound. Past the bound one held effect is dropped per overflow: the oldest notice, then message, then user message, and an entry only when nothing else is held.
  - `hasPendingMessages` answers only for user messages the call (or its work) sent, never for late ones of earlier calls that ride its reply.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/loopless-loop-hooks.ts`: `extensionLoopHooks` serves `agent_end` (`{ messages, aborted?, leafId, handEntries }`) as a call that ends the turn, and every reply carries `entries` and `userMessages` when present. Its doc comment lists every served event and the provenance filter.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/runtime-factory-loopless.ts`: binds `loopHookWrite`.

### Why

- senpi#5898: the user's Stop hooks never ran for a hosted loop. On a hand, the Stop path's reads (`getLeafId`, `reduceHistory`) and writes (`appendEntry`, `sendUserMessage`) are loop-side.
- #6751 review: a Stop held for the hand's live background work ran after its call ended, where the call-bound readers threw, `isIdle` stayed at the value carried during the turn and the signal was aborted, so its stop state and follow-up were lost. A held late stop-state entry could be dropped before messages, lowering the Stop reentry count.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The loop-hook lane and the call's session facts belong to the loopless runtime.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- MEDIUM: `extensionLoopHooks` event branches and `dispatchLoopHook` (other #5898 slices add events there).

## 2026-11-15 - A Stop held for background work reports its own turn, from its own context (senpi#5898)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/hooks/stop-lifecycle.ts`: an `agent_end` reads the ended turn (`StopTurn`: session id and turn key) and an `AsyncLocalStorage.snapshot()` when it builds the Stop. A held Stop runs inside that snapshot, so its writes go where the turn's own would, and with no signal: the drain dispatches only when the session is quiet, where a local session's `ctx.signal` is undefined.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/hooks/stop-adapter.ts`: `applyStopHookResult` takes the `StopTurn` instead of a turn key, so it no longer reads the session id when the result arrives. The output-record and unsupported-field diagnostics moved unchanged to `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/hooks/stop-output.ts` (the file was over the size ceiling).

### Why

- On a hand (senpi#5898) a held Stop runs after the loop-hook call that held it has ended: the drain fires from whatever work cleared the wake source, and the call's identity and signal are gone by then. Locally nothing changes: the turn key and session id are the same values read earlier, and an idle session's signal is undefined.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The Stop lifecycle is the hooks builtin's own state.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `PendingStop`, `dispatchStop` and the `agent_end` handler in `stop-lifecycle.ts`; the `applyStopHookResult` signature.

## 2026-11-15 - Correction: what `--hand` as its extensions' home changes (senpi#5898, corrects #6747)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/hand/hand-entry.ts`: `runHand` builds its hand with `twoSidedHome: "hand"` (#6747). Its forwarded loop hooks that run there are `input`, `before_agent_start`, `before_provider_request` and `session_start` (#6747's entry left out `before_provider_request`), and `agent_end` from this PR. The same option also moves two-sided extensions' tool hooks: on `--hand`, `extensionToolHandlers` (`packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/loopless-tool-handlers.ts`) no longer forwards their `tool_call`/`tool_result` to the loop; they run on the hand, beside the same extensions' loop hooks.

### Why

- #6747's review (L1): its fragment named neither `hand-entry.ts` nor the tool-hook placement change. Fragments are immutable once written, so the correction rides this PR.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Where a two-sided extension's hooks run is the loopless runtime's choice, made by the hand's entry point.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `createLooplessHand` call in `runHand`.
