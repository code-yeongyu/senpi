## 2026-11-15 - The loop host acknowledges a hook reply it consumed (senpi#5898)

### What changed

- `packages/agent/src/execution/loop-hook-host-protocol.ts`: a new message, `hook.ack { callId }`, loop host to hand.
- `packages/agent/src/execution/loop-hook-host.ts`: the host sends `hook.ack` once it consumed a call's `hook.reply`.
- `packages/agent/src/execution/loop-hook-host-hand.ts`: a call that replied stays reachable for its cancel until the host acknowledges the reply. A cancel then still aborts its hooks' signal, because the host settled the call by the cancel and dropped the reply.

### Why

- Review of #6808 (L1): a cancel that came after the hand replied but before the host read the reply reached nothing on the hand, so the effects in that dropped reply were lost.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The loop-hook link protocol belongs to the agent package's execution layer.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the message union and decoder in `loop-hook-host-protocol.ts`, the `onFrame` handlers of the host and the hand.

## 2026-11-15 - A dropped hook reply's effects reach the loop once, in order (senpi#5898)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/loop-hook-call.ts`: a call keeps its cancel listener after it returns, so a cancel that comes after the reply sends the reply's effects through the late route. The cancel aborts the call before it flushes anything, and the flush keeps the order the work made the effects in.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/execution/hand-wake-route.ts`: the late route's sends never throw; an effect whose value cannot travel to the loop (a cycle, a BigInt) is reported to its `dropped` reporter, and the flush goes on.

### Why

- Review of #6808 (L1, L2, L3): a late cancel lost the reply's effects, a value that could not travel threw out of the abort listener and skipped the rest of the flush, and the flush grouped effects by kind.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The loop-hook call and its effect routing belong to the loopless runtime.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `run` in `loop-hook-call.ts` and `lateLoopRoute` in `hand-wake-route.ts`.
