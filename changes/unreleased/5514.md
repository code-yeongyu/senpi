## 2026-11-02 - A turn whose own output was dropped before its replay claim fails instead of resolving empty (senpi#4137)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/session-registry-pump.ts`: in `handleMessage`, an unclaimed `result` that matches the active turn (`user_message_uuid`) after the pre-replay buffer overflowed (`turn.preReplayOverflowed`) throws `SessionTurnAttributionError` instead of claiming and finishing the turn. A foreign result still resets the overflow, and a matching result with nothing dropped still resolves.

### Why

The pre-replay buffer drops an overflowing segment as another turn's (senpi#2192). Our own result arriving without the replay echo proves that segment was this turn's output; resolving it as an answer recorded the turn as synced, so the empty-response retry sent a zero-message delta onto a transcript that already held the lost tool call, and the model answered text-only (senpi#4137). Failing the turn keeps the attempt's retry checkpoint at the pre-turn boundary.

### Why an extension could not handle it

The resident session pump and its turn attribution are internal to this provider extension; no hook observes the SDK message stream before the pump claims a turn.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the unclaimed `result && resultMatchesTurn` branch of `handleMessage`.
