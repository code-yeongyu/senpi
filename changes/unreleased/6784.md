## 2026-11-15 - A call's wake port and relocation host end with the call (senpi#6754)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-port-hosts.ts`: `carriedWakePort` and `carriedRelocationHost` take the call's `live` state; after the call ended, `arm`, `cancel` and `request` throw `LoopHookCallEndedError` instead of recording a change nothing reads.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/loop-hook-call.ts`: passes `() => live` to both.
- Correction to the #6780 entry "A hand's call materializes the session's documents and answers wakes and relocation": with carried documents, `getSessionDir`/`getSessionFile` answer the call's private directory from `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/loop-hook-session.ts` (`readOnlySession`), not `loop-hook-call.ts`; the code moved there in the same PR.

### Why

- A port retained past its call silently lost its writes (review L2 on #6780); the corrected path is review L1 on #6780.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The call's session ports belong to the hand engine.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the two factories in `session-port-hosts.ts` and their call sites in `createLoopHookCall`.
