## 2026-11-27 - A startup phase that keeps reporting progress is extended, not cut at its allowance (senpi#7545)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-startup-wire.ts`: `StartupMilestone` gains `progress`, a record that completes nothing; `STARTUP_PROGRESS_INTERVAL_MS` (2 s) is how often a producer checks for progress and `STARTUP_PROGRESS_MIN_CPU_MS` (50 ms) the CPU a tick needs to count. `StartupHandOff.progress: true` is the launcher's statement that it reads `progress` records; `parseStartupHandOff` keeps it only when it is exactly `true`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-startup-run.ts`: `beginStartupAttempt` sets `progress: true` in the supervisor's hand-off. The supervisor passes the same hand-off on to its host child.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-startup-beacon.ts`: `startStartupProgress(handOff, report, cpuMs)` runs only when the hand-off says the launcher reads progress. A launcher built before progress records (for example the old host of a handover) never says so and receives none. Every interval it reports `progress` if the process spent at least 50 ms of CPU since the last tick. It stops at the producer's last milestone (`public_published` for the supervisor, `host_runtime` for the host child) or at the decision budget. Neither timer keeps the process alive.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-startup-events.ts`: a `progress` record extends the current phase only when it comes from the producer that owns that phase; from the other producer it is `ignored`, not a protocol failure. Progress records keep the strict per-producer sequence and have their own cap (256) apart from the 32-milestone cap.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-startup-policy.ts`: `StartupController.progress()` moves the phase deadline to `STARTUP_PROGRESS_STALL_MS` (15 s) after the report when that is later. The deadline never moves past twice the phase's allowance from its entry, and stays clipped to `STARTUP_DECISION_BUDGET_MS` (180 s, unchanged). A phase whose deadline progress moved, and that then went a stall bound without a report, times out with `limit: "stall"`. A timeout at the unextended allowance or at the cap stays `limit: "phase"`.

### Why

- On a loaded machine (agents-newyork-1, load average ~35 on 14 cores), ensure stopped host starts that were slow but working, for example `phase=public_published; limit=phase; phase_budget_ms=15000; elapsed_ms=16808`. A fixed per-phase allowance cannot tell a slow start from a stuck one. CPU spent on the start's own work can, and a producer parked on a hung await spends none.
- Review of senpi#7545: a bare event-loop ticker held a phase stuck on an await until the 180 s budget, and a `progress` record sent to a launcher built before it would have failed a handover's successor as an unknown milestone.
- Launcher-owned phases (`presenter_resolved`, `public_verified`) are unchanged.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The startup controller, the startup pipe and the supervisor's beacon run before any extension loads, in the launcher and the supervisor, which run no extensions.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `host-startup-policy.ts` `startStartupController` (`enter`/`expire` and the returned controller).
- LOW: `host-startup-events.ts` `startupEventIngress`, the record dispatch after the sequence checks.

### Fixed
- An RPC host that starts slowly on a loaded machine is no longer stopped at the first deadline while it is still working. A startup phase the host is actively working through is extended up to twice its allowance, and fails 15 s after the work stops. The 180 s overall startup budget is unchanged. ([#7545](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/senpi/pull/7545))
