## 2026-11-22 - Drain deferred MCP startup work on disposal (#3911)

### What changed
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/mcp/service.ts` detaches connections before waiting for startup work within the deferred-dispose deadline. `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/mcp/startup-race.ts` tracks background connects even without a registrar.

### Why
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/mcp/service.ts` cleared the tracker while a catalog write could still hold its lock. `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/mcp/startup-race.ts` did not report deferred completion for unbound connects.

### Why an extension could not handle it
- The shutdown barrier in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/mcp/service.ts` and startup continuation in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/mcp/startup-race.ts` are owned by the MCP service.

### Expected merge conflict zones
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/mcp/service.ts`: `dispose`; `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/mcp/startup-race.ts`: `McpDeferredAttach` and `raceMcpStartupConnect`.
