## 2026-11-22 - Exhausted goal probes park on live channels (senpi#5325)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/goal/continuation.ts`: a monitor-delayed repetition or cap denial becomes `live-wait` while a wake source is active.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/goal/lifecycle-helpers.ts`: `live-wait` preserves the active goal without recording a continuation stop.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/goal/monitor-continuation.ts`: exhausted probes park without another timer; draining the last channel arms the normal guarded continuation, including direct-input admission holds.

### Why

- Unchanged backstop replies blocked goals while children or monitors were still running, freezing elapsed accounting. Parking stops those probes while keeping the goal active.
- A channel that ends without delivering still reaches the repetition or cap guard, so an exhausted goal cannot remain parked on a dead channel.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Admission, persisted stop decisions, and channel scheduling belong to the builtin goal extension.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/goal/continuation.ts`: verdict reasons and repetition/cap admission.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/goal/lifecycle-helpers.ts`: denial handling.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/goal/monitor-continuation.ts`: timer admission and wake-source draining.
