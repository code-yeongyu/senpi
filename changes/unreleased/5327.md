## 2026-11-01 - A goal waiting on a live channel is never blocked by its backstop re-checks (senpi#5325)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/goal/continuation.ts`: `evaluateGoalContinuation` takes `liveWakeSources`; on the `monitorDelayed` path with a live wake source, a re-check that would trip the repetition guard or the continuation cap is refused as the new `live-wait` reason instead of `repetition` / `cap`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/goal/lifecycle-helpers.ts`: `live-wait` records no stop and no block; the goal stays active.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/goal/monitor-continuation.ts`: the verdict input carries the live wake-source count; a `live-wait` refusal parks the goal (no further backstop), and the channel's drain still arms the drain fire, which runs the normal guards.

### Why

- While a child task or monitor was still running, the model answered each backstop re-check the same way ("still waiting"). The 3rd unchanged reply tripped the repetition guard and the 9th unchanged delivery the cap; both set the goal `blocked`, which stopped its elapsed timer. The child's later delivery turns did real work against the blocked goal until a user message reactivated it (senpi#5325).
- The guards keep their job: with no live channel they block as before, re-checks still stop at the same point, and a channel that ends without delivering lets the drain fire block an exhausted goal, so a dead channel cannot park a goal forever.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The continuation verdict and the backstop scheduling are internal to the builtin goal extension.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `evaluateGoalContinuation` guard order, the deny-reason switches, `#setWakeSourceCount` in `monitor-continuation.ts`.
