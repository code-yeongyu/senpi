## 2026-11-25 - The Windows Python kernel job keeps a stalled kernel's diagnostics (senpi#3600)

### What changed

- `.github/workflows/ci.yml`: the `python-kernel-windows` job installs `py-spy==0.4.4` before `Slow and hung bootstrap regressions`, and afterwards uploads `$RUNNER_TEMP/py-kernel-diagnostics` as the `py-kernel-stall-diagnostics` artifact (`if: always()`, `if-no-files-found: ignore`). The bundle is written by `packages/senpi-codemode/test/py-kernel/stall-diagnostics.ts` only when a live kernel makes no progress for 10 s.

### Why

- The job's cold-start stall (senpi#3600: no progress at `interpreter-launch` or `stdlib-imports`, or a 30 s test timeout) appears on main about once in a dozen runs and did not reproduce on demand. The next occurrence has to carry its own mechanism: the stage reached, the CPU samples, the thread wait reasons and `py-spy dump --native` stacks. The verdict and the timeouts are unchanged.

- This is the CI workflow. Extensions do not run in it.
