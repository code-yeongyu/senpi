## 2026-10-30 - Selected SIWC account lifecycle (part of #5003)

### What changed

- `packages/ai/src/providers/chatgpt-subscription.ts` routes reauthorization directly by the selected credential and dispatches revocation to the matching OAuth flow.

### Why

- Returning SIWC accounts must reuse their issued registration and cannot switch to legacy during reauthorization. Sign-out must reach the SIWC revocation hook.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Provider composition owns credential-route dispatch before terminal or RPC interaction.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- The ChatGPT subscription OAuth login, refresh and revoke object.

## 2026-10-30 - Shared SIWC account state and logout results (part of #5003)

### What changed

- `model-runtime.ts` forwards selected logout options and revocation results, synchronizes surviving credentials, and attributes actual stored-account selection per session.
- `credential-accounts.ts` and `credential-account-order.ts` share active and paused metadata. `credential-pool/classify.ts`, `state-store.ts` and `rotation-stream.ts` retain the SIWC usage-limit classification independently of the internal probe cooldown.
- `credential-pool/rotation-slots.ts` excludes signed-out registrations from selection. Single ChatGPT OAuth registrations also use the existing pool health path.

### Why

- An account must be shown active only after actual selection, including failover. A SIWC quota pause has no known reset time. Signing out one account must preserve its registration and signed-in siblings.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Runtime credential synchronization and revision-bound pool health are shared across terminal and RPC.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- Runtime login/logout and streaming attribution; pool health schema and failure/success writes; shared account summaries.

## 2026-10-30 - Reauthorize and sign out selected ChatGPT accounts (part of #5003)

### What changed

- `builtin/gpt-account.ts` adds `reauthorize <id>` and `signout <id>` through the shared model runtime. Listing renders actual active and paused states and the Usage link.

### Why

- A user can reconnect or sign out one registration without deleting it, and sees an explicit warning when remote revocation is not confirmed.

### Expected merge conflict zones

## 2026-11-05 - Reauthorizing a signed-out ChatGPT account restores it in place (part of #5003)

### What changed

- `packages/ai/src/auth/types.ts`: `LoginOptions.reauthorizeSlot` names the saved account that `reauthorize` selects.
- `packages/ai/src/auth/pool/slots.ts`: `appendLoginSlot` takes `reauthorizeSlot` and rewrites exactly that account. It keeps the name, label and source, clears `signedOut`, and moves the flat projection to it when it carried that account or when the whole credential was signed out.
- `packages/ai/src/auth/pool/slots.ts`: before it rewrites the account, the sign-in must be the same ChatGPT account. The route must match; SIWC must match issuer, subject and client ID; legacy must match the token identity, or the workspace once signed out. Otherwise `ReauthorizeAccountMismatchError` is thrown and nothing is written.

### Why

- A signed-out account holds blank tokens. Neither the token-identity match nor the sign-in takeover could find it, so reauthorizing it appended a duplicate `login-N`.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The credential pool's login merge is shared by every login entry point.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- `appendLoginSlot` and its options type.

## 2026-11-05 - Login forwards the reauthorized account name (part of #5003)

### What changed

- `packages/ai/src/models.ts`: `Models.login` passes `LoginOptions.reauthorizeSlot` to `appendLoginSlot`.
- `Models.login` rethrows `ReauthorizeAccountMismatchError` unwrapped, so the user sees why nothing changed.

### Why

- So a reauthorization restores the selected account instead of adding a copy.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `Models.login` owns the credential write for every provider.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- The `appendLoginSlot` call inside `Models.login`.
