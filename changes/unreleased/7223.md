## 2026-11-22 - Name the ledger hold at turn end (senpi#4014)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/goal/turn-end-liveness-report.ts` reads the pause records after the engine reports a hold.

### Why

- A user Stop and an inherited Stop were both reported as `unattended`.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The builtin goal extension collects this verdict; a second extension cannot change the reason it publishes.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the goal collector's `ledgerHold` input.
