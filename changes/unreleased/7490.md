## 2026-11-26 - One admission id space for prompts and deliveries (senpi#7479)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/admission-id-book.ts`: new; the per-session book of admitted ids keyed by the raw id, with the message digest and the first answer of a delivery.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/admit-external-message-command.ts`: a delivery under an id a prompt or another delivery already admitted answers `already_admitted` (same message) or the new `id_conflict` (different message), across origins.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/admit-external-message-wire.ts`: the reply type gains `{ kind: "id_conflict", deliveryId }`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/client-admission-intake.ts`: a prompt under an id a delivery already admitted is answered as that admission (same message) or refused `client_message_id_conflict` (different message); nothing runs.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/client-admission-record.ts`: admission records gain an optional `messageDigest`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/client-admissions.ts`: `ClientAdmissions.admittedUnder` reads a prompt admission without building a ledger.

### Why

- One logical send could run twice when it arrived once as a prompt and once as a delivery, or once from each of two devices, and a different message under an admitted delivery id was answered as a duplicate.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Admission and its replies are the RPC host's own commands; no extension sees them before the engine answers.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the duplicate checks at the top of `admitExternalMessage` and in `admitClientInput`.

### Fixed
