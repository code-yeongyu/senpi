## 2026-10-30 - A failing turn moves to at most one cold seat (oh-my-openagent#10304, senpi#5013)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/cold-seat-budget.ts` (new): per-session memory of the one cold seat the current turn used, keyed by a digest of the turn's sent messages, expiring after 1 h and capped at 256 sessions; `ColdSeedLimitError` names the cache-holding account, the cold account and when either is due back.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/failover.ts`: `runFailover` takes `warmAccount` and `turnKey`. With the warm seat in the pool and account fallback on: while the warm seat can serve, another attempt (a pin) spends nothing and a failure there falls through to it; once it cannot, the turn may use one cold seat (turn retries may run there again) and any other cold seat throws `ColdSeedLimitError` without dispatching. A cold seat's account-level failure is still blocked as before, then the turn stops instead of selecting another seat. Same-seat transient retries, the refused-1M retry and the superseded-token retry are unchanged. A served turn clears the record.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/auth-lane.ts`: passes `preferredAccount` as `warmAccount`, and `turnKey`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/session-stream.ts`: computes `turnKey` from the turn's sent-message hashes.

### Why

- Prompt caches are per organization and every subscription seat is its own organization, so each move off the seat that served the session re-sends the whole conversation uncached. One failing turn walked every seat in the pool, and each of the session's turn retries moved to the same cold seat again (6 seat changes for one turn with the default 5 retries).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Account selection and failover run inside this builtin provider's `runFailover`; no extension hook sits between attempts.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `FailoverOptions`, the attempt-selection head of the `runFailover` loop and its post-failure `onFailover` branch in `failover.ts`; the `runFailover` options in `queryWithAuthLane` (`auth-lane.ts`); `residentAuthLaneMessages` in `session-stream.ts`.

### Fixed
- Anthropic Subscription: a failing turn moves to at most one account other than the one holding the session's prompt cache. If that account fails too, the turn stops with a message naming both accounts and when the first is due back, instead of re-sending the whole conversation to every remaining account or on every retry ([#5013](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/senpi/pull/5013))
