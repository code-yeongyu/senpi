## 2026-10-30 - Prefer reported subscription headroom (senpi#5053)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/credential-pool/selection-order.ts`: add a pure preference for accounts whose live reported windows are below 95% usage.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/credential-pool/rotation-stream.ts`: apply the preference after normal account ordering and pins, retaining a session's replacement account.

### Why

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/credential-pool/rotation-stream.ts` selected an account already near its subscription limit even when another account had reported headroom.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/credential-pool/rotation-stream.ts` selects the credential before the provider request runs.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: imports and the `select` callback in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/credential-pool/rotation-stream.ts`; classification and block lifetimes are unchanged.

## 2026-10-30 - Apply headroom preference to Claude accounts (senpi#5053)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/affinity.ts`: use the shared preference after excluding blocked accounts, preserving pins and resident-account affinity.

### Why

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/affinity.ts` ignored the reported allowance when selecting the next account.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/affinity.ts` is the builtin provider's account-selection owner.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: imports and the return path of `selectUnblocked` in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/affinity.ts`.
