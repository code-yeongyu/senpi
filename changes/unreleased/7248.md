## 2026-11-22 - A full provider logout drops a credential left fully signed out (senpi#6644)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/model-runtime.ts`: `logoutProvider` deletes a credential that a full logout (no `slotId`) left fully signed out — no session material in the flat fields or any pooled account, on either ChatGPT sign-in route — instead of leaving the retained registration answering "stored" at every listing boundary; it retires that provider's slot health in the pool sidecar (as `login()` already does, #5043) and emits `accounts_changed` so subscribed pickers refresh. One-account sign-outs keep the #5974 contract: the provider and its signed-in siblings stay.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/auth-credential-values.ts`: new `fullySignedOutOAuthCredential` predicate; true only when no slot (flat or pooled) holds tokens that are present and not signed out.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/credential-pool/login-health.ts`: `retireSignedOutSlotHealth` retires pool health for every slot whose material a sign-out removed or replaced.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/auth-storage.ts`: `remove`, `removeSlot`, `has` and async `delete` now reach the legacy provider spellings that reads already fall back to (senpi#1989 read boundary), so a readable credential can never survive removal.

### Why

- `/logout` of the ChatGPT entry kept it listed in the `/logout` picker, across restarts: the provider's revoke-capable logout clears tokens but retains the registration entry (pi-ai's contract) and `models.logout` returns early, so `credentials.delete` never ran. The retained fully-signed-out entry kept answering "stored" for `AuthStorage.list()` (the picker source), `getAll()`, `has()` and `getAuthStatus()`, including for a fresh process over the same auth.json. The issue's legacy-key hypothesis is not the operative cause (the load seam migrates legacy keys with a backup write-back), but the exact-key mutation/lookup asymmetry made a legacy-keyed entry readable yet unremovable, and is closed here too.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Credential lifecycle (logout, deletion, pool health retirement) lives in `ModelRuntime` and `AuthStorage`; extensions can only reach it through the same public runtime the fix changes.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `model-runtime.ts` `logoutProvider`/`login` region; `login-health.ts` helpers; `auth-credential-values.ts` tail; `auth-storage.ts` `remove`/`removeSlot`/`has`/`delete`.

## 2026-11-22 - A fully signed-out logout deletes the registration it leaves behind (senpi#6644)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/model-runtime.ts` and `packages/coding-agent/src/core/auth-credential-values.ts`: a full logout deletes a credential with no signed-in material anywhere (the new `fullySignedOutOAuthCredential` predicate), retires that provider's slot health and emits `accounts_changed`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/auth-storage.ts`: `remove`, `removeSlot`, `has` and async `delete` reach the legacy provider spellings that reads already fall back to, so a readable credential can never survive removal.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/credential-pool/login-health.ts`: `retireSignedOutSlotHealth` retires pool health for every slot whose material a sign-out removed.

### Why

- The retained fully-signed-out entry kept answering "stored" for the listing, `has()` and `getAuthStatus()` boundaries, so the account stayed in the `/logout` picker across restarts; a legacy-keyed entry was readable yet unremovable.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Credential lifecycle and storage live in `ModelRuntime` and `AuthStorage`, below the extension surface.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `auth-storage.ts` provider-key fallback and `model-runtime.ts` logout path.
