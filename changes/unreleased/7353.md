## 2026-11-22 - The catch-up scrollback replay skips ESC[3J on Windows (senpi#3926)

### What changed

- `packages/tui/src/tui.ts`: `renderScrollbackReplay` emits the `ESC[3J` scrollback clear only when `process.platform !== "win32"`, the same guard `fullRender` already applies.

### Why

- On ConPTY `ESC[3J` makes the host re-emit the whole buffer; the key-press catch-up replay after a held turn added that re-emit on top of its own full-history rewrite, contributing to the Windows repaint freeze in senpi#3926.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The scrollback replay is internal to the TUI render loop; no extension hook sees or filters the bytes it writes.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the first lines of `renderScrollbackReplay` in `tui.ts`.
