### Fixed
- The kill guard refuses a negative `kill` target before `--` that procps-ng `kill` reads by its first digit: `kill -9 -1429035` signalled every process (`-1`) while the guard judged it as process group 1429035, and `kill -9 -2429035` signals group 2. It now refuses these (R3 for a first digit of 1, R4 otherwise) in the judge, the sh fast path and the bash `kill` wrapper, and points at `kill -9 -- -<pgid>` ([#6668](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/senpi/pull/6668))
