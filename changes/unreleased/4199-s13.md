## 2026-10-27 - Fence, retire and tombstone abandoned reservation generations (desktop#4199)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/endpoint-reservation-store.ts`: moves to schema 5. `retire` retires a fenced (`cancelling`) generation at the revision its census was taken at (`revision_changed` otherwise), only with a proof (`presence_released` or `process_gone`) for exactly the reserving owner until its `released` commit and every granted role without one (`discharge_incomplete` otherwise), and persists the discharges with the fenced revision. A retired generation refuses every later claim, offer, read of an offer, grant, serving commit, fence and retirement with `generation_retired`. `reserve` refuses a second generation for an endpoint while an earlier one is not retired (`generation_active`). `activeGenerations` and `roleRows` list what recovery reads.

### Why

- Design 4.2a, "Crash, cancellation and commit" and "Recovery transaction order" steps 2, 4 and 5: cancellation is a durable fence, retirement needs every recorded authority entry discharged and the fenced revision revalidated, the census result is persisted with the fenced generation, tombstones keep delayed actors from reviving a retired generation, and exactly one successor is admitted.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The reservation store runs before extensions load and owns the endpoint's launch admission.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/endpoint-reservation-store.ts`: schema SQL, the rejection union, `reserve`, `cancel` and the row mapping.
