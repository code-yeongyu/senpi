## 2026-11-15 - The active set and pending messages refuse after a call ended (senpi#6754)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/loop-hook-call.ts`: after the call ended, `setActiveTools` and `hasPendingMessages` throw `LoopHookCallEndedError` instead of dropping the change or answering from stale effects; `writer()` returns the call's facts, so a late `pi.setActiveTools` reaches the call.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/loop-hook-session.ts`: `LoopHookCallEndedError`'s doc lists both refusals.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/loop-hook-effects.ts`: `effectsReply`'s doc says a throwing dispatch builds no reply and its collected effects, taken-over late messages included, are discarded with the error.

### Why

- Review L1/L2 on #6790: a late active-set change was silently lost, and the reply builder's doc read as if every path sealed at reply build.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The call's lifecycle belongs to the hand engine.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `hasPendingMessages` and `setActiveTools` members in `createLoopHookCall`.

## 2026-11-15 - A late pi.setActiveTools reaches its call (senpi#6754)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/runner.ts`: a runner without a session routes `pi.setActiveTools` through the call's writer (`loopHookWrite`, now typed as the call's facts), as `appendEntry`/`sendUserMessage` already are; outside any call it stays a no-op.

### Why

- Routed through `current()`, a late `pi.setActiveTools` from a call's work was a silent no-op; through the writer it reaches the call, which refuses it (review L1 on #6790).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `pi.setActiveTools` is bound by the runner.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `bindSessionlessFacts` in `runner.ts`.
