## 2026-11-19 - A graceful close drains the turn and leaves a clean-stop record (oh-my-openagent#14313, senpi#7046)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/agent-session.ts`: `holdForClose()` / `releaseCloseHold()` add and release the `unattended` engine-turn hold keyed `closing` (release cause `session_close`). `recordClosedOnRequest(record)` appends the `session-closed` custom entry and sets `closedOnRequest`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/agent-session-runtime.ts`: `dispose()` emits `session_shutdown { reason: "quit", closed: true }` when the session was closed on request.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/session-closed.ts` (new): the `session-closed` entry type, its record shape and the `closing` hold key.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/engine-turn-hold.ts`: `HoldReleaseCause` gains `session_close`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/external-admission.ts`: `closed` reads why admission is closed.

### Why

- oh-my-openagent#14313. A busy or terminal-hosted session could not be closed without stopping its turn, and a stop left work that a reopen restored and an interrupted run that held peer deliveries. The clean-stop record and the `closed` shutdown let extensions stop that work for good.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The engine-turn hold, the shutdown event and the session runtime's dispose are core; no extension sees the close request.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `holdAutomaticTurns` neighbourhood and the private field list in `agent-session.ts`; the `session_shutdown` emit in `AgentSessionRuntime.dispose`.

## 2026-11-19 - `session_shutdown.closed` (oh-my-openagent#14313, senpi#7046)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/types.ts`: `SessionShutdownEvent` gains `closed?: true`, set when a graceful close ended the session on request. The terminal builtin then records no monitor or background job to restore, loops end `stopped`, and an active goal is paused.

### Why

- oh-my-openagent#14313: a session closed on purpose must reopen with nothing restored and nothing held.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The field is part of the public event contract that every extension's shutdown handler reads.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `SessionShutdownEvent` interface.

## 2026-11-19 - A terminal closes its own session gracefully on request (oh-my-openagent#14313, senpi#7046)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/interactive/interactive-mode.ts`: the control surface's `closeSession` runs the normal quit path after the close's answer is sent.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/interactive/session-control-close.ts` (new): `unload_session { graceful: true }` on the control endpoint. It refuses while the composer holds unsent text (before the fence and again after the drain), drains like a host, answers, and then asks the terminal to exit.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/interactive/session-control-commands.ts`: lists `unload_session` and the `unload_graceful` capability, and routes the command.

### Why

- oh-my-openagent#14313: moving or closing a terminal-hosted session needed a kill or TERM, which reopened as an interrupted run.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The control endpoint's command set and the terminal's quit path are the interactive mode's own.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `sessionControlContext()` in `interactive-mode.ts`.

## 2026-11-19 - A graceful close leaves nothing to restore (oh-my-openagent#14313, senpi#7046)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/terminal/terminal-manifest.ts`: `recordClosed()` drops every monitor, background job and pending checkpoint, and the manifest file goes away with the last entry.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/terminal/restore-session.ts`, `extension.ts`: a `session_shutdown` with `closed: true` records `recordClosed()` instead of `recordShutdown()`.

### Why

- oh-my-openagent#14313 (and senpi#7033's shape): a restored monitor that fires on reopen opens a run the restore sweep then marks interrupted. A session closed on purpose has nothing to restore.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- This is the terminal builtin's own manifest.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `stopPersistence` in `restore-session.ts`.

### Added
- `unload_session { graceful: true, waitMs }` closes a session without interrupting it. New deliveries are refused `session_closing`, queued input is handed back unread, the running turn ends on its own (bounded), and monitors, loops and the goal stop cleanly. A reopen finds no interrupted run and no hold. A drain that outlives `waitMs` is answered typed, and the session stays open. A terminal's control endpoint takes it too and then exits by itself. Capability `unload_graceful` ([#7046](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/senpi/pull/7046))
