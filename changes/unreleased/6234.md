## 2026-11-08 - Device-proxy egress and the egress_call ledger (custody CK9, senpi#6234)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/egress/device-proxy.ts` (new): `createDeviceProxyEgress`, the device route of hosted model-key custody for an admitted loop step: the re-admission replay rules (committed step: no dial; cancelled step: aborted message; ended success: replay without device contact or hooks; ended error: closed and redialed with the stored request; dispatched: result query, re-attach or supersede), byte-for-byte reissue with `step.reset`, `session/variants` on a definition mismatch, the callback-stream user choice, and claim refusal handling (renew, key backoff, error row).
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/egress/device-proxy-call.ts` (new): follows one call: `hooked` stored before later frames, the terminal event pushed only after the row is durably `ended`, then `ack`; liveness probe, offline wait after the resume window, `cancel{budget}` after one idle plus one resume window.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/egress/device-proxy-request.ts` (new): wire-option allowlist (options hygiene) and the 4.5 request-hook rules.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/egress/device-proxy-waits.ts` (new): tracked waits bracketed by `onWait`/`onWaitEnd`, registry-key backoff, and the live-digest board that wakes only on a changed digest.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/egress/device-proxy-ports.ts` (new): the ports the hosted wrapper supplies.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/egress/ledger.ts`, `ledger-schema.ts` (new): the `egress_call` ledger: rows, the admitted request in 96 KiB chunks, W4/W4'/ended/supersede/W14 writes through the host's admitted mutation, and `closeCallWrites` for the step commit.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/egress/wire.ts`: the `env` comment names the adapters that derive the dial target from `env`.

### Why

Hosted brains route a provider through the user's own device as an add-on to sealed custody; the device route must replay, reissue and pause exactly once per step without falling back silently (#5847).

### Why an extension could not handle it

The device route is a provider-pipeline layer below every extension hook, and its ledger writes are part of the hosted session's durable step commit.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- None: every file is new and fork-only except a comment in the fork-only `wire.ts`.
