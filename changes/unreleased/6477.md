## 2026-11-12 - A failed auto-reap stops being promised, and a parked reap_failed is still delivered (senpi#6252)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/terminal/background-age.ts`: `BackgroundAgeSchedule.clearAutoReap` forgets a session's auto-reap age; milestones keep their schedule.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/terminal/session-bundle.ts`: `notifyBackgroundReapFailed` clears the subject's reap age after dispatching the failure notice, so later `running` events carry `autoReapMinutes: null`. A `reap_failed` raised while the bundle is parked is kept in a separate `#parkedReapFailed` map and flushed on `bind` before the newest parked age notice; an exit while parked drops it, and `teardown` clears it.

### Why

- senpi#6252: after a `reap_failed`, later `running` notices still carried the auto-reap age and promised a reap that will not happen, contradicting the documented contract that `autoReapMinutes` is null "when nothing will stop it"; and a `reap_failed` raised while the bundle is parked was overwritten by the next `running` milestone, so the one-shot failure notice and its reason never reached the agent or RPC.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The terminal builtin owns the background sessions, their bundle and their age schedule; this is fork-only code.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `notifyBackgroundReapFailed`/`bind`/`teardown` in `session-bundle.ts`, `BackgroundAgeSchedule` in `background-age.ts`.

### Fixed

- After a failed auto-reap, later background age notices no longer promise another auto-reap; a failure raised while the terminal bundle is parked is still delivered on rebind ([#6477](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/senpi/pull/6477)).
