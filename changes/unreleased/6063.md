## 2026-11-05 - The rule-activation entry records which detector fired (senpi#6063)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/ttsr/index.ts`: the stream-remediation path passes the detector match (`reason`, `detail`) and the stream kind into the `rule-activation` entry it appends. `rule-activation/types.ts` carries them as optional `stream`, `reason` and `detail` fields; a malformed optional field is dropped and never invalidates the entry. `rule-activation/renderer.ts` shows them when the entry is expanded.
- The stop after a second hit in one turn is unchanged.

### Why

- A `collapse-repetition` hit persisted only owner, rules and remediation, so repeated hits on one agent could not be judged as a real runaway loop or a false positive. The detector already computes the mechanism, counts and a capped sample.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The entry is written by the built-in TTSR extension from the match it alone holds; nothing outside it sees the detector result.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `recordInjection` and the `pendingRemediation` block in `ttsr/index.ts`; senpi#4623 edits the stop block of the same file.
