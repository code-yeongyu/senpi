## 2026-10-30 - A ChatGPT re-login replaces the saved account instead of appending a copy (senpi#5009)

### What changed

- `packages/ai/src/auth/types.ts`: `OAuthAuth.accountIdentity?(credential)` (optional, synchronous): the signed-in account a credential's own material belongs to, or `undefined`.
- `packages/ai/src/auth/helpers.ts`: `lazyOAuth` passes `accountIdentity` through without loading the flow module.
- `packages/ai/src/auth/pool/slots.ts`: `appendLoginSlot` takes `{ accountIdentity }`. A flat OAuth login whose identity matches saved accounts rewrites the first match in place (`replaceSameAccount`), keeping its name, label, source and position and dropping stale block fields. Later copies of the same account are removed, a pin/order naming one moves to the kept name, and the flat projection is re-pointed only when it carried one of them. A lone flat credential of the same account is overwritten. No identity, or no match, appends as before.

### Why

- oh-my-openagent#9633, senpi#4086: every re-login appended `login-N` and kept the old slot. Once the provider invalidated that token, session affinity kept ranking the dead slot first, and `/login` could not recover the session.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The login write step is the shared persistence path inside `Models.login` and the auth store; no hook runs between the provider's login result and the write.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the flat-login tail of `appendLoginSlot` and the `OAuthAuth` interface; `lazyOAuth`'s returned object.

## 2026-10-30 - Models.login passes the provider's account identity to the login write (senpi#5009)

### What changed

- `packages/ai/src/models.ts`: `Models.login` passes `provider.auth.oauth?.accountIdentity` to `appendLoginSlot`, so a re-login of a saved account replaces it.

### Why

- oh-my-openagent#9633: the interactive `/login` path writes through `Models.login`.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The write happens inside `Models.login` under the credential lock.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `appendLoginSlot` call in `Models.login`.

## 2026-10-30 - extractChatGptSubscriptionIdentity (senpi#5009)

### What changed

- `packages/ai/src/utils/chatgpt-subscription-auth.ts`: `extractChatGptSubscriptionIdentity(token)` returns the per-member `chatgpt_account_user_id`, or `chatgpt_user_id`/`user_id` scoped to `chatgpt_account_id`; `chatgpt_account_id` alone is never an identity (Team members share it). `extractChatGptSubscriptionAccountId` shares the same claim reader. The claim reading follows senpi#2048.

### Why

- oh-my-openagent#9633: tell one saved ChatGPT account from another, including slots stored by older builds.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Shared, browser-safe token helper used by the provider definition.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the body of `extractChatGptSubscriptionAccountId`.

## 2026-10-30 - AuthStorage.set replaces a re-logged-in saved account (senpi#5009)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/auth-storage.ts`: `set` passes the provider's `accountIdentity` to `appendLoginSlot`; `login` and `set` share a private `oauthAuth(providerId)` lookup.

### Why

- oh-my-openagent#9633: RPC OAuth login writes through `AuthStorage.set`.
