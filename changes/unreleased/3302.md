## 2026-10-29 - Recover fallback heads on automatic wakes and chain exhaustion (senpi#3302)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/agent-session.ts`: share turn-boundary fallback restoration, circuit routing and spent-account preparation between prompts and admitted idle custom wakes. Preserve lifecycle restoration, recheck cancellation before authentication, and retain cancelled or refused wakes through the existing queue contract.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/retry-fallback/controller.ts`: consider the original primary once after tail candidates are exhausted, excluding spent heads and retaining circuit probes, request-refusal filters, network-failure handling and per-rung hard-error cooldowns. Preserve pins, manual selections, the never policy and thinking overrides.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/retry-fallback/controller-types.ts`: expose the revert policy to candidate selection.

### Why

- Automatic wakes skipped primary recovery and spent-account routing. A chain tail could also fail without trying a primary that had recovered during the turn.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Core owns authentication and provider admission ordering, fallback state, candidate selection and circuit probe accounting.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- HIGH: prompt preparation and idle custom-trigger admission in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/agent-session.ts`.
- HIGH: primary restoration and `nextCandidate` in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/retry-fallback/controller.ts`.
- LOW: fallback settings in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/retry-fallback/controller-types.ts`.

### Fixed
- Automatic wakes now recover the primary model before provider admission, and an exhausted fallback tail can try the original primary once without looping. Billing and refusal pins, manual selections, the never policy, reset-window recovery, network-failure handling and per-rung hard-error cooldowns remain authoritative ([#3302](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/senpi/pull/3302)).
