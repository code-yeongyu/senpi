## 2026-10-29 - Recover fallback heads on automatic wakes and chain exhaustion (senpi#3302)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/agent-session.ts`: an admitted idle custom wake (a monitor notification, a goal continuation, a chat-bridge wake) runs a narrow turn-boundary fallback preparation before provider admission, and only when there is fallback state to act on, so wakes without it keep the exact existing admission ordering. The preparation restores the recovered primary on the `"continuation"` boundary — a network-blip hold survives the wake, matching the other automatic continuations, and the next real prompt boundary clears it — then reroutes off a provider the session knows is spent, refusing the wake before admission when no candidate remains. A wake cancelled during the awaited preparation is retained through the existing queue contract. The prompt path keeps its own inline preparation unchanged.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/retry-fallback/controller.ts`: when the last chain entry is exhausted, consider the chain's original head once, but only once it has actually recovered: its circuit closed (or free for this session to claim as the half-open probe) and no session cooldown — a Retry-After or a usage-limit reset window — still holding it. A circuit that is still open does not wrap and never becomes an all-open probe; spent heads, billing and refusal pins, the never policy, manual selections and thinking overrides never wrap.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/retry-fallback/controller-types.ts`: expose the revert policy to candidate selection.

### Why

- Automatic wakes skipped primary recovery and spent-account routing. A chain tail could also fail without trying a primary that had recovered during the turn.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Core owns authentication and provider admission ordering, fallback state, candidate selection and circuit probe accounting.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- HIGH: idle custom-trigger admission in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/agent-session.ts` (the prompt-boundary preparation stays inline).
- HIGH: primary restoration and `nextCandidate` in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/retry-fallback/controller.ts`.
- LOW: fallback settings in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/retry-fallback/controller-types.ts`.

### Fixed
- Automatic wakes now recover the primary model before provider admission (a network-blip hold survives the wake), and an exhausted fallback tail can return to the original primary once it has actually recovered, without looping. Billing and refusal pins, manual selections, the never policy, reset-window recovery, network-failure handling and per-rung hard-error cooldowns remain authoritative ([#3302](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/senpi/pull/3302)).
