## 2026-11-05 - A removed owner record drops the last read (senpi#6040)

### What changed

- `packages/agent/src/execution/remote-epoch.ts`: the fence's copy of a scope's owner forgets the last record read once `ownerOf` answers `undefined`.

### Why

With record reads (#6030), a removed record still let the last-read owner raise and be acked, breaking "a superseded owner cannot raise" (senpi#6040).

### Why an extension could not handle it

The decision is inside the hand's epoch fence.

### Expected merge conflict zones

`remote-epoch.ts` owner-copy helper.
