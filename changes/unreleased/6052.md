### Fixed
- A scope whose owner record was removed no longer lets the owner the hand last read raise its epoch floor (senpi#6040, invariant I5 of #6030). The fence keeps the newest record read per scope, a removal included, and decides a raise at its turn on that newest read (its own or a later one, unless the copy names a higher epoch): a raise whose own read is older than a read naming a new owner, or finding the record removed, is refused `peer_unauthorized`. A missing copy, or a removal the newest read found, names no owner: a raise arriving then reads the record again, and a raise already on the admission line with no read of its own is refused rather than decided on a guess.

## 2026-11-05 - A removed owner record ends the last-read owner's raises (senpi#6040)

### What changed

- `packages/agent/src/execution/remote-epoch.ts`: the fence records the newest record read per scope, ordered by read sequence and including a read that found the record removed; a raise is decided at its turn on the newest read, and a missing copy or a recorded removal names no owner until a fresh read names one.

### Why

With record reads (#6030), a removed record still let the last-read owner raise and be acked, and a raise decided on its own older read could ack an owner a later read had already superseded, breaking "a superseded owner cannot raise" (senpi#6040).

### Why an extension could not handle it

The decision is inside the hand's epoch fence.

### Expected merge conflict zones

`remote-epoch.ts` record-read helpers (`refreshed`, `copyOf`, `ownerAtTurn`).
