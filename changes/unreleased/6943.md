## 2026-11-18 - The over-budget sentence leads a hand result that has no text part (senpi#6943)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/html-render/hand-page-budget.ts`: when a hand's over-budget show_html_page result has no text part at all, `applied` puts the over-budget sentence first. A result whose page text a handler rewrote still gets it appended.

### Why

- senpi#6939. The older-hand fallback appended the sentence after the result's other parts, while the brain's rule before the marker put it first.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The brain-side page budget is this builtin's own code.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `applied` in `html-render/hand-page-budget.ts`.
