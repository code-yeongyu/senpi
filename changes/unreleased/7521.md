## 2026-11-26 - Record external delivery custody before returning a receipt (senpi#7513)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/agent-session.ts` reserves the payload in work custody before launching or enqueueing it. Each held brief has its own notice. Abort retains an unwritten admission; an explicit handback cancels it.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/session-manager.ts` keeps files containing external admission records even before the first conversation entry.

### Why

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/agent-session.ts` used to acknowledge a launch or queue insertion without requiring a replayable payload. The sender could discard its copy before the receiver wrote anything.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/session-manager.ts` treated all work custody as disposable startup bookkeeping.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/agent-session.ts` owns the admission decision, native queues, and lifecycle replay.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/session-manager.ts` decides which session files survive header-only cleanup.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/agent-session.ts`: external admission wiring, custom-turn cancellation, and held-notice release.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/session-manager.ts`: `isDisposableEntry`.

## 2026-11-26 - Refuse an external delivery when custody cannot be persisted (senpi#7513)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/admit-external-message-command.ts` returns `delivery_not_persisted` when the session cannot retain the payload.

### Why

- A positive receipt tells the remote sender it can discard its copy.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The host and terminal control endpoint share this engine-owned RPC receipt.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/admit-external-message-command.ts`: admission result mapping.
