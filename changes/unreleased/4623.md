## 2026-10-28 - DSML in thinking resamples, a stop never blocks the next person turn, and TTSR has a setting (senpi#4621)

### What changed

- `detectors/token-grammar.ts`: the candidate parser reads DeepSeek special tokens with a fullwidth bar (`<｜end▁of▁sentence｜>`, names may carry `▁`) and DSML tool-call tags with attributes, opening or closing (`<｜DSML｜invoke name="...">`, `</｜DSML｜parameter>`, ASCII-bar form too), as a `dsml` token family.
- `detectors/control-leak.ts`: one DSML tag outside a quoted or fenced example is a control-token leak, so the reply is resampled (`provider-error`) instead of being imitated into a `collapse-repetition` loop.
- `follow-up-limit.ts`: the one-correction episode also ends at a person turn recorded as a hidden custom message (`.` manual continue, continue-from-leaf, stop-resume). `ttsr-loop-stopped` carries a typed `reason: "repeated-after-correction"` and is written and shown once per stop (`loopStopAlreadyReported`).
- `settings.ts` (new), `index.ts`, `commands.ts`: `ttsr.enabled` and `ttsr.disabledRules` in settings.json turn rules off like `--ttsr-disabled` / `--ttsr-rules-disabled`; `/ttsr` names which one did. Turning TTSR off also turns off the `repetitive-turns` lane, which runs outside the rule manager.
- `follow-up-limit.ts`: an interrupted-run recovery (`session-resumed` with `resumeCause: "interrupted"`) does not renew the correction, matching `resetsEngineTurnHistory`.

### Why

- On DeepSeek V4.1 Flash at thinking `max`, DSML envelopes in reasoning were not recognised as leaked control tokens, were kept and replayed, and the model imitated them until `collapse-repetition` fired; after its one correction every `.` continue was stopped at its first hit with no correction, and the desktop app, which starts the runtime with fixed arguments, had no way to turn TTSR off (senpi#4621, reported in detail by @asmond-lab in oh-my-openagent#11610).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The detectors, the follow-up bound and the flag/setting resolution are internal to the built-in ttsr extension, and a user extension cannot override its flag defaults (the report tried).

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `ttsr/index.ts` `ensureInitialized` and the `agent_settled` stop branch; `follow-up-limit.ts` (stacked on senpi#4538).

## 2026-10-28 - openai-completions drops DeepSeek DSML envelopes from reasoning (senpi#4621)

### What changed

- `packages/ai/src/utils/dsml-envelope.ts` (new): `DsmlEnvelopeFilter` removes DSML tool-call envelopes from a reasoning stream split at any point and keeps all other text; the text of an envelope that never closes is released as reasoning.
- `openai-completions.ts`: reasoning deltas pass through the filter before they are stored in the thinking block or emitted as `thinking_delta`; replayed thinking is stripped too, so sessions written before the fix stop feeding envelopes back to the model. Replayed `reasoning_details` (OpenRouter) are stripped as well: unsigned `reasoning.text` and every `reasoning.summary`; a signed `reasoning.text` is sent verbatim because the provider verifies the signature over that text.

### Why

- DeepSeek V4.1 Flash at thinking `max` writes DSML envelopes into `reasoning_content`; kept, they were shown, stored and replayed, and the model imitated them until the reply collapsed (senpi#4621, reported by @asmond-lab in oh-my-openagent#11610).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Thinking deltas are produced inside the provider stream; an extension sees them only after they are shown and stored.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the reasoning-delta branch and `finishBlock` thinking case in `openai-completions.ts`.

## 2026-10-28 - DsmlEnvelopeFilter removes DeepSeek DSML envelopes from reasoning text (senpi#4621)

### What changed

- `dsml-envelope.ts` (new): `DsmlEnvelopeFilter` (streaming, any split) and `stripDsmlEnvelopes` (complete text) remove DSML tool-call envelopes by tag depth. An envelope confirmed as tool syntax (a DSML tag nested in another) is payload and is dropped however long it runs. Text after a lone opening tag at depth 0 is held until the tag closes (dropped as payload) or the stream ends (released as reasoning, the tag dropped), so prose that quotes a tag is kept; a lone `parameter` tag is held without limit, any other lone tag is released past 4096 chars. An opening tag that never completes is released as text.

### Why

- DeepSeek V4.1 Flash writes DSML envelopes into its reasoning stream (senpi#4621, reported by @asmond-lab in oh-my-openagent#11610).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The filter runs inside the provider stream, before any extension sees the delta.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- NONE: new file.

## 2026-10-28 - Settings carry a `ttsr` section (senpi#4621)

### What changed

- `settings-shapes.ts` / `settings-manager.ts`: `Settings.ttsr?: TtsrSettingsConfig` (`enabled`, `disabledRules`), read by the built-in ttsr extension through `getSettings()`.

### Why

- The desktop app starts the runtime with fixed arguments, so `--ttsr-disabled` could not be passed and a user had no way to turn stream rules off (senpi#4621).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- A user extension cannot change the built-in extension's flag defaults; the report tried and the built-in re-registration won.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `Settings` interface next to `engineTurns`.
