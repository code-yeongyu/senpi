## 2026-11-17 - show_html_page runs on a hand (senpi#6920)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/html-render/tool.ts`: on a runtime without a session (a hand in a brain/hand split), `show_html_page` no longer reads loop-side context members. The page is held to `MAX_HANDED_PAGE_CHARACTERS` alone, because the transcript it joins is the brain's, and the prompt surface comes from the environment.

### Why

- senpi#6919. Every `show_html_page` call on a real `--hand` failed with "getSystemPromptOptions is loop-side and not bound". Once a hand reported its calls as model-issued, the call would also have failed on the missing `ctx.sessionManager`.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `show_html_page` is this builtin's own code.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `hostRendersPage` and the `inTranscript` line of `execute` in `html-render/tool.ts`.

## 2026-11-17 - ExtensionToolContext.issuedByModel on hands (senpi#6920)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/runner.ts`: `bindSessionlessFacts` accepts `isModelIssuedToolCall`, which backs `ctx.issuedByModel` on a runtime without a session.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/types.ts`: the `issuedByModel` contract names the hand case. On a hand, a call the brain's agent loop dispatched counts as issued by the model.

### Why

- senpi#6919 L2. Only `bindCore` provided the flag, so a hand reported every call as `false`, although the brain's model issued it.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The origin of a hand's call is known only to the runtime that runs it.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `bindSessionlessFacts` parameter list and the head of its body in `runner.ts`; the `issuedByModel` doc comment in `types.ts`.

## 2026-11-17 - Cursor exec-frame calls are released on every end (senpi#6920)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/agent-session.ts`: the session preflight only marks an exec-bridge call (`execBridge`). The new `releaseExecBridgeToolCall` drops it, and the session no longer drops it on a preflight block or throw or in `emitExecBridgeToolResult`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/cursor-exec-bridge.ts`: `executeTool` reports the end of every call that reached the prepared pipeline from one `finally` (`settleToolCall`). That includes a block after a passing preflight (`NO_ACTIVE_RUN`) and an executor abort, which skip `afterToolCall`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/cursor-exec-bridge-session.ts`: wires `settleToolCall` to the session's `releaseExecBridgeToolCall`.

### Why

- senpi#6919 L1. A call blocked after its preflight passed kept its id in `_execBridgeToolCalls` for the life of the session.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The bridge's call lifecycle and the session's origin set are core state.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the head of `preflightToolCall`, `releaseExecBridgeToolCall` and `emitExecBridgeToolResult` in `agent-session.ts`; the `host.execute` call in `cursor-exec-bridge.ts`.
