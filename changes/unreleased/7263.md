## 2026-11-22 - Concurrent fallback loggers preserve new events (senpi#6351)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/retry-fallback/log.ts` shares pending first-start cleanup for loggers writing to the same resolved log path.

### Why

- A second logger could delete the first logger's freshly flushed lines while retiring older files.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Session construction starts the built-in logger; extensions do not control its filesystem cleanup or queue.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: initialization in `packages/coding-agent/src/core/retry-fallback/log.ts`. The existing marker, per-logger queues and redaction still apply.
