## 2026-11-22 - Long ask-user questions stay readable in the widget and the fullscreen dock (senpi#4331)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/interactive/components/ask-user-async-widget.ts`: the pending-question widget wraps the question to the terminal width with `wrapTextWithAnsi` instead of cutting it with an ellipsis; every wrapped row keeps the click-to-expand hit.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/interactive/chat-viewport.ts`: the fullscreen input dock is wrapped in a `ScrollView` (follows its end, chains unused wheel deltas to the transcript, no scrollbar), so a dock taller than the screen scrolls instead of clipping its rows.

### Why

- A long ask-user question ended in an ellipsis in the widget, and in fullscreen mode the expanded question overlay lost its bottom rows (question end, options, hints) when it was taller than the screen, with no way to reach them (senpi#4331).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The widget and the fullscreen dock layout are core interactive-mode rendering; extensions cannot replace how the built-in ask-user widget renders or how the dock is laid out.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `createChatViewport` return block in `chat-viewport.ts`; the question-row block of `AskUserAsyncWidget.render` in `ask-user-async-widget.ts`.
