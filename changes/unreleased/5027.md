## 2026-10-30 - An unsettled probe hands its lease back; a leased slot names its retry time (senpi#4094)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/credential-pool/failover.ts`: `runCredentialFailover` takes an optional `releaseSlot(slot)`, run once in a `finally` when the request leaves a selected slot with neither a success nor a persisted block (an abort, a request-scoped failure, exhausted same-slot retries, the caller stopping the stream).
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/credential-pool/rotation-stream.ts`: `releaseSlot` hands back the half-open probe lease the attempt held, matched by lease id.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/credential-pool/rotation-slots.ts`: `releaseProbeLease` is shared with the model-block lease release. A slot whose probe another request holds stays listed, blocked until that lease expires with reason `probe_in_flight`, instead of being dropped.

### Why

- A probe that ended without a verdict kept its 30 s lease, and every other caller dropped the slot, so a one-slot pool refused with "No credential slots available" and no retry time.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The probe lease and the slot listing are internal to the credential pool engine.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `try`/`finally` around the attempt loop in `runCredentialFailover`; the lease branches of `listRotationSlots` and `listEnvRotationSlots`.
