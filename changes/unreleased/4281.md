## 2026-10-26 - A handoff drain hands a session over only by a strict transfer (code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent#10091, senpi#4281)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-transfer-quiescence.ts` (new): the one quiet check `release_session` and the handoff drain share; a missing or throwing activity signal is `unknown`, never quiet.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-drain.ts`: drain verdicts hold every session that is not proven quiet (`held` with its blockers): prompts in preflight, queued input and admitted deliveries hold it, and so does unreadable activity.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-transfer-seal.ts` (new), `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-router-admission.ts` (new, out of `session-router-routing.ts`): a session is sealed for its transfer in the step that judged it quiet. New work is answered `session_closing` with `errorData { reason: "handoff_transfer", transfer_id }`; reads, aborts, question answers and the session's lifecycle commands pass, and an explicit close wins.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-transfer-park.ts` (new), `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-router-occupancy.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-router-close.ts`: a retained session's park record is written durably (with its `transferId`) before the teardown, which follows one more quiet check in the same synchronous step; a record that cannot be written keeps the session open and owned here, retried with a backoff.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-handover-parked.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-state-json.ts`: park records are synced (file and directory, `writeFileDurably`) and removed only for the transfer that wrote them.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-reservations.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-generations.ts`: claims carry `strict_transfer: true` and stand while their owner lives; the claim reading and owner verdicts moved into `host-generations.ts` (re-exported), keeping the supervisor's module budget.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-release.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-held.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-router-context.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-handover-wire.ts`: the shared predicate, the exported read/control set, the seal and retry state, and the drain verdict's new shape.

### Why

- The drain parked sessions whose activity could not be read, skipped prompts in preflight, tore a retained session down after its park record failed to write, and a successor could claim a file a superseded generation was still writing (code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent#10091 design, revision 2).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The drain, the router's admission and the cross-generation claims are host internals no extension sees.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `session-router-occupancy.ts` `evictIdleSession`, `session-router-routing.ts` `handle`, `host-reservations.ts` and `host-generations.ts` (claim reading moved between them).
