## 2026-11-19 - Preserve fullscreen Kitty images on redraw (senpi#6968)

- `packages/tui/src/tui-alt-screen.ts`: redraw Kitty placements after text and invalidate images when a covered row changes.

### Why

- `packages/tui/src/tui-alt-screen.ts`: later row writes could erase parts of a multi-row Kitty image.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `packages/tui/src/tui-alt-screen.ts`: fullscreen frame ordering and image invalidation are owned by the renderer.
