## 2026-10-17 - Fall back to primary account slot in mergeRefreshed when desynced (senpi#3407)

### What changed

- `packages/ai/src/auth/pool/slots.ts`: In `mergeRefreshed`, fall back to `current.accounts[0]` when neither `access` nor `refresh` match the top-level credentials, and in `mergeRefreshedSlot`, treat the primary slot (`current.accounts[0]`) as mirroring the flat projection.

### Why

When an external write or manual edit causes the top-level OAuth credentials in `auth.json` to desync from `accounts[0]`, `mergeRefreshed` failed to identify the target slot, silently leaving the expired tokens in `accounts[0]` while only rotating the top-level fields.

### Why an extension could not handle it

`mergeRefreshed` and `mergeRefreshedSlot` are internal pool mutation functions inside `packages/ai/src/auth/pool/slots.ts` executed during credential storage updates.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `mergeRefreshed` and `mergeRefreshedSlot` in `packages/ai/src/auth/pool/slots.ts`.

### Fixed
- In credential pool rotation, `mergeRefreshed` now falls back to updating the primary account slot (`accounts[0]`) when top-level OAuth credentials are desynchronized from the pool ([#3407](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/senpi/issues/3407)).
