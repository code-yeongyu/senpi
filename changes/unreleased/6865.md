## 2026-11-16 - The temp guard no longer blames a job for a concurrent job's scope (senpi#6862)

### What changed

- `scripts/test-temp-scope-owner.mjs` (new): owns the exclusive `sj-*` scopes.
  - `writeScopeOwner` writes an owner record into each scope. The record names the scope's own job registry and the registry of the guarded job that ran the snapshot, if any. It is written to a temporary name and renamed into place.
  - `concurrentJobScope` treats a fresh `sj-*` in system temp as a concurrent job's scope only when all of these hold: its record names a registry that still exists; that registry is not this job's and was not handed down from it; and the registry lies outside this job's scope and outside every entry this job created after its snapshot. A scope that vanished before it was read also counts as not this job's. A missing, empty, forged or orphaned record stays a leftover.
  - `retireScope` renames a scope out of the `sj-` namespace before deleting it, so a concurrent check never sees the scope without its record. Where the rename fails, the record is removed last.
- `scripts/test-temp-guard.mjs`: `snapshot --export-env` writes the owner record, and both scope removals go through `retireScope`. The system-temp scan of `check` logs a concurrent job's scope instead of reporting it, and never removes it. Every other repo-named escape is still a leftover.
- `scripts/test-temp-concurrent.test.mjs` (new): contracts for two jobs with separate snapshots.
  - B's scope is not A's leftover, either while B is live or while B tears it down; the teardown case is interleaved deterministically through an `rmSync` hook.
  - A's own file leak, its nested scope, an unmarked `sj-*`, a scope whose record has no owner, an orphaned grandchild and a forged empty record are all still reported.

### Why

- senpi#6862: on a machine that runs several test jobs at once, every job's scope sits in the shared `/tmp` under the same `sj-` prefix. A sibling's live scope therefore failed jobs whose own tests left nothing. Four coding-agent shards of #6831 went red only at the temp check, and each was green when rerun alone.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The guard is CI/bootstrap tooling outside the extension runtime.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the system loop and the scope teardown of `check` in `scripts/test-temp-guard.mjs`, and the scope setup at the top of the snapshot `try`.
