## 2026-11-16 - The temp guard no longer blames a job for a concurrent job's scope (senpi#6862)

### What changed

- `scripts/test-temp-guard.mjs`: `snapshot --export-env` writes an owner record into the new exclusive scope, naming the enclosing guarded job's registry (`SENPI_TEST_TEMP_REGISTRY`), or none for a top-level job. The record is written to a temporary name and renamed into place. The system-temp scan in `check` uses `concurrentJobScope` to skip a fresh `sj-*` whose record names another owner, or which vanished before it was read. It logs that scope as a concurrent job scope and never removes it. Unmarked `sj-*` entries, scopes nested in this job's own tests, and every other repo-named escape are still leftovers.
- `scripts/test-temp-system.test.mjs`: a contract with two jobs that take separate snapshots. Job B's scope is not job A's leftover, and A's own file leak, nested scope and unmarked `sj-*` are still reported.

### Why

- senpi#6862: on a machine that runs several test jobs at once, every job's scope sits in the shared `/tmp` under the same `sj-` prefix. A sibling's live scope therefore failed jobs whose own tests left nothing. Four coding-agent shards of #6831 went red only at the temp check, and each was green when rerun alone.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The guard is CI/bootstrap tooling outside the extension runtime.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `reportTempLeaks` and the system loop of `check` in `scripts/test-temp-guard.mjs`, and the scope setup at the top of the snapshot `try`.
