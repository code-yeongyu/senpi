## 2026-11-22 - A sizes-only startup leaves the session's terminals alone (senpi#4104)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/terminal/extension.ts`: `session_start` skips `startPersistence` when `event.sizesOnly` is set, so `--print-prompt-sizes` never takes the session's terminal lease or restores its manifest.

### Why

- Pointed at an existing session, the one-shot inspection acquired that session's lease and restored its terminals, which can kill confirmed orphan processes.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The behavior lives in the builtin terminal extension's own session_start handler.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `session_start` handler in `terminal/extension.ts`.
