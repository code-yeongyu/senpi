## 2026-11-19 - A top-level fallbackChains in settings.json gets a startup warning (senpi#6673)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/settings-diagnostics.ts`: a top-level `fallbackChains` key in global or project settings becomes a startup warning that names the scope and points at `retry.fallbackChains`. A private `settingsWarnings()` feeds both the plain collector and `collectSettingsDiagnosticsWithContext()`, next to the existing `providers.<id>.maxConcurrency` warning.

### Why

- senpi#6673. Only `retry.fallbackChains` is read, so chains placed at the top level were ignored with no warning and the engine quietly used the default chain.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Settings diagnostics are collected by the core before extensions load; an extension cannot add to the startup settings warnings.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the new `misplacedSettingsWarnings()` and `settingsWarnings()` in `settings-diagnostics.ts`, and the two collector lines that call `settingsWarnings()`.
