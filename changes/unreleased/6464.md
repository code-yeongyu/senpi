## 2026-11-11 - Repeated turn admission keeps its restored-binding decision (senpi#6412 review)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/session-restored-admission.ts`: `repeatableAdmission` wraps one restored-binding admission so a repeat reports what the first run decided. `admitRestoredBinding` forgets a binding whose transcript it rejects, so the repeat that `admitSettledTurn` runs when a model switch lands mid-admission read no binding and called the transcript available. A rejection is reused only while no binding has been recorded since; a binding recorded meanwhile (a timed-out switch keeps its lineage) is admitted afresh.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/session-stream.ts`: `createResidentAttempt` passes its admission through `repeatableAdmission`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/anthropic-subscription/session-turn-attempt.ts`: the `admitSettledTurn` docstring states that a switch issued after admission returns still calls `setModel` at once, like one issued during a running turn, and the next turn's admission waits for it. Comment only; no behaviour change.

### Why

- A live session with a restored binding that has a saved prefix hash, hit by a switch mid-admission, took its continuity decision from a state the first admission had already cleared, losing the `transcript_missing` outcome the first run reached.
- Gating `setModel` for the window between admission and submission would hold switches there but not during a running turn, and would have to cover an entry still being reattached, so the docstring was corrected instead.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Turn admission and the restored-binding check belong to this builtin provider; no hook runs between them.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the admission call at the head of `createResidentAttempt` in `session-stream.ts`, and the end of `session-restored-admission.ts`.
