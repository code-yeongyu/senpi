## 2026-11-04 - A submit parked during startup runs once startup finishes (senpi#5737, fixes #5731)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/interactive/interactive-mode.ts`: `handleStartupSubmit` records the text it parks (`startupParkedSubmission`). `init()` calls the new `replayStartupSubmission()` after `setupEditorSubmitHandler()`, `attachEditor()` and `adoptDefaultEditorActions()`. When the editor still holds exactly the parked text, the editor is cleared and the text goes through `defaultEditor.onSubmit`, the real submit path with its session-control submission edge. An edited draft is left in the editor, and the parked text is consumed once.
- `packages/coding-agent/test/suite/regressions/5731-startup-submit-replays.test.ts` (new): a parked `/resume` is submitted exactly once; an edited draft stays; nothing parked submits nothing.

### Why

- Startup parks a submit until the restored transcript renders (#4826), but nothing ran it afterwards, so an early `/resume` sat typed in the editor behind `Startup is still in progress` until a second Enter. On a loaded machine the window lasts seconds.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The startup submit path and its handoff to the real submit handler live inside `InteractiveMode.init()`; an extension cannot see or resubmit a parked editor submission.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `adoptDefaultEditorActions(...)` line at the end of `init()`, `handleStartupSubmit`, and the private field block near `preResolvedSubmissionImages`.
