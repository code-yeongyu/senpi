## 2026-11-16 - A store another process holds still shows its stored sign-ins (senpi#6841)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/auth-storage.ts`: when `reload()` finds the lock held before the store ever loaded, the in-memory credentials come from `auth.json` read without its lock (`readWithoutLock`), not an empty placeholder. The read is never written back, and the store stays busy (`isCredentialStoreBusy()`), so the next read still takes the lock. An unreadable or unparsable file keeps the old placeholder.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/model-registry.ts`: `retryBusyCredentialRead()` re-reads a busy store at most once per synchronous pass. A guard set after a busy re-read is cleared on the next microtask.

### Why

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/auth-storage.ts`: while another process held `auth.json`'s lock, every stored sign-in read as missing, so a host's provider list showed a connected account as not connected.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/model-registry.ts`: each provider row re-read the busy store and waited out the 1 s sync lock budget, so one `get_auth_providers` call blocked for about 45 s. Measured with a second process holding the lock.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The busy fallback and the re-read live inside the credential store and the model registry that every surface reads; an extension cannot change what they answer.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `auth-storage.ts` `reload()` busy branch, and `model-registry.ts` `retryBusyCredentialRead()`.

## 2026-11-16 - get_auth_providers says when the sign-in store is busy (senpi#6841)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/connection-handler.ts`: `get_auth_providers` adds `storeBusy: true` while another process holds `auth.json`'s lock.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/rpc-read-response-types.ts`: the response type gains `storeBusy?: true`.

### Why

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/connection-handler.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/rpc-read-response-types.ts`: a client could not tell a busy store from a missing sign-in, so it showed "not connected" instead of saying the store is busy and reading again.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The RPC response shape is owned by the RPC mode; an extension cannot add a field to it.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `get_auth_providers` case in `connection-handler.ts`, and its response type.
