## 2026-11-22 - Report the asking connection's own id in get_protocol_info (#5393)

### What changed
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-router-host-reads.ts` adds the asking connection's `connectionId` to a multi-session host's `get_protocol_info` answer; a call with no connection context omits it. `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/rpc-protocol-info-types.ts` declares the optional field and `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-protocol-info.ts` keeps it through the client-side boundary parse, absent when absent.

### Why
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-router-host-reads.ts`: `raisedBy` and effect receipts name connections by id, but no RPC surface told a client its own, so a client could not recognise a question it raised. `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/rpc-protocol-info-types.ts` and `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-protocol-info.ts` carry the same field on the typed wire and client boundary.

### Why an extension could not handle it
- The connection identity lives in the host's session writer, which `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-router-host-reads.ts` reads; extensions do not see which RPC connection sent a request, and the protocol types in `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/rpc-protocol-info-types.ts` and `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-protocol-info.ts` are owned by the RPC mode.

### Expected merge conflict zones
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-router-host-reads.ts`: `answerProtocolInfo` data; `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/rpc-protocol-info-types.ts`: `RpcProtocolInfo`; `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-protocol-info.ts`: `HostProtocolInfo` and its parse.
