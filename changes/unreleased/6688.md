## 2026-11-14 - list_sessions activity detail: awaiting_approval, queued, errored, error_code, turn_id (senpi#6688)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-activity.ts` (new): one derivation of a session's activity from the same `liveStatus()` session_status uses: awaiting_approval (waiting_input), queued (running with steering or follow-up queued), running, errored (idle after a provider failure; `error_code` is that failure's category), idle; `turn_id` is the admission turn epoch.
- `session-status.ts` (exports `liveStatus`), `session-status-source.ts` (`sessionTurnView`), `session-registry-types.ts`, `session-worker-protocol.ts`, `session-worker-snapshot.ts`: every list_sessions row carries the three fields; worker snapshots carry `turnId` and `errorCode`.

### Why

- Thread lists on every device need to tell a waiting, queued or failed thread from a plain running or idle one, without a get_state per session (design N3).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- list_sessions rows and the status derivation belong to the RPC host.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: fork-only RPC files next to #6677's activity field.

## 2026-11-14 - Terminal control endpoint row activity detail (senpi#6688)

### Why
