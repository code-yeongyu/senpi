## 2026-11-22 - Tool-image quotas hold across RPC hosts (senpi#2628)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/tool-media-store.ts`: measure and write under the existing RPC host file lock, use asynchronous filesystem operations, and yield between bounded base64 decode and hash chunks.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/media-placeholders.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-event-writer-destinations.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-event-writer-state.ts`, and `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-event-writer-connections.ts`: capture the durable scope at enqueue and wait for persistence at the capable socket's FIFO position.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/socket-event-fanout.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/socket-event-queue.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-admission-routing.ts`, and `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compact-materializer-line.ts`: preserve queue position, routing and compact record identity while the media line is pending.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-event-fanout.ts`, `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/legacy-replay-store.ts`, and `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/legacy-view-cursor.ts`: allow a frozen legacy replay frame to share a pending media projection.

### Why

- The per-process reservation could overshoot the durable session limit during a host handover. A cold write also blocked other sessions while decoding and measuring media.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The RPC store owns quota enforcement and the socket writer owns when a persisted path becomes visible.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the tool-media store, placeholder projection, socket queue, compact envelopes and legacy replay under `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/`.

### Fixed
- RPC hosts now share the 256 MiB tool-image quota for each durable session. The first image write after restart no longer blocks the host loop while measuring stored media or decoding and hashing large images ([#2628](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/senpi/issues/2628)).
