## 2026-11-11 - CI retries the apt download with backoff after a runner DNS failure (senpi#6446)

### What changed

- `scripts/install-ci-system-deps.sh`: after the `archive.ubuntu.com` fallback, the apt download retries with waits of 30, 60 and 120 s, each attempt bounded at 150 s. A deadline 570 s after the fallback starts skips any wait that would leave too little time for a full attempt (when attempts are slow), so the step ends with this script's `::error::` line inside its 12-minute limit; fast DNS failures still get every wait.
- `scripts/install-ci-system-deps.test.mjs`: new, drives the script through `sudo`/`timeout`/`apt-get`/`sleep`/`date` shims, with a fake clock for the slow-attempt case.

### Why

- `scripts/install-ci-system-deps.sh`: hosted runners lost DNS in windows of about 4 minutes on 2026-11-11 (14:16-14:20Z, 16:05-16:09Z, 16:31-16:35Z); the single immediate retry failed inside the same window, so jobs died with apt exit 100 before any test ran.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `scripts/install-ci-system-deps.sh`: CI setup is repository tooling.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the fallback block in `scripts/install-ci-system-deps.sh`.

## 2026-11-11 - The system-dependency steps allow 12 minutes so the apt backoff can ride out a DNS outage (senpi#6446)

### What changed

- `.github/workflows/ci.yml`: the four `Install system dependencies` steps (in `check`, `test-coding-agent`, `grep-native-contract` and `test-workspaces`) raise `timeout-minutes` from 5 to 12.

### Why

- `.github/workflows/ci.yml`: the 5-minute cap from senpi#4131 stopped a slow mirror from eating shard time, but it also cut off the new 30/60/120 s apt backoff (`scripts/install-ci-system-deps.sh`) before it could outlast the measured ~4-minute runner DNS outages. The jobs stay inside their 30-minute limit (tests take 9-18 minutes), and the coding-agent shard budget clock starts after this step.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `.github/workflows/ci.yml`: step timeouts are workflow configuration.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `timeout-minutes` line of the four system-dependency steps.
