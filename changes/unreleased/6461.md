## 2026-11-11 - The OAuth refresh leeway is capped at half the access token lifetime (senpi#6461)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/mcp/auth/oauth-refresh.ts`: `isTokenStale()` compares the time left with the smaller of `REFRESH_LEEWAY_MS` and half of the record's `tokenLifetimeMs`, instead of the fixed five minutes.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/mcp/auth/oauth-grant.ts`: `mergeTokensIntoStoredAuth()` stores `tokenLifetimeMs` (the response's `expires_in`) next to `expiresAt`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/mcp/auth/token-store.ts`: `McpStoredAuth` gains optional `tokenLifetimeMs`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/mcp/auth/oauth-provider.ts`: `invalidateCredentials("tokens")` clears it with `expiresAt`.

### Why

A token issued with `expires_in: 300` had five minutes or less left the moment it was saved, so every `ensureFresh()` refreshed it again and the server's tools never loaded. A record saved before this change has no `tokenLifetimeMs` and keeps the fixed leeway until its next refresh.

### Why an extension could not handle it

The refresh decision is made inside the builtin MCP client's token handling; no extension hook sees it.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `isTokenStale()` and `REFRESH_LEEWAY_MS` in `oauth-refresh.ts`, and the `expiresAt` line of `mergeTokensIntoStoredAuth()` in `oauth-grant.ts`.
