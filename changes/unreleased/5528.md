## 2026-11-02 - kill_bash state-dir removal is best-effort (senpi#2883, PR #5528)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/terminal/tools/kill-bash.ts`: `dropStateDir()` catches a failed state-dir removal and appends it to the `Killed ...` text as a note instead of turning a completed kill into an error; with `all: true` every remaining monitor's dir is still attempted.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/terminal/monitor-state-dir.ts`: `removeMonitorStateDir()` keeps one in-flight removal per dir, which every caller shares.

### Why

- A stopped watch has two removers of its state dir, `kill_bash` and the monitor-ended sink in `extension-state.ts`, and they raced two recursive `rm`s of one tree. The unguarded `rm` in `kill_bash` then replaced the success result with `EPERM`/`EACCES`, and `all: true` stopped at the first failing dir.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Both files are the terminal builtin's own tool and state-dir helpers.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the body of `execute` in `tools/kill-bash.ts`; `removeMonitorStateDir` in `monitor-state-dir.ts`.
