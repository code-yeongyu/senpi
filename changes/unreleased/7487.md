## 2026-11-26 - A superseded extension compaction reports to its caller (senpi#7482)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/agent-session.ts`: the extension `compact` binding calls the request's `onError` when a newer compaction generation owns the terminal transition, instead of returning with no callback. The newer generation still publishes the only `compaction_end`.

### Why

- The context-reduction handoff stays in flight until its compaction reports. A superseded compaction never reported, so every later turn that needed a handoff was aborted with an empty reply (oh-my-openagent#15026).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The superseded ending is decided inside the session's compaction ownership, which extensions cannot observe; the callback contract of `ctx.compact` is the session's to keep.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the catch block of the extension `compact` binding in `agent-session.ts`.
