## 2026-10-30 - Reauthorize the selected SIWC registration (senpi#5046)

### What changed

- `packages/ai/src/auth/oauth/openai-chatgpt.ts` reuses that account's issued client ID and retained hints, omits `agent_name_hint`, binds both callback paths to the pending client, and rejects a different verified subject before returning credentials.

### Why

- `packages/ai/src/auth/oauth/openai-chatgpt.ts` must preserve the registration and verified identity required by OpenAI's SIWC sign-in sections 2-4.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `packages/ai/src/auth/oauth/openai-chatgpt.ts` owns callback validation and token exchange before credential persistence.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- `packages/ai/src/auth/oauth/openai-chatgpt.ts`: callback arguments, authorize parameters, and post-exchange identity validation.

## 2026-10-30 - Pass an explicitly selected account to OAuth login (senpi#5046)

### What changed

- `packages/ai/src/auth/types.ts` adds optional `LoginOptions.reauthorize` for the selected saved OAuth credential; omission means Add account.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- `packages/ai/src/auth/types.ts`: LoginOptions.

## 2026-10-30 - Keep SIWC ID-token hints out of account notices (senpi#5046)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/oauth-login-interaction.ts` removes ID-token hints from printable authorization URLs while sending the original hinted URL to the TUI browser launcher.

### Why

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/oauth-login-interaction.ts` sends notices to terminal and RPC consumers that may retain their text.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/oauth-login-interaction.ts` is the shared account-login relay used by those extensions.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/oauth-login-interaction.ts`: auth_url relay.
