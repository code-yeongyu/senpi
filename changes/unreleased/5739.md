### Added
- A hand fences a moved session's old owner with an epoch floor (`src/execution/remote-epoch.ts`, brain/hand S35, relay design 4.4): `createHandEpochFence({ ownerOf, durable? })` keeps one floor per execution scope that only the scope's owner raises, one epoch at a time, that never lowers and remembers its owner; with `durable` it persists on the hand's store on the ledger's admission line. `createRemoteExecutor({ fence })` answers `floor` frames and refuses an invoke below the floor (`epoch_fenced`) before anything of it is recorded, opened or run, also inside each step of a call's walk. `createRemoteToolHost({ epoch })` dispatches a scope lane's calls only after the hand acked exactly its epoch; a late or duplicate floor answer cannot change a decided lane, and after a hand restart a pending call is re-sent only once the re-sent floor is acked. Without a ledger the hand re-checks the epoch right before the tool runs, and an acknowledgement is fenced inside its admission slot. A second link's executor on a live hand no longer re-runs restart recovery. Adds the EPOCH, MULTI-EPOCH and KILL-EPOCH conformance cases ([#5739](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/senpi/pull/5739)).

## 2026-11-04 - Epoch floor on the hand (senpi#5739)

### What changed

- `packages/agent/src/execution/remote-epoch.ts` and `remote-epoch-gate.ts` are new: the hand's per-scope epoch fence and the session's floor gate.
- `packages/agent/src/execution/remote-protocol.ts`: `floor`, `floor_ack` and `floor_refused` messages; an optional `epoch` on invoke, abort and ack.
- `packages/agent/src/execution/remote-executor.ts`: optional `fence`; floor frames answered; fenced invokes refused (without a ledger, again right before the tool runs) and fenced aborts and acks ignored.
- `packages/agent/src/execution/remote-tool-host.ts`: optional `epoch`; each lane dispatches after its floor ACK; on `hello` the floor is re-sent and pending calls wait for its new ACK.
- `packages/agent/src/execution/ledger.ts`, `ledger-types.ts`: an optional in-slot guard on `admit` and `apply`; refusal `epoch_fenced`.
- `packages/agent/src/execution/remote-hand-ledger.ts`: the guard on every walk step and on the ack, a fenced walk's record cancelled, recovery once per process.
- `packages/agent/src/index.ts`: exports the fence surface and the floor message types.

### Why

Brain/hand split S35 (senpi#5729): after a move, an old owner's delayed command must not run after the new owner's first one, and the new owner dispatches only after the hand acknowledged its floor.

### Why an extension could not handle it

The check must run inside the hand's own ledger admission slot and before its scope opens.

### Expected merge conflict zones

`remote-executor.ts` frame dispatch and options; `remote-tool-host.ts` dispatch; `src/index.ts` exports.
