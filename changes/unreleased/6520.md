## 2026-11-12 - The other cold-start waits of the experimental server are load-tolerant too (senpi#6487)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/experimental/coordinator.ts`: `ensureCoordinator` waits for the coordinator it started under `COORDINATOR_STARTUP_HANG_MS` (120 s) measured with `performance.now()`. It used 10 s on `Date.now()`. The wait still ends at once when the control socket accepts or the coordinator exits; it also checks the caller's lock (`assertHeld`, the launcher lock a server starts under) on every poll, and a wait that ends without a reachable coordinator stops the coordinator it started.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/experimental/lock-wait.ts` (new): `waitForFileLock` takes a file lock one attempt at a time on the monotonic clock, with one bound for a held lock and another for transient acquire errors (`EMFILE`, `ENFILE`, `EBUSY`, `EAGAIN`, `EINTR`); other errors are thrown at once.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/experimental/server.ts`: `acquireServerActivation` waits up to `ACTIVATION_LOCK_WAIT_MS` (130 s) for another activator's lock. `acquireServerProfile` waits for the launcher lock as long as its holder lives: a server holds that lock through its whole startup (plugin builds, its coordinator, its backend, worker discovery) and proper-lockfile refreshes it every 10 s, so a live holder never looks stale, and a dead holder's lock goes stale 30 s after its last refresh and is taken over then. Both used proper-lockfile's own retry budgets (10 s and 30 s) on the wall clock. Each launcher-lock holder records its pid and the inode of the lock directory it took in `launcher-<id>.owner`; an activator that stops its own server after a failed startup removes that lock at once, and only when both still match, so a newer holder that took a stale lock over (a takeover re-creates the directory) is never touched. A launcher waiter gives up after 10 minutes on a holder that lives but never finishes, with a `LauncherLockTimeoutError` naming that holder's pid. Lock staleness and refresh are unchanged. `startServer` passes its launcher lock check to `ensureCoordinator`.

### Why

- #6504 made the activator's startup wait load-tolerant, but a cold start still had wall-clock bounds on the same path: the server's wait for its coordinator (10 s), a second activator's wait for the first one's lock (10 s), and a second launcher's wait for a starting server's launcher lock (30 s, while that server may now wait 120 s for its coordinator).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- These waits are the experimental server's own startup path; no extension runs before them.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `ensureCoordinator`, `acquireServerProfile`, `acquireServerActivation`, and the lock and activation constants in `server.ts`.

### Fixed
- A cold experimental server under host load now gets up to 120 s, on the monotonic clock, for its coordinator to start (it was 10 s on the wall clock), and a second client starting the same server waits for the first one instead of failing after 10 s (activation lock) or 30 s (launcher lock); a server stopped during a failed startup no longer blocks the next start for 30 s; a coordinator whose startup wait fails is stopped instead of left running ([#6520](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/senpi/pull/6520))
