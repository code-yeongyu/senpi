## 2026-11-12 - The other two cold-start waits of the experimental server are load-tolerant too (senpi#6487)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/experimental/coordinator.ts`: `ensureCoordinator` waits for the coordinator it started under `COORDINATOR_STARTUP_HANG_MS` (120 s) measured with `performance.now()`. It used 10 s on `Date.now()`. The wait still ends at once when the control socket accepts or the coordinator exits.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/experimental/server.ts`: `acquireServerActivation` makes one lock attempt at a time and waits for another activator's lock up to `ACTIVATION_LOCK_WAIT_MS` (the 120 s startup bound plus 10 s), measured with `performance.now()`. It used proper-lockfile's own retry budget of 10 s, measured on the wall clock. Lock staleness (20 s) and refresh (10 s) are unchanged.

### Why

- #6504 made the activator's startup wait load-tolerant, but a cold start still had two 10 s wall-clock bounds on the same path: the server's wait for its coordinator, and a second activator's wait for the first one's lock while that one waits out a slow start.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Both waits are the experimental server's own startup path; no extension runs before them.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the startup wait in `ensureCoordinator`, `acquireServerActivation`, and the activation constants in `server.ts`.

### Fixed
- A cold experimental server on a loaded machine no longer fails with "Timed out waiting for coordinator startup", and a second client starting the same server no longer gives up after 10 s while the first one is still starting it: both waits now tolerate a slow cold start and are measured on the monotonic clock ([#6520](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/senpi/pull/6520))
