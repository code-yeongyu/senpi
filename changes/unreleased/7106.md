## 2026-11-20 - Stop wins while a peer resume is being admitted (senpi#7104)

### What changed

- `agent-session.ts` checks the observed pause and live holds during held-input preparation and at final admission. A refused peer resume leaves the inputs and notices in custody. Successful settlement acknowledges only the notice IDs carried by its own turn.

### Why

- The previous guard ran before an asynchronous read. A newer Stop could remain visible while the resumed turn ran and consumed its input.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Only the engine owns final turn admission, the pause sequence and the held-input ledger.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- `agent-session.ts`: guarded admission and settlement of a resumed turn.

## 2026-11-20 - Prove guarded interrupted resume on the target session (senpi#7104)

### What changed

- `resume_session` accepts `mode: "interrupted"` only with `expectedSeq` and `unlessStopped: true`. A held extension command needs human Resume. The host advertises `resume_session_guarded_admission_v1`; the target's pause state supplies the independent `guardedResumeAdmission: 1` marker.

### Why

- Older hosts can echo negotiated capability strings and a package version alone cannot distinguish builds. A caller needs proof from the session that will handle its request.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The host owns protocol negotiation, command validation and the session state sent on the wire.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- The `resume_session` schema, capability lists and pause state types.
