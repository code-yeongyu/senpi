## 2026-11-17 - show_html_page hands the host a page with its local images inlined (senpi#6899)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/html-render/tool.ts`: `details.html` is the page with its absolute-path local images (`<img src>` and CSS `url()`) inlined as `data:` URIs by `inlineLocalImages`, not the page as written. A page naming a local image that cannot be read is refused with `HtmlRenderImagesNotFoundError` and nothing is written; the `missingImages` details field is removed.

### Why

- A host publishes `details.html`. For a session on another machine the viewer got raw paths on the producing machine, which it cannot read and the page sandbox blocks, so those images stayed blank. A page with a broken image was also reported as shown although the desktop refuses to publish it.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `show_html_page` is this builtin's own tool; its result details are defined here.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `execute` body and `ShowHtmlPageDetails` in `html-render/tool.ts`.
