## 2026-11-22 - Keep frequently read skill sources in the bounded cache (#4444)

### What changed

`packages/coding-agent/src/core/skill-source-cache.ts` refreshes map order on hits and skips cache insertion for files with zero change time.

### Why

`packages/coding-agent/src/core/skill-source-cache.ts` previously evicted entries by insertion time, even if read recently, and reused a same-size backdated rewrite when no ctime was available.

### Why an extension could not handle it

`packages/coding-agent/src/core/skill-source-cache.ts` owns the process-wide map used during discovery before an extension can observe the read.

### Expected merge conflict zones

`packages/coding-agent/src/core/skill-source-cache.ts`: cache-hit order, timestamp admission and byte-budget eviction.
