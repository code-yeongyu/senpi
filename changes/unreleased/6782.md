## 2026-11-15 - A loop-hook call's queue wait bounds each call ahead, and its timer stays in range (senpi#5898)

### What changed

- `packages/agent/src/execution/loop-hook-session-queue.ts`: a waiting call gives the call ahead of it `waitMs` to settle, and the bound restarts each time a call ahead settles (it used to start when the call arrived and cover the whole queue). Each wait timer is clamped to `MAX_TIMER_DELAY_MS` (`0x7fffffff`, about 24.8 days): `setTimeout` runs a longer delay after 1 ms.
- `packages/agent/src/execution/loop-hook-host-hand.ts`: `DEFAULT_LOOP_HOOK_QUEUE_WAIT_MS`, `queueWaitMs` and the `queue_timeout` refusal message describe the per-call bound and the clamp.
- `packages/agent/src/execution/loop-hook-host-protocol.ts`: the ordering paragraph describes the per-call bound.
- `packages/agent/src/index.ts`: exports `MAX_TIMER_DELAY_MS`.

### Why

- Review of #6774 (L1): a cap past the timer range made every waiting call refuse `queue_timeout` after 1 ms.
- Review of #6774 (L2): a total bound tripped on a healthy session whose calls were slow but kept settling. A per-call bound catches a stuck call and never a queue that keeps moving.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The loop-hook link is agent-core protocol.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `createLoopHookSessionQueues` and `waitForTurn` in `loop-hook-session-queue.ts`, the queue-wait docs in `loop-hook-host-hand.ts` and the protocol header, and the loop-hook export block of `index.ts`.
