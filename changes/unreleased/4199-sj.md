## 2026-10-27 - Recovery and grants verify the process by its retained handle, creation time and token SID (desktop#4199)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/process-evidence.ts` (new): the design's observation table over one observation of a recorded process (`classifyObservation`): absent, a different creation FILETIME or a signalled process object is `gone`; the same `GetProcessId`, exact creation FILETIME and `TokenUser` SID on one handle is `live`; a denied open or an unreadable field is `unknown` (`unreadable`), the recorded creation time under another SID or a handle whose PID is not the opened one is `unknown` (`inconsistent`), a record that cannot be compared (no SID recorded, a noncomparable start time) is `unknown` (`incomplete`). Evidence sources: `nativeProcessEvidence` (the bridge: one `OpenProcess` per `open`, the handle retained until `close`, `observe` rereading it), `startTimeEvidence` (a start-time reader, today's path off Windows), `identityReaderEvidence` (the budgeted identity reader recovery used before), `processEvidence(native, fallback)` choosing by bridge availability, and `ownProcessIdentity(native)` reading this process's PID, creation FILETIME and SID on one handle of itself.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-launch-reservation.ts`: `LaunchIdentity` gains an optional `userSid`; `identityKey` records it when present and keeps the two-field encoding otherwise, so every earlier row compares as before. `grantClaimedParticipant` takes a `ProcessEvidence` instead of a start-time function: it opens the claimed PID, refuses `participant_mismatch` for a different creation time, an exited object or an absent PID, `identity_inconsistent` (new `LaunchReservationRejection`) for a same-birth SID mismatch or an inconsistent handle, `identity_unverified` for an unreadable or noncomparable observation, closes a refused handle at once and returns the `GrantedParticipant` whose `release` closes the handle it granted on. A start-time reader's identity is still compared exactly by the store's compare-and-grant.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/endpoint-recovery.ts`: the prober observes through the bridge when it is available (each observation opens, reads and closes), the identity reader otherwise; `observeRecorded` applies the observation table, requeries an unreadable or inconsistent answer once inside the budget and then escalates `owner_identity_unreadable` or `owner_identity_inconsistent` (which nothing emitted before), and answers `owner_identity_incomplete` at once for a record it cannot compare, a recorded identity without a SID included. A recorded identity's `userSid` is read from the row.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-ensure-start.ts`: the reserving owner records its identity through `ownProcessIdentity`; the supervisor is granted through `processEvidence` (the bridge on Windows, the same start-time probe elsewhere) and its handle is kept until the readiness verdict. `host-lifecycle.ts`: the supervisor claims with `ownProcessIdentity`, grants its host through the bridge and keeps that handle until the child's exit. `supervisor-route.ts`: the host claims with `ownProcessIdentity`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/socket-secret-native.ts`: `NativeProcessBinding` (`processOpen`, `processObserve`, `processClose`) and `NativeProcessObservation`; the placement loader requires the three exports.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/endpoint-reservation-store.ts`: the `RecoveryEscalationReason` comment names the emitter of `owner_identity_inconsistent`.
- `crates/senpi-socket-secret/src/process_binding.rs` (new): the N-API surface of the retained-handle adapter, a process-rooted registry of `RetainedProcess` by handle ID; `process_handle.rs` marks the owned handle `Send` for it. `tests/native_process_binding.rs`: typed `unavailable` off Windows; a retained handle's facts, reread after exit, close, on a real child on Windows.

### Why

- Design 4.2a "OS identity" and the Windows contracts ("Verify each actual Windows target using `OpenProcess` ... `GetProcessId`, `GetProcessTimes` creation `FILETIME`, and `OpenProcessToken`/`GetTokenInformation(TokenUser)` on that same process handle ... Retain the local non-inheritable handle while granting/observing that participant"), Revision 3/4 ("A matching creation time with unexpected SID, access denial, unreadable fields or inconsistent handle/PID is unknown"; "an account mismatch with the same birth identity is an unresolved inconsistency, not permission to reclaim"), Revision 8 clause (ii). The recovery and grant paths read a CIM creation time only: a process with the recorded creation time under another account counted as the recorded one, and `owner_identity_inconsistent` was reserved but never emitted.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The verification runs inside the issuer's grant transaction and the endpoint-locked recovery, before any extension loads, and needs the native process handle.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-launch-reservation.ts`: `LaunchIdentity`, `identityKey`, `parseIdentity`, `grantClaimedParticipant`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/endpoint-recovery.ts`: the imports, `RecoveryProber.observe`, `recoveryProber`, `parseRecorded`, `observeRecorded`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-ensure-start.ts`, `host-lifecycle.ts`, `supervisor-route.ts`: the identity reads and the grant calls.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/socket-secret-native.ts` and `crates/senpi-socket-secret/src/lib.rs`: the process exports and the loader's required list.

## 2026-10-27 - Daemon records carry the token SID and the supervisor learns host exit from the process object (desktop#4199)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/process-evidence.ts`: two observation domains, never compared against each other. A record with a `userSid` was read on a process handle (the complete `GetProcessTimes` FILETIME); one without was read by the platform's start-time reader (on Windows the CIM table, whose `CreationDate` keeps microseconds only, so its FILETIME differs from the handle's in the last digit). `classifyObservation` answers `incomplete` for a handle observation of a SID-less record and for a reader observation of a SID-bearing record, before any creation-time comparison: an old two-field row stays readable and stays unknown, never gone by a cross-domain mismatch. `bridgeProcessIdentity(native, pid)` (new) reads a pid's identity on one handle, closed at once; `ownProcessIdentity` is that read of `process.pid`. `RetainedProcess.waitExit(signal)` (new, `ProcessExitWait`): the process-object exit wait on the retained handle through `processWaitExit`/`processCancelWaitExit`, `exited` with the exit code, `cancelled` on abort or close, `unavailable` off the bridge.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-launch-reservation.ts`: `GrantedParticipant.waitExit(signal)`, the participant's exit as its process object signals it through the handle the issuer retains.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-daemon-registration.ts`: `HostRegistration.record` is a `ProcessRecord`; `writeGenerationRecord` writes `userSid` beside `processStartTime` when the record carries one. `host-ensure-start.ts`: on the reservation path the generation record names the supervisor by the identity the issuer verified on its retained handle (PID, creation FILETIME, SID), the start-time probe's record otherwise.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-lifecycle-scratch.ts`: `recordChildPid` takes an optional identity reader; given one, `host-child.pid` carries the child's creation FILETIME and SID read on one handle, the CIM waits otherwise. `host-lifecycle.ts`: a reservation-admitted supervisor passes the bridge's reader (from the lazy reservation path, so the startup import graph is unchanged); the host's end is recorded once, by `onChildExit`, from whichever comes first: the granted host's process object (`waitExit`, armed after the grant, cancelled by the shutdown's watcher stop or the handle's release, answering with the object's exit code) or the ChildProcess exit event, the only signal off the bridge and a secondary one on it.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-reservations.ts`: `SessionPathOwner.userSid`; `createSessionPathReservations`/`createEndpointReservations` take an optional `identity` reader whose record the claims publish (`userSid` beside `processStartTime`), the start-time reader of `pid` otherwise; `readOwner` reads `userSid` back, so the recovery census compares a claim in the domain it was written in. `multi-session-host.ts`: the host passes `ownProcessIdentity(loadPresencePlacement())`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/socket-secret-native.ts`: `NativeProcessBinding.processWaitExit`/`processCancelWaitExit`, `NativeProcessObservation` statuses `exited` (`exitCode`) and `cancelled`; the placement loader requires the two exports.
- `crates/senpi-socket-secret/src/process_handle.rs`: `RetainedProcess::duplicate_for_wait` (`DuplicateHandle`, same access, non-inheritable), `CancelEvent`, `ProcessWaitHandle::wait_exit_or` (`WaitForMultipleObjects` on the object and the event, `GetExitCodeProcess` on signal), `ExitSignal`. `process_binding.rs`: `begin_exit_wait` registers a wait under its wait ID (`held`/`wait_pending`, `not_held`, `failed`), `processWaitExit` resolves a deferred promise from a dedicated thread that blocks on it, `processCancelWaitExit` sets the event, `processClose` sets the events of its handle's pending waits. `tests/native_process_binding.rs`: the exit wait on a real child (signal with exit code 1, cancel by wait ID, cancel by close), typed `unavailable` off Windows.
- Stacked on senpi#4481 (the fold-pass heads): `process-evidence.ts` `processRecordKey` is the one canonical identity key the store (`identityKey`) and the recovery census share, so a daemon record the bridge wrote matches its generation's recorded authority with its SID. `grantClaimedParticipant` reopens an unreadable claimant once inside the smaller of the deadline and five seconds (`IDENTITY_OBSERVATION_ATTEMPTS`, senpi#4469 fold) and grants the role- and path-bound commitment. A claimant reads itself through the bridge first and with the bounded start-time retry (`readClaimantIdentity`) off it. In `endpoint-recovery.ts` the prior-boot proof and the repair anchor (senpi#4481 fold) resolve an entry every observation found unreadable or one it cannot compare (a record from the other observation domain included), and never one found inconsistent: a process answering the recorded birth under another SID stays `owner_identity_inconsistent` whatever the boot evidence says.
- `packages/coding-agent/test/helpers/fake-windows-presence.ts`: the fake bridge's parked exit waits (`signal(pid, exitCode)`, cancel, close). `test/suite/regressions/desktop-4199-recovery-process-identity.test.ts`: the domain rule, `host-child.pid` and a session claim written through the bridge reading `census_live`/`drain_owner`, `startHost` registering the granted identity, `waitExit` on a granted participant; the slice-J case "a different creation time still proves a legacy row gone" now expects `owner_identity_incomplete`.

### Why

- Design 4.2a, the two open items of the previous entry. "Verified common boot/PID/clock domain and units, same PID, coherent observation, different exact creation/start discriminator" is the only gone-by-mismatch row of the observation table; a CIM-written record against a handle FILETIME is not a common domain, and with the last digit truncated it would have read a live owner gone. The daemon's own records (the generation record, `host-child.pid`, the session claims) were CIM-written and SID-less, so a live census entry read `owner_identity_incomplete` and routed to Repair instead of `drain_owner`. "Retain the local non-inheritable handle while granting/observing that participant; use process-object signalling for exit (`RegisterWaitForSingleObject` or equivalent), not wrapper exit": the supervisor learnt its host's death from the ChildProcess exit event alone.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The records are written inside the issuer's grant transaction, the supervisor's spawn and the host's session open, and the exit wait needs the retained native handle; none of that runs through an extension.

### Changed

- On Windows, a reservation grant and the recovery of an abandoned generation verify a recorded process through one retained native handle: `GetProcessId`, the exact creation FILETIME and the `TokenUser` SID must all match, the issuer keeps that handle while it grants and observes the participant, a process with the recorded creation time under another account is refused (`identity_inconsistent`) or escalated (`owner_identity_inconsistent`) instead of counting as the recorded one, and an unreadable answer stays unknown. Other platforms keep their PID and start-time check (sisyphuslabs/omo-desktop-app#4199, design 4.2a "OS identity", Revision 8 clause (ii)).
- On Windows, the daemon's own records (the generation record, `host-child.pid`, the session-path claims) now carry the process's creation FILETIME and `TokenUser` SID read on one handle, so a still-running supervisor, host or session owner found by the recovery of an abandoned generation reads live and answers `drain_owner` instead of `owner_identity_incomplete` and Repair; a record written before, without a SID, is never compared against a handle's reading and stays unknown. The supervisor learns its host's death from the host's process object through the handle it retains, never from a wrapper's exit; the child's exit event is a secondary signal (sisyphuslabs/omo-desktop-app#4199, design 4.2a "OS identity").
