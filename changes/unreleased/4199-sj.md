## 2026-10-27 - Recovery and grants verify the process by its retained handle, creation time and token SID (desktop#4199)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/process-evidence.ts` (new): the design's observation table over one observation of a recorded process (`classifyObservation`): absent, a different creation FILETIME or a signalled process object is `gone`; the same `GetProcessId`, exact creation FILETIME and `TokenUser` SID on one handle is `live`; a denied open or an unreadable field is `unknown` (`unreadable`), the recorded creation time under another SID or a handle whose PID is not the opened one is `unknown` (`inconsistent`), a record that cannot be compared (no SID recorded, a noncomparable start time) is `unknown` (`incomplete`). Evidence sources: `nativeProcessEvidence` (the bridge: one `OpenProcess` per `open`, the handle retained until `close`, `observe` rereading it), `startTimeEvidence` (a start-time reader, today's path off Windows), `identityReaderEvidence` (the budgeted identity reader recovery used before), `processEvidence(native, fallback)` choosing by bridge availability, and `ownProcessIdentity(native)` reading this process's PID, creation FILETIME and SID on one handle of itself.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-launch-reservation.ts`: `LaunchIdentity` gains an optional `userSid`; `identityKey` records it when present and keeps the two-field encoding otherwise, so every earlier row compares as before. `grantClaimedParticipant` takes a `ProcessEvidence` instead of a start-time function: it opens the claimed PID, refuses `participant_mismatch` for a different creation time, an exited object or an absent PID, `identity_inconsistent` (new `LaunchReservationRejection`) for a same-birth SID mismatch or an inconsistent handle, `identity_unverified` for an unreadable or noncomparable observation, closes a refused handle at once and returns the `GrantedParticipant` whose `release` closes the handle it granted on. A start-time reader's identity is still compared exactly by the store's compare-and-grant.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/endpoint-recovery.ts`: the prober observes through the bridge when it is available (each observation opens, reads and closes), the identity reader otherwise; `observeRecorded` applies the observation table, requeries an unreadable or inconsistent answer once inside the budget and then escalates `owner_identity_unreadable` or `owner_identity_inconsistent` (which nothing emitted before), and answers `owner_identity_incomplete` at once for a record it cannot compare, a recorded identity without a SID included. A recorded identity's `userSid` is read from the row.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-ensure-start.ts`: the reserving owner records its identity through `ownProcessIdentity`; the supervisor is granted through `processEvidence` (the bridge on Windows, the same start-time probe elsewhere) and its handle is kept until the readiness verdict. `host-lifecycle.ts`: the supervisor claims with `ownProcessIdentity`, grants its host through the bridge and keeps that handle until the child's exit. `supervisor-route.ts`: the host claims with `ownProcessIdentity`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/socket-secret-native.ts`: `NativeProcessBinding` (`processOpen`, `processObserve`, `processClose`) and `NativeProcessObservation`; the placement loader requires the three exports.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/endpoint-reservation-store.ts`: the `RecoveryEscalationReason` comment names the emitter of `owner_identity_inconsistent`.
- `crates/senpi-socket-secret/src/process_binding.rs` (new): the N-API surface of the retained-handle adapter, a process-rooted registry of `RetainedProcess` by handle ID; `process_handle.rs` marks the owned handle `Send` for it. `tests/native_process_binding.rs`: typed `unavailable` off Windows; a retained handle's facts, reread after exit, close, on a real child on Windows.

### Why

- Design 4.2a "OS identity" and the Windows contracts ("Verify each actual Windows target using `OpenProcess` ... `GetProcessId`, `GetProcessTimes` creation `FILETIME`, and `OpenProcessToken`/`GetTokenInformation(TokenUser)` on that same process handle ... Retain the local non-inheritable handle while granting/observing that participant"), Revision 3/4 ("A matching creation time with unexpected SID, access denial, unreadable fields or inconsistent handle/PID is unknown"; "an account mismatch with the same birth identity is an unresolved inconsistency, not permission to reclaim"), Revision 8 clause (ii). The recovery and grant paths read a CIM creation time only: a process with the recorded creation time under another account counted as the recorded one, and `owner_identity_inconsistent` was reserved but never emitted.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The verification runs inside the issuer's grant transaction and the endpoint-locked recovery, before any extension loads, and needs the native process handle.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-launch-reservation.ts`: `LaunchIdentity`, `identityKey`, `parseIdentity`, `grantClaimedParticipant`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/endpoint-recovery.ts`: the imports, `RecoveryProber.observe`, `recoveryProber`, `parseRecorded`, `observeRecorded`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-ensure-start.ts`, `host-lifecycle.ts`, `supervisor-route.ts`: the identity reads and the grant calls.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/socket-secret-native.ts` and `crates/senpi-socket-secret/src/lib.rs`: the process exports and the loader's required list.

### Changed

- On Windows, a reservation grant and the recovery of an abandoned generation verify a recorded process through one retained native handle: `GetProcessId`, the exact creation FILETIME and the `TokenUser` SID must all match, the issuer keeps that handle while it grants and observes the participant, a process with the recorded creation time under another account is refused (`identity_inconsistent`) or escalated (`owner_identity_inconsistent`) instead of counting as the recorded one, and an unreadable answer stays unknown. Other platforms keep their PID and start-time check (sisyphuslabs/omo-desktop-app#4199, design 4.2a "OS identity", Revision 8 clause (ii)).
