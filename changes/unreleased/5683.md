## 2026-11-03 - Durable scope lifecycle on the endpoint; LIFECYCLE, SCOPE and MULTI-LIFE-01 cases (S15, senpi#5681)

### What changed

- `packages/agent/src/execution/scope-lifecycle.ts` (new): `createScopeLifecycle` keeps a durable record per scope. A lifecycle event from the issuer commits its handler's effect before it is admitted, and later calls of that scope wait for it. Only the issuer closes. A forced close writes every live resource's terminal outcome first. The idle TTL never closes a scope with live resources.
- `packages/agent/src/execution/testing/{lifecycle-cases,scope-cases,multi-lifecycle-cases,lifecycle-registry}.ts` (new): LIFECYCLE-01..03, SCOPE-01..02, MULTI-LIFE-01. `batch-harness.ts`: `scopes(idleTtlMs, dieAt?)`.
- `packages/agent/src/conformance/{registry,index}.ts`: the cases are registered and exported with `createScopeLifecycle`.

### Why

- A split scope had no durable lifecycle: no commit-before-next-call order, no issuer-only close, and no guard on the TTL or a forced close (design 5.6.6, senpi#5681).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- It is the agent package's own execution layer.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: new files; one line each in the registry, the conformance index and the run-suite case map.

## 2026-11-03 - Lifecycle bridge for a split endpoint (S15, senpi#5681)

### What changed

- `src/core/execution/lifecycle.ts` (new): `createLifecycleBridge` runs a session's lifecycle handlers and tool calls through the endpoint's scope lifecycle. A refused event throws `LifecycleRefusedError`.

### Why

- The commit-before-next-call order of design 5.6.6 needs a seam between a session's lifecycle handlers and its tool calls.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- It sits under the execution layer that dispatches every tool call.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: a new file.
