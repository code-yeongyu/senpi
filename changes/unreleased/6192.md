## 2026-11-07 - Tool-call waits without a deadline log a stall warning (senpi#6189)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/stall-warning.ts` (new): `watchForStall` logs once when a wait outlives its threshold (30 s, or the call's own shorter `timeout`), and `PendingQueueLedger` records the unsettled entries of a promise chain so a stalled wait can name the entry at its head.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/agent-session.ts`: `preflightToolCall` watches its wait on `_agentEventQueue` (`tool_call_event_queue_stalled`, with `queueDepth`, `queueHead`, `ageMs`) and its tool-barrier plus `tool_call` hook phase (`tool_call_preflight_stalled`). Every append to `_agentEventQueue` is recorded in the ledger. The waits, their order and their outcomes are unchanged.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/session-log.ts`: the data allowlist admits `toolCallId`, `parentToolCallId`, `toolName`, `thresholdMs`, `elapsedMs`, `queueDepth` and `queueHead`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/terminal/tools/bash.ts`, `tools/context.ts`: a bash spawn that outlives its threshold logs `bash_spawn_stalled` to the session log (`TerminalToolContext.sessionLogger`, defaulting to the agent directory's session log).

### Why

- senpi#6189: a `tool.bash` call from an eval cell never settled and left no trace. No timer fired, because the kill timer and detach gate are armed only after spawn, and the event-queue wait and the permission hooks before it have no deadline. These warnings say which wait stalled on the next occurrence. They change no behaviour.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The event-queue wait and the preflight run inside `AgentSession` before any extension hook sees the call, and the queue is private session state.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `preflightToolCall` body and the three `_agentEventQueue` append sites in `agent-session.ts`; the `ALLOWED_DATA_KEY` regex in `session-log.ts`.

### Added
- A tool call stalled before it runs now leaves a trace: when its wait on the session's event queue, its `tool_call` hooks (the permission decision), or a bash spawn outlives 30 s (or the call's own shorter `timeout`), one warning naming the call goes to the session log, and the queue warning also names the entry the call waits behind. Nothing is aborted or delayed ([#6189](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/senpi/issues/6189))
