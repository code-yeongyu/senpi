## 2026-11-18 - show_html_page page ledgers belong to their session, and a hand's additions survive the budget (senpi#6931)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/html-render/handed-pages.ts`: the module-level map of pages not yet recorded, keyed by session id, is gone, and so is `forgetHandedPages`. `PendingHandedPages` (by tool call id) is passed to `reserveHandedPage` by its owner, and `HandedPageSession` narrows to `getEntries`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/html-render/tool.ts`: `createShowHtmlPageTool(pending)` replaces the module-level `showHtmlPageTool` (the same shape as senpi#6926).
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/html-render/index.ts`: each extension load owns a ledger and clears it on `agent_end` and `session_shutdown`.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/builtin/html-render/hand-page-budget.ts`: `withHandPageBudget` owns the ledger for the pages a session's hand link lets through, so it ends with that link. An over-budget page replaces only its own text part (the one naming its saved copy) and keeps every part a hand-side `tool_result` handler added.

### Why

- senpi#6928. The brain-side downgrade dropped what a hand's `tool_result` handlers added. Reservations in the host-wide map outlived a session whose brain does not load html-render, because only that extension's shutdown hook cleared them.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- `show_html_page`, its ledger and the brain-side budget are this builtin's own code.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- MEDIUM: `html-render/tool.ts` and `html-render/index.ts` against senpi#6926, which makes the same `createShowHtmlPageTool(pending)` change; take either side's shape. LOW: `html-render/handed-pages.ts` and `html-render/hand-page-budget.ts`.

### Fixed
- A `show_html_page` result from a hand that goes over the session's image budget keeps whatever the hand's `tool_result` handlers added. Pending page bookkeeping is owned by the session (its html-render instance or its hand link), so it no longer outlives a session that does not load html-render ([#6931](https://github.com/code-yeongyu/senpi/pull/6931))
