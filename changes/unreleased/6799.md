## 2026-11-15 - A hand's boundary walk resumes only where its last step said (senpi#5898)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/loop-hook-boundary-walks.ts`, `loop-hook-turn-events.ts`: an open boundary walk records the `resume.next` its last step returned, and a later step must send exactly that `from`. A repeated step, a skipped step, or a point past the walk's handlers is refused (`walk <id> expects from <next>, not <from>`).
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compaction-settings-schema.ts`: uses `amount` directly for the settings, which may be fractional, instead of a local `count` alias.

### Why

- Review of #6787 (L1, L2): a repeated `from` reran a handler and an out-of-range one silently ran nothing, and a fraction-accepting `count` alias shadowed the integer `count`.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The loop-hook link and its wire contract belong to the loopless runtime.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `loop-hook-boundary-walks.ts` and the stepped boundary in `loop-hook-turn-events.ts`.
