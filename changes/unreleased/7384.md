## 2026-11-23 - A park record carries the live session's launch choices, and a client reopen of a parked path takes them (#7384, senpi#7380)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/host-handover-parked-complete.ts` (new): `completeParkedEntry` fills a park record's missing model and thinking level, and its permission preset, from the live session, the in-process runtime or a worker session's last snapshot (a live preset wins, since `set_permission_preset` moves it after the open); `missingLaunchChoices` names what a record still lacks; `parkRecordCompletable` answers whether a park can write a complete record now.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-router-residency.ts`: `recordRetainedSession` writes the completed record and throws `IncompleteParkRecordError` instead of writing one without a model or thinking level when the live session cannot be read.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-router-occupancy.ts`: the idle sweep keeps a retained session resident when its park record could not be completed, and tries again on the next sweep.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-router-parked-choices.ts` (new), `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/session-router-open.ts`: a client's `open_session` of a parked path takes the record's model, thinking level and permission preset wherever the request names none; a choice the request names wins.

### Why

- A retained session opened without explicit launch choices was parked with a record naming none of them, though the live session knew all three. Its reopen ran under the host defaults, so a session moved to `ask` came back running gated tools unasked, and a client reopening the parked path cleared the record without taking its choices (code-yeongyu/oh-my-openagent#14799).

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The park record, the idle sweep and `open_session` are the RPC host's own.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the head of `open()` in `session-router-open.ts`, `recordRetainedSession`, and the claim step of `evictIdleSession`.
