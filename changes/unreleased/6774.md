## 2026-11-15 - A forwarded session_shutdown's signal also aborts at the loop's cancel (senpi#5898)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/core/extensions/runner.ts`: `runSessionShutdownHandler` passes each handler a signal that aborts at the handler cap and, when the event brings one (a shutdown a hosted loop forwarded to a hand), at that event's own signal too. A local session's shutdown event has no `signal`, so it keeps the cap alone.

### Why

- Review of #6753: the loop's cancel reached a forwarded shutdown handler's `ctx.signal`, but its `event.signal` aborted only at the cap.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The runner builds the signal each shutdown handler receives.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `runSessionShutdownHandler`'s `run` in `runner.ts`.

## 2026-11-15 - A hand's loop-hook parsers state which parts are strict; the #6753 entry's type name is corrected (senpi#5898)

### What changed

- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/loop-hook-envelope.ts`: new. Lists the fields each forwarded event's envelope may carry (besides `sessionId`, `idle`, `scope`), and the fields of a compaction preparation and its file sets.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/loopless-loop-hooks.ts`: an envelope field the event's list does not name is refused (`malformed <event> payload: unknown field <name>`). A forwarded `session_shutdown` carries the call's signal, so its handlers' `event.signal` aborts at the loop's cancel.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/loop-hook-payloads.ts`: a preparation or a file-set object with a field it does not name is refused.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/compaction-settings-schema.ts`: the settings are a strict object; an unknown field is refused.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/agent-message-schema.ts`, `session-entry-schema.ts`: still loose, now on record. An unknown field on a transcript message or session entry reaches the handlers untouched, because a newer loop adds optional fields a hand one version behind must hand on, not refuse.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/system-prompt-options-schema.ts`: new. A forwarded `before_agent_start`'s `systemPromptOptions` are strict: every field `BuildSystemPromptOptions` or `BuildDynamicSystemPromptOptions` names that survives JSON, typed, and no other. The context files and skills inside stay loose, like transcript messages.
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/rpc/remote-executor-scope.ts`: a call's `scope` is strict (`id`, `root` only). `LooplessHand` adds `loopHookSessionOf` (a call's scope and loop session, for per-session order) and `loopHookQueueWaitMs` (the hand's `extensionEventHandlerTimeoutMs`, or the 20-minute default when that cap is off or invalid).
- `packages/coding-agent/src/modes/hand/hand-entry.ts`: the hand orders loop-hook calls per session (`sessionOf`) and bounds a call's wait for its turn (`queueWaitMs`).
- Correction to the #6753 entry ("A hand serves a hosted loop's compaction and shutdown events"): `LoopHookCall.run` takes a `LoopHookCallBinding` (#6751's binding, plus the loop's `cancel`), not a `LoopHookCallScope`; no type of that name exists.

### Why

- Review of #6753 (L1): the commit called the payloads strictly parsed while every object was loose. The rule is now explicit per part: the parts the loop-hook lane defines itself are strict, and the loop's own records (messages, entries) are loose.
- Review of #6753 (L3): the fragment named a type that does not exist.
- Review of #6774 (M1, L1, L3): every scope's sessions share the hand's one loop-hook lane, so per-lane order let one session's stuck call hold every other session; a waiting call's wait had no bound; the scope and prompt options were loose.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- These are the hand's own boundary parsers.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the prologue of `extensionLoopHooks`, `preparationOf` and `fileOpsOf`, and the schema module headers.

## 2026-11-15 - The hand orders loop-hook calls per session, bounds their wait, and a cancel still reaches them (senpi#5898)

### What changed

- `packages/agent/src/execution/loop-hook-session-queue.ts`: new. One queue per session: a session's calls take their turns one at a time, in arrival order; different sessions' calls never wait on each other. A call's wait ends at its turn, at its cancel, or after the wait cap.
- `packages/agent/src/execution/loop-hook-host-hand.ts`: `serveLoopHooks` takes `sessionOf(event, payload)` (required) and `queueWaitMs` (default `DEFAULT_LOOP_HOOK_QUEUE_WAIT_MS`, 20 min). It registers and queues each call when it arrives; the frame handler still returns at once, so `hook.cancel` reaches a running call (its signal aborts) or a waiting one. A call that is cancelled while it waits, or waits past `queueWaitMs`, answers `hook.failed` with `reason` `cancelled` or `queue_timeout` and never starts its hooks (no `hook.started`).
- `packages/agent/src/execution/loop-hook-host-protocol.ts`: `hook.failed` carries an optional `reason` (`LoopHookRefusal`). The header documents per-session ordering, which handlers a runner bounds (on a senpi hand: lifecycle and compaction handlers; not `input`, `before_agent_start` or `before_provider_request`, as in a local session), and the always-bounded wait.
- `packages/agent/src/execution/loop-hook-host.ts`: a `hook.failed` with `reason: "queue_timeout"` rejects with `LoopHookQueueTimeoutError`.
- `packages/agent/src/execution/testing/kill-hook.ts`: the KILL-HOOK hand names its one session.
- `packages/agent/src/index.ts`: exports `LoopHookQueueTimeoutError`, `LoopHookRefusal` and `DEFAULT_LOOP_HOOK_QUEUE_WAIT_MS`.

### Why

- Review of #6753 (L4): #6753 stopped awaiting `serve()` so a cancel could arrive, and with it the hand stopped serializing a session's calls. A cancelled call whose handlers ignore their signal could then run alongside the session's next call.
- Review of #6774 (M1, L1, L2): ordering per lane made the order hand-wide on a hand that serves every session on one lane; a stuck handler held its session's queue forever; a call cancelled while it waited still ran its hooks for a caller that had gone.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The loop-hook link is agent-core protocol.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: `serve` and `LoopHookHandOptions` in `loop-hook-host-hand.ts`, `LoopHookFailed` and the protocol header in `loop-hook-host-protocol.ts`, the `hook.failed` branch of `loop-hook-host.ts`, and the loop-hook export blocks of `index.ts`.
