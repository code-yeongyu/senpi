## 2026-11-20 - Keep a live session reachable across a generation handoff (senpi#7098)

### What changed

- A successor attaches to the strict-transfer owner's authenticated private endpoint. The caller keeps its routing handle when the successor reopens the parked transcript. Session listings identify the writer's generation.

### Why

- A detached session can still be mid-turn. Building another runtime forks its transcript; refusing the open leaves the caller unable to steer the running turn.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- The host owns path reservations, socket authentication, routing handles, and attachment lifetime.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- The shared host's socket setup and the session router's open, close and listing paths.
