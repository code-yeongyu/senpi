## 2026-11-01 - Fixed releases of the test toolchain and shell-quote (part of senpi#4820)

### What changed

- `vitest` and `@vitest/coverage-v8` 5.0.1 -> 5.0.5 in the root and every workspace's `devDependencies` and in the `vitest-evals` override; the root `vite` override 8.2.2 -> 8.3.5; the root `shell-quote` devDependency and override 1.10.0 -> 1.12.0; a new root `source-map-js` override pins 1.2.2. `package-lock.json` and `bun.lock` are regenerated with `npm run refresh-lock`.

### Why

- The full `npm audit` (dev included) flagged shell-quote 1.10.0 (critical, GHSA-pqg4-j6r4-53mv), vite 8.2.2 (high, GHSA-587h-7wmj-h5pm and five more), source-map-js 1.2.1 (high, GHSA-68fv-2mgg-jv7q) and vitest / @vitest/mocker / @vitest/coverage-v8 5.0.1 (moderate, GHSA-qxg6-89mg-rqv8, GHSA-xp26-3454-mjgp). All are dev-only; nothing here ships. The `source-map-js` override keeps bun's tree on 1.2.2 too, since bun.lock does not follow npm's in-range update.

### Why an extension could not handle it

- Development dependencies and overrides are root workspace configuration outside the extension runtime.

### Expected merge conflict zones

- LOW: the `vitest` / `@vitest/coverage-v8` devDependency lines in each workspace `package.json`, the root `overrides` block, and the lockfile hunks for vite, vitest, shell-quote and source-map-js.
